Grilled Chicken Eggs Benedict


Ingredients:

1 lb thinly sliced chicken breast

Parmigiano - Reggiano cheese

1 can diced stewed tomatoes

1 can diced herbed and garlic tomatoes

1/2 cup panko bread crumbs

1/4 tsp garlic powder

1/2 cup parmigiano - reggiano cheese

2 poached eggs

Olive Oil

Salt / Pepper

Fresh basil

Directions:

1. Cook the tomatoes on the stove.

2. Toast the panko, parm-regg cheese, 1 Tbls olive oil, salt / pepper, garlic powder in a skillet for 4 - 6 min.

3. Poach the eggs.

4. Grill the chicken 4 min, turn and add some parm - Regg cheese. Cook 4 more min.

5. Combine and Enjoy.
