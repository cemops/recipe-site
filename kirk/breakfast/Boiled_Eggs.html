Boiled Eggs

Soft, Medium (Jammy), or Hard Boiled


Ingredients

Eggs
Water
Salt
Vinegar
Ice (for ice bath)

Egg Doneness Chart

Doneness Boil / Simmer Time* Yolk Texture Best Uses
Soft Boiled 6 minutes Very runny, custard-like Ramen, toast soldiers
Medium / Jammy 9–10 minutes Set whites, creamy center Salads, bowls, snacking
Hard Boiled 12 minutes (off heat) Fully set Deviled eggs, egg salad

*Timing starts once eggs are placed in boiling water or once simmering begins, depending on method.

Instructions

Hard Boiled Eggs

Place eggs in pan with cold water, 1–2 inches above eggs. Add salt and vinegar.
Slowly bring to a boil over high heat.
When boiling starts, remove briefly and reduce heat.
Return pan and simmer 1 minute.
Cover and remove from heat for 12 minutes.
Cool immediately in cold water or ice bath.

Half Boiled (Medium)

Boil water with salt and vinegar.
Prick large end of eggs with a pin.
Add eggs and return to low boil.
Boil 9–10 minutes.
Ice bath 10–15 minutes.

Soft Boiled Eggs

Bring large pot of water to a boil.
Prick eggs at large end.
Lower eggs gently with slotted spoon.
Simmer 6 minutes.
Ice bath 2 minutes, peel under running water.
