Mollet Eggs Florentine

A classic French dish with creamy spinach and eggs, perfect for brunch.


Ingredients:

8 large eggs
2 pounds spinach
3 tbsp unsalted butter
2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp black pepper
1/4 tsp grated nutmeg
3 tbsp grated Gruyère cheese
1 tbsp unsalted butter (for sauce)
1 tbsp flour
1 cup half-and-half
1/2 tsp salt (for sauce)
1/4 tsp white pepper
1 egg yolk
1 1/2 tbsp Parmesan cheese

Directions:

- Boil water and cook eggs for 6 minutes.
- Cool and shell the eggs under cold running water.
- Boil and drain the spinach. Sauté with butter, salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
- Arrange spinach in a dish and place eggs on top. Sprinkle with Gruyère cheese.
- Make the Mornay sauce: whisk flour, butter, half-and-half, salt, and pepper.
- Mix egg yolk and Parmesan cheese into the sauce and pour over eggs.
- Broil for 5 minutes until browned.
