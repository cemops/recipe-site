Rainbow Veggie Frittata

A delicious and colorful frittata packed with fresh vegetables!


Ingredients:

2 Garlic Cloves, Finely Chopped
1 Red Pepper, Sliced
1 Orange Pepper, Sliced
Sea Salt
1 Zucchini, Cut into Small Chunks
5 Stalks Rainbow Chard, Thinly Sliced (can sub with Kale, Swiss Chard, etc.)
8 Eggs, Beaten
Freshly Ground Black Pepper

Directions:

- Pre-heat the broiler.
- Heat oil in a 10" nonstick, ovenproof skillet over medium heat.
- Add garlic and cook until just softened.
- Add bell peppers and salt, sauté for 5-6 minutes until softened.
- Add zucchini and cook for 2 minutes.
- Add chard and hot water, cook for 4-5 minutes until wilted.
- Season eggs with salt and pepper, pour over vegetables.
- Cook over medium heat for 5-7 minutes.
- Transfer skillet to broiler, cook for a few minutes until top is golden.
- Loosen edges, slide frittata onto a plate, cut into wedges and serve.
