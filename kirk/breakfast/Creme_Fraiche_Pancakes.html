Crème Fraîche Pancakes

A delicious breakfast treat with a rich flavor from crème fraîche.


Full Ingredients:

1 1/2 Cups Flour
3 Tbs Sugar
2 tsp Baking Powder
1 tsp Kosher Salt
1 Cup Whole Milk
1/2 Cup Crème Fraîche
1/4 Cup Sour Cream
2 Eggs
1 1/2 tsp Vanilla

Half Ingredients:

3/4 Cups Flour
1 1/2 Tbs Sugar
1 tsp Baking Powder
1/2 tsp Kosher Salt
1/2 Cup Whole Milk
1/4 Cup Crème Fraîche
1/8 Cup Sour Cream
1 Egg
3/4 tsp Vanilla



Instructions:

1. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together.
2. In a separate bowl, mix milk, crème fraîche, sour cream, eggs, and vanilla.
3. Slowly combine the wet ingredients with the dry ingredients until you reach the desired consistency.
4. Cook on a skillet with butter until golden brown.
