Shrimp and Grits

A lighter take on a Southern classic dish.


Ingredients:

1/2 Cup REAL Grits
1 Cup Seafood or Chicken Stock
1 Cup Whole Milk
1 tsp Smoked Paprika
1/2 Cup Shredded Parmesan
1 TBS EVOO
1 LB Peeled and Deveined Shrimp (16-20)
Approx 1 Cup Andouille Sausage (or Tasso Ham)
1/4 tsp Kosher Salt
2 Garlic Cloves, Crushed
Red Pepper, diced
3 Scallions, cut into 2-inch pieces
1/4 tsp Dried Thyme
1/4 tsp Black Pepper
1 15-oz Can Fire Roasted Diced Tomatoes (I like to sub 5 roma tomatoes fire roasted on the grill then diced)

Instructions:

1. In a medium pot, combine the broth, milk, and paprika, and bring to a boil.
2. Gradually add the grits, stirring vigorously. Reduce heat to medium-low and cook covered, stirring periodically, for 6-8 minutes.
3. Add the Parmesan cheese in three parts, then remove from heat.
4. Toss the shrimp with seasoning and olive oil. Heat oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat.
5. Add sausage to the skillet and cook for 1 minute per side. Then add red pepper, garlic, and scallions, and sauté for 2-3 minutes.
6. Add the diced tomatoes, Creole seasoning, and thyme. Simmer for 6 minutes until sauce thickens.
7. Add shrimp to the sauce and cook until pink, about 3-4 minutes per side.
8. Serve the shrimp mixture over the grits in bowls.
