Bacon, Egg & Cheese Sliders


Ingredients:

6 hard boiled eggs

6 thin slices of cheddar cheese

3 slices of cooked bacon

1/2 avocado

1/2 tsp lime juice

1/2 tsp ground cumin

Salt & pepper

Instructions:

1. Combine the avocado, lime juice, cumin, salt & pepper and blend until creamy.

2. Slice each egg in half lengthwise. Next time, take a small slice off of half of the eggs to make a flat surface, these will be the bottom half.

3. Stack the cheddar cheese, bacon and a spoonful of avocado mixture on half of the eggs and cover with the other egg halves. Place a toothpick in the center to hold them together.

4. Sprinkle with salt and pepper at the end.
