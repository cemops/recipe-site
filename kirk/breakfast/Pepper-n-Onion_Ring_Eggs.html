Pepper and Onion Ring Eggs

A colorful and simple way to enjoy eggs with fresh bell peppers and onions.


Ingredients:

Bell Peppers (colored) or Onion
Eggs
Salt & Pepper

Directions:

- Cut pepper and onion into 1/2" rings.
- Place rings in a hot pan with oil and let them cook.
- Crack eggs into the rings and cook until done.
- Season with salt and pepper. Serve and enjoy!
