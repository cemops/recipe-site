Avocado Toast


Ingredients:

Bread – cracked wheat
1 Avocado per serving
2 Eggs per serving
1 Lemon
Salt
Fresh Cracked Pepper
Olive Oil
Unsalted Butter
Water
Tabasco Sauce (optional)

Directions:

Slice and toast the bread.
Peel, seed, and mash the avocado.
Add 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
Spread the avocado mixture onto the toast.
Heat an 8" pan, add olive oil and butter, and swirl to combine.
Cook 2 eggs sunny side up, adding 1 tsp water to the edge of the pan and covering it.
If the yolks turn white, lightly scrape off the white film.
Place eggs on top of the avocado toast.
Drizzle with Tabasco sauce (optional) and serve.
