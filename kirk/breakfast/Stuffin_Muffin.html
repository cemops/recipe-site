Stuffin' Muffin

A creative way to use leftover stuffing and turkey in a breakfast muffin, topped with eggs and sage hollandaise.


Ingredients:

3 Cups Leftover Stuffing, cooked
6 Slices Leftover Turkey, cooked and sliced (or use bacon, pancetta, sausage)
6 Large Eggs
Kosher Salt and Pepper, to taste

For Sage Hollandaise:

1 Stick Unsalted Butter
3 Egg Yolks
1 TBS Sage Leaves, roughly chopped
1 tsp Lemon Juice
¼ tsp Kosher Salt
1/8 tsp Pepper

Directions:

- Preheat oven to 425°F. Spray jumbo muffin tin with non-stick cooking spray.
- Place ½ cup of stuffing into each muffin tin cavity and press gently to level.
- Top each with a slice of turkey or other protein (ham, roast beef, sausage).
- Crack an egg on top of each and season with salt and pepper.
- Bake for 15-20 minutes until eggs are just cooked. Let cool in pan for 3-5 minutes.
- Prepare the Sage Hollandaise: melt butter, blend with egg yolks, sage, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
- Slowly drizzle in melted butter while blending until smooth and creamy.
- Serve muffins drizzled with hollandaise or with sauce on the side for dipping.
