Eggs Jeannette

A classic French dish that's perfect for brunch or a light meal.


Ingredients:

4 Half Boiled Eggs
2 TBS Parsley
1 Large Garlic Clove
1 Anchovy Filet
2 TBS Milk
1 TBS Water
2 tsp Dijon Mustard
Dash of Red Wine Vinegar
Olive Oil

Instructions:

1. Peel, cut, and remove yolks from half-boiled eggs.
2. Add parsley, garlic, and anchovy filet to yolks and mash together.
3. Add salt, pepper, and milk to create a puree.
4. Stuff the eggs with the mixture, reserving some for the sauce.
5. Saute eggs, stuffed side down, in olive oil for 3-4 minutes.
6. For the sauce, add mustard, red wine vinegar, anchovy oil, and water to the reserved mixture and emulsify with olive oil.
7. Pour the sauce onto a gratin plate and place the eggs in the sauce.
