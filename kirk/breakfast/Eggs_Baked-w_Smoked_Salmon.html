Eggs Baked with Smoked Salmon

A sophisticated and delicious breakfast or brunch recipe for seafood lovers.


Ingredients:

1 1⁄2 tbsp. unsalted butter, melted
6 oz. thinly sliced smoked salmon
3⁄4 cup heavy cream
4 eggs
Kosher salt, to taste
Freshly ground white pepper, to taste
2 tbsp. freshly grated horseradish or prepared horseradish, drained
2 chives, cut into 1″ pieces

Instructions:

1. Heat oven to 375°F. Grease four 6-oz. ramekins with melted butter and place them in a 9" x 13" baking dish lined with parchment paper.
2. Arrange the salmon along the bottom and sides of the ramekins, reserving 1 slice for garnish.
3. Pour 1 tbsp. cream into each ramekin and crack an egg on top; season with salt and pepper.
4. Place the baking dish in the oven and pour boiling water halfway up the sides of the ramekins. Bake for 15–17 minutes, until the egg whites are set but yolks remain soft.
5. Whip the remaining cream until soft peaks form, then fold in horseradish and season with salt and pepper.
6. Julienne the reserved slice of salmon and garnish the baked eggs with the salmon, horseradish cream, and chives before serving.
