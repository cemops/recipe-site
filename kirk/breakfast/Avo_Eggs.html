Avo Eggs


Ingredients:

1 Avocado

2 Eggs

Salt and Pepper

Herbs (optional)

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).

2. Cut the avocado in half, remove the seed, and leave the skin on.

3. Separate the eggs and place one yolk in each avocado half.

4. Season with salt, pepper, and herbs as desired.

5. Bake for 15 minutes or until the eggs are cooked to your liking.
