Cajun Omlette


Ingredients:

8 eggs
½ lb spicy sausage, casing removed
⅓ cup sliced mushrooms
½ onion, diced
½ medium bell pepper, diced
¼ cup green onions, chopped
1 jalapeno pepper, seeded and chopped
2 tablespoons water
1 tablespoon Dijon or homemade mustard
Cooking fat
1 pinch cayenne pepper
Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Directions:

Fry sausage meat in a large frying pan with some cooking fat. Set aside and reserve.
Add cooking fat to the pan and fry mushrooms, onion, bell pepper, and jalapeno until mushrooms are browned.
Whisk eggs with water, mustard, cayenne, salt, and pepper.
Pour egg mixture over the vegetables in the pan.
Add the cooked sausage back into the pan.
Gently stir until eggs are nearly set.
Fold omelet in half and cover; cook for 1 more minute.
