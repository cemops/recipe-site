Smoked Salmon Eggs Benedict

A delightful twist on the classic Eggs Benedict with smoked salmon and avocado.


Ingredients:

2 English Muffins, Split
1 Large Avocado, Sliced
2 TBS Fresh Dill
1 tsp Capers
Heirloom Tomatoes, for Serving
4 Large Eggs
1 TBS White Vinegar
3 oz Smoked Salmon

For Hollandaise Sauce:

4 Egg Yolks
1 TBS Fresh Lemon Juice
Salt and Black Pepper
1/2 Cup Butter, Melted

Directions:

- Prepare ingredients: thaw smoked salmon, slice avocado, chop fresh dill.
- Prepare the Hollandaise Sauce: whisk egg yolks, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
- Slowly whisk in melted butter over simmering water until sauce thickens.
- Toast the English Muffins.
- Poach the eggs with a dash of vinegar in simmering water.
- Assemble: layer muffin with avocado, smoked salmon, and poached egg.
- Smother with Hollandaise Sauce, garnish with dill and capers.
- If sauce is too thick, thin with hot water and whisk.
