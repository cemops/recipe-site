Omelette - Jelayne's Favorite


Ingredients:

2 Eggs

1/2 Roma Tomato

Large Handfull Spinach

Shredded Mozzarella Cheese

Salt and Pepper

Herbs (for Kirk: Oregano, Basil)

Instructions:

1. Preheat small skillet with olive oil

2. Rough chop the spinich and ad to skillet, fry, turning once. Remove from the skillet.

3. Quarter the tomatoe vertically. Lay with the skin side down, and seed the tomato by sliding a knife along the tomatoe. Dice the tomato after seeding them.

4. Beat eggs (ad Kirk's herbs). Reheat the skillet with olive oil and unsalted butter. Ad eggs and actively stir with a spatula and shake the pan until the eggs are evenly cooked, but still very wet.

5. Ad the spinach, tomato, cheese, salt and pepper to the center of the eggs in a thin row. You may need to remove the skillet from the heat if they are getting too done.

6. Fold one side of the egg over the filling, sliding the entire omelette to that edge of the pan. Fold the other half of the egg over the filling.

5. Tilt the skillet over the serving plate and invert the omelette onto the plate. Click Here to see Jacques Pépin's technique. I do it slightly different but the concept is the same.
