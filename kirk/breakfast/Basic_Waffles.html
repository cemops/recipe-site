Basic Waffles


Ingredients (Full Recipe for 8 Waffles):

2 Cups all-purpose flour

2 Tbs Sugar

1 Tbs Baking Powder

1/2 tsp Salt

1 3/4 Cups Reduced Fat Milk

6 Tbs Vegetable Oil

2 Large Eggs

Ingredients (1/2 Recipe for 4 Waffles):

1 Cup all-purpose Flour

1 Tbs Sugar

1/2 Tbs Baking Powder

1/4 tsp Salt

1 Cup Reduced Fat Milk (just shy)

3 Tbs Vegetable Oil

1 Large Egg

Directions:

- Place ingredients in a large mixing bowl and combine until well blended and smooth. Let batter sit for 5 min. before using.

- Preheat waffle maker on setting #3 (green indicator light will be illuminated when preheated).

- Pour 1/2 Cup batter onto the center of the grid and close waffle maker. Indicator light will turn red. Indicator light will turn green when waffle is ready.

- You may keep waffles warm in a 200 degree oven until ready to serve.
