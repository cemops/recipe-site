Breakfast Potatoes


Ingredients:

2 - 3 lbs red potatoes, diced

2 - 3 cloves garlic, minced

1 small onion, chopped

1 small green bell pepper, chopped

1 small red bell pepper, chopped

1/4 cup olive oil

1/4 stick butter, melted

1 tsp seasoned salt

1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

Kosher salt & black pepper

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).

2. Toss all ingredients in a large bowl.

3. Bake for 20-25 minutes, shaking pan twice.

4. Raise heat to 500°F (260°C), bake for 10-15 minutes until crisp.

5. Sprinkle more salt & pepper before serving.
