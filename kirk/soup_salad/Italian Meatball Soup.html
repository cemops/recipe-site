Italian Meatball Soup


Ingredients

FOR THE MEATBALLS

1 LB Ground Beef
1/4 - 1/2 Cup freshly grated Parmesan Cheese
1 Egg
1 Cup Bread Crumbs (whole wheat)
2 TBS Fresh Parsley
1 tsp Dried Oregano
1/2 tsp Salt
1/3 tsp Black Pepper

FOR THE SOUP

2 Quarts Chicken Broth
3 TBS Tomato Paste
1 Onion, diced
2 Bay Leaves
4-5 Sprigs Fresh Thyme
1/2 tsp Whole Black Peppercorns

Instructions

Combine all the ingredients (except the olive oil) for the meatballs and mix gently. Form into small, bite sized meatballs. Brown meatballs in stock pot on all sides for a few min. Remove meatballs and set aside.
Add diced onion to the oil. Saute a few min. until softened and scrape up browned bits from meatballs. Add the rest of the soup ingredients and bring to a boil. Reduce heat, cover and simmer for 5 - 10 min.
Add meatballs and simmer a few more minutes.
Garnish with fresh parsley, basil and Parmesan cheese.
