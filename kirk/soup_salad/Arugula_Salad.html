Arugula Salad


Ingredients

5 oz Baby Arugula
1 Apple, Cut Into Small Slivers
Shaved Parm
Pine Nuts
Olive Oil
Lemon Juice
Honey
S & P

Instructions

In a large bowl, mix together the oil, lemon juice, honey, S & P.
Add the lettuce, pine nuts and apple. Toss to coat.
Add the shaved parm at the end over the salad.

** Could also add raisins, red onion, sun-dried tomatoes, other nuts etc.  **
