Peas and Carrot Stew


Ingredients

3 Carrots - diced
4 Scallions - diced
1/2 Onion - diced
1 lb Frozen Peas
2 Tbs Butter
1 Tbs Olive Oil
1 Tbs Flour
1 tsp dried Thyme
2 Cups Water
Salt and Pepper to taste

Instructions

Melt Butter over medium high heat
Saute Scallions, Onion, Thyme, Salt & Pepper for a couple of minutes
Add Olive Oil, heat and add Flour. Stir immediately so flour doesn't clump.
Cook for another couple of minutes
Add Carrots and Water, bring to a boil and cook for 5 min.
Add Peas and simmer until peas are warm, approx 10 min.
