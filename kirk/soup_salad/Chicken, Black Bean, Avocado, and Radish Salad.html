Chicken, Black Bean, Avocado, and Radish Salad


Ingredients

(Makes about 6 servings)

INGREDIENTS:

2 Cups diced chicken breasts from rotisserie chicken ( fair chicken )
1 - 15 oz can black beans, rinsed and drained well
2 avocados diced into 1 inch pieces
1 Tbs fresh-squeezed lime juice (to toss with avocado)
1 Bunch radishes (about 10) washed, trimmed and cut into pieces
1/2 Cup sliced green onion
1/2 Cup chopped fresh cilantro

DRESSING INGREDIENTS:

1 1/2 Tbs Olive oil
3 Tbs mayo
2 Tbs fresh squeezed lime juice
1 tsp ground cumin
1 - 2 tsp Green Tabasco Sauce

Instructions

Place the avocado in a bowl and toss with the lemon juice
Put the black beans in a colander and rinse well with cold water until no more foam appears
Whisk together the olive oil. mayo, lime juice, cumin, and Green Tabasco Sauce for the dressing.
Put the well drained beans into a bowl and toss with about half of the dressing, let marinate
Add the chicken and chopped radishes to the beans and toss with the additional dressing
Stir in the green onion and chopped cilantro, then gently stir in the avocado.
Season well with salt and ground pepper and serve right away.
