Roasted Cauliflower and Cheddar Soup


Ingredients

1 large head of cauliflower chopped
2 tbsp olive oil
Sea salt and pepper
1 tsp garlic powder
1/2 onion chopped
3 tbsp minced garlic
5 cups chicken broth
2 - 2 1/2 cups double cheddar cheese

Instructions

Lightly toss the cauliflower in 1 Tbs olive oil, salt, pepper and garlic powder. line a baking sheet with foil and roast the florets at 425 degrees until the florets begin to brown. about 30 min.
Over medium, heat up your olive oil and saute your onion until soft in a stock pot.
Add cauliflower and stir around for a min.
Add Chicken Broth. Bring to a boil and then reduce the heat down to a simmer and cover. Let simmer for 30 - 35 min.
Use and Immersion blender and puree.
Add the cheddar and stir to melt and serve.
Garnish soup with pepper and Olive Oil
