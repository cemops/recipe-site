Kiwi Avocado and Tomato Salad


Ingredients

Kiwi Dressing:
4 Kiwi peeled and quartered
1/4 Cup Olive Oil
1 tsp Honey
1 1/2 TBS Apple Cider Vinegar
Salad
4 - 6 Cups Mixed Greens
2 Avocados, Sliced
1 Cup Cherry or Grape Tomatoes (from Buehlers tomatoe bar), halved
2 Kiwi, Peeled, Sliced and Quartered (Consider Gold Kiwi with Green)
P1/4 Cup Pepitas
Goat Cheese
Salt and Pepper to taste

Instructions

To make dressing: Place all dressing ingredients into immersion blender bowl. Blend until smooth and place into container and refrigerate until ready to use.
Place mixed greens, avocados tomatoes, kiwi, and pepitas into a large serving bowl. Plate and add goat cheese and dressing.
