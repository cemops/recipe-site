Wedding Soup


Ingredients

2 Quarts Chicken low sodium broth
2 large carrots, finely diced
2 celery ribs, finely diced
Kosher salt, ground pepper
1/2 cup orzo
1 lb ground pork
1/4 cup freshly grated parmigiano-Reggiano
1/4 cu dry bread crumbs
5 oz baby spinach, chopped
one 16- oz can chickpeas, drained and rinsed

Instructions

In a large pot bring the chicken stock to a boil with carrot and celery.  Season with salt and pepper. Add the orzo and cook until al dente, about 7 min.
Meanwhile, in a bowl, knead the pork with the 1/4 cup of cheese, the bread crumbs and 1/2 teaspoon each of salt and pepper. Form the mixture into 1-inch balls.
Drop the balls into the boiling soup and simmer for 5 min. Sir int he spinach and chickpeas and simmer until the meatballs are cooked through, 5 min longer.
