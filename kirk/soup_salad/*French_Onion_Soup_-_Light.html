French Onion Soup - Light


Ingredients

** We agreed to double this recipe so we can freeze half and still have leftovers **

DOUBLE RECIPE (with Jan 2020 adjustments)

2 TBS EVOO
4 Large Onions, Halved and Thinly Sliced
1 Clove Garlic
Kosher Salt
Lg Bundle Bouquet Garni (Fresh Thyme and Fresh Bay Leaves)
1/2 Cup (strong) Dry White Wine
1/2 Cup (strong) Bourbon (Jim Beam)
2 TBS Soy Sauce
12 Cups Beef Broth
8 1/2-Inch Thick Slices Baguette
1/2 Cup Freshly Grated Gruyère Cheese

SINGLE RECIPE

1 TBS EVOO
2 Large Onions, Halved and Thinly Sliced
Kosher salt
1/4 Cup Dry White Wine
1 TBS Soy Sauce
6 Cups Beef Broth
Four 1/2-inch-thick slices of whole-grain baguette
1/4 cup freshly grated Gruyère cheese

Instructions

Instructions (with Jan 2020 adjustments)

Step 1

In a large enameled cast-iron casserole, heat the oil until shimmering.
Add the onions and garlic and cook over moderate heat, stirring occasionally, until the onions are wilted and just starting to brown, about 7 minutes.
Add a generous pinch of salt and cook over moderately low heat, stirring occasionally, until the onions are soft and golden, 25 to 30 minutes.

Step 2

Add the wine, bourbon ( cognac ) and soy sauce and cook over moderate heat, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom, until evaporated, about 3 minutes.
Add the stock and bouquet garni and bring to a boil. Simmer over moderately low heat, stirring occasionally, until the broth is well flavored and slightly reduced, about 10 minutes.
Season the soup with salt. SKIP It's salty enough

Step 3

Meanwhile, preheat the broiler. Arrange the bread on a baking sheet and top each piece with 1 tablespoon of the cheese.
Broil 6 inches from the heat until the cheese is melted and just starting to brown, about 2 minutes.
To serve, ladle the soup into bowls and top with the toast.

Make Ahead
The recipe can be prepared through Step 2 and refrigerated for up to 2 days. Reheat gently.

Notes
One Serving: 145 cal, 6 gm fat, 1.6 gm sat fat, 14 gm carb, 2 gm fiber, 8 gm protein.
