Slow Cooker Split Pea Soup


Ingredients

16 oz dried split peas
1 1/2 lb ham bone ( OR Strebs Hickory Smoked Ham Shank from Buehlers)
3 carrots, diced
1 yellow onion, diced
1 shallot, diced
2 stalks celery, diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp dried thyme
1/2 tsp ground black pepper
1 bay leaf
6 cups low sodium chicken broth

Instructions

In the bowl of the slow cooker, combine, split peas, carrots, yellow onion, shallot, celery, garlic, thyme, pepper, bay leaf, and chicken stock. Mix well.
Add the ham bone on top and nestle into the contents of the slow cooker.
Cover and cook on Low for 5 - 6 hours, or until ham is tender and easily pulls off the bone.
Remove the ham and shred into bite-sized pieces using two forks, then return the ham meat to the slow cooker, and discard the bone.
Remove the bay leaf and serve.
