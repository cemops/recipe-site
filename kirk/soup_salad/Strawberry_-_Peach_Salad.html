Strawberry - Peach Salad


Ingredients

5 Strawberries, hulled and cut
1 Peach, pitted and sliced
3 Cups Spinach
1/4 Cup Olive Oil
1 TBS Balsamic Vinegar
1/4 Cup Feta Cheese, crumbled
1/4 Cup Almonds
Salt and Pepper to taste

Instructions

Mix the feta cheese, oil, vinegar, salt and pepper together
Add the spinach and coat well.
Toss with the strawberries, peach slices and nuts.
