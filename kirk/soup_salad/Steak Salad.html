Steak Salad


Ingredients

Standard

12 Oz Steak
Leaf Lettuce
Spinach
Cucumber - peeled and quartered
Green Pepper
Red Pepper
2 Boiled Eggs
Shredded Mozzarella Cheese
1 Can Sliced Beets - Skip the beets
Tomatoe

Optional

Shredded Carrots
Bean Sprouts
Water Chestnuts
Edamame

Instructions

Marinate steak in Weber's Canadian Steak Seasoning marinade for at least 30 min.
Chop leaf lettuce, spinach, cucumber, peppers, eggs
Grill steak until medium, let cool slightly, cut into bite size pieces
Combine all ingredients
Serve with baguette slices
