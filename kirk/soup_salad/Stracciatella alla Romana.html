Stracciatella alla Romana


Ingredients

7 Cups Hot Chicken Stock
Salt
2 TBS Finely Grated Parm. Cheese
Grated Zest of 1 Lemon
2 TBS Finely Chopped Flat Leaf Parsley
*** Last time added some shredded roasted chicken for volume ***
*** You can alternatively ad dried prosciutto, seeded peperoncini etc. ***

Instructions

In a large pan, bring the stock to a boil and reduce by 1/3. Season to taste w/ salt??
In a bowl, whisk the eggs with the parm., nutmeg, and lemon zest until well blended.
Reduce the heat to a steady simmer and whisk in the egg mixture. Continue to whisk for 1 - 2 min. as the egg forms fine pasta like threads.
If adding chicken, ad it after the eggs are done.
Taste and add more cheese and seasoning if you like. Garnish with parsley.
