3 Cheese Spinach Tortellini Soup


Ingredients

6 Cups low sodium chicken stock
1 - 2 tsp minced garlic
1 - 9 oz package 3 cheese tortellini (frozen)
Dried basil
1 package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed OR Fresh spinach OR Kale (Last time I tried combo of spinach and kale, cooked in the broth first then added rest of ingredients)
Salt & Pepper
1 - 14 oz can Italian stewed tomatoes
Parmesan cheese

Instructions

In a large pot, bring stock to simmer
Stir in tortellini and simmer gently for 3 min.
Stir in spinach, garlic, basil, salt, pepper and tomatoes
Return to simmer and cook for 2 - 3 more min.
Serve hot with Parmesan cheese
