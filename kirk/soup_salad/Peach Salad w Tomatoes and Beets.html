Peach Salad w/ Tomatoes and Beets


Ingredients

2 Medium Sized Golden (or red) Beets
2 Medium Sized Tomatoes (Colorful Heirlooms Preferred)
3 Medium Peeled Peaches, Sliced
Salt / Pepper
3 TBS EVOO
2 TBS Fresh Lemon Juice
1 TBS Honey
1 Medium Shallot, Thinly Sliced
3 TBS Small Fresh Mint Leaves
2 tsp Fresh Thyme
1/2 Cup Crumbled Goat or Feta Cheese

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425. Scrub beets and trom tops to 1 inch. Place beets in glass dish, fill 1/3 full of water. Cover with foil and bake for 1 hour. (if one of the beets is much thicker, slice it in half)
Cool, peel beets (wipe with a paper towel while still warm) and cut into 1/4 inch slices.
Core tomatoes and cut into 1/4 inch slices. Plate the beets and tomatoes and sprinkle with salt and pepper.
Combine remaining salt, oil, 1 Tbs lemon juice, honey and shallot in medium bowl. Toss peach slices with remaining lemon juice. Add peach mixture to honey mixture; toss. Mound peach mixture on tomatoes and beets; sprinkle with mint, thyme and cheese.
