Mediterranean Tomato Soup


Ingredients

4 TBS Olive Oil
2 Medium Yellow Onions, thinly sliced
1 tsp Salt
2 tsp Curry Powder
1 tsp Red Curry Powder
1 tsp Ground Coriander
1 tsp Ground Cumin
1/4 to 1/2 tsp Red Pepper Flakes
1 - 15 oz Can Diced Tomatoes
1 - 28 oz can Diced or Plum Tomatoes (I used plum last time)
5 1/2 Cups Chicken Broth
1 - 14 oz can Coconut Milk
Add - ins: Choose any you'd like - Cooked brown rice, lemon wedges, toasted almond slices, fresh thyme or oregano, oregano pesto, poached egg

Instructions

Heat oil over medium heat. Add the onions and salt. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onions really soften up and start to turn brown, 10 - 12 min.
Stir in the curry powder, coriander, cumin and red pepper flakes and cook 30 seconds, stirring constantly. Add the tomatoes with juices from the cans, and broth. Simmer for 15 min, then puree with a hand blender until smooth.
Add the coconut milk to taste
