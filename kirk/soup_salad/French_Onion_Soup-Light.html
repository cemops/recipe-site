French Onion Soup – Light

Double Recipe (Jan 2020 Adjustments) + Single Recipe Reference

Ingredients – Double Recipe (Used Version)

  • 2 tbsp EVOO
  • 4 large onions, halved and thinly sliced
  • 1 clove garlic
  • Kosher salt
  • Large bundle bouquet garni (fresh thyme + fresh bay leaves)
  • 1/2 cup strong dry white wine
  • 1/2 cup strong bourbon (Jim Beam)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 12 cups beef broth
  • 8 slices baguette (1/2-inch thick)
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Gruyère cheese

Ingredients – Single Recipe (Reference)

  • 1 tbsp EVOO
  • 2 large onions, halved and thinly sliced
  • Kosher salt
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 6 cups beef broth
  • Four 1/2-inch-thick slices whole-grain baguette
  • 1/4 cup freshly grated Gruyère cheese

Instructions (Jan 2020 Adjustments)

  1. In a large enameled cast-iron casserole, heat the oil until shimmering.
  2. Add the onions and garlic and cook over moderate heat, stirring occasionally, until onions wilt and begin to brown (about 7 min).
  3. Add a generous pinch of salt and cook over moderately low heat, stirring occasionally, until onions are soft and golden, 25–30 minutes.
  4. Add wine, bourbon (or cognac), and soy sauce. Cook over moderate heat, scraping up browned bits, until evaporated (about 3 minutes).
  5. Add beef stock and bouquet garni. Bring to a boil, then simmer over moderately low heat until well flavored and slightly reduced (about 10 minutes).
  6. Taste — do not add salt; it’s salty enough.
  7. Preheat broiler. Arrange bread slices on a baking sheet and top each with 1 tbsp Gruyère.
  8. Broil 6 inches from heat until cheese melts and begins to brown (about 2 minutes).
  9. Ladle soup into bowls and top with cheese toasts.

Make Ahead

Soup can be made through Step 2 and refrigerated for up to 2 days. Reheat gently before serving.

Notes

One serving: 145 cal, 6g fat, 1.6g sat fat, 14g carbs, 2g fiber, 8g protein.
