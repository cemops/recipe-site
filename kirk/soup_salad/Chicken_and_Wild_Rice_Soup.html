Chicken and Wild Rice Soup


Ingredients

1 Cup Uncooked Quick-Cooking Wild Rice
1 Cup Chopped Onion
2 Garlic Cloves, Minced
3 Cups Chicken Broth
1 1/2 Cups Cubed Peeled Potato
3 Cups 2% (maybe fat free) Milk
1/3 Cup Flour
10 oz Light Processed Cheese, Cubed (such as Velveeta Light) ** Maybe Can Substitute Asiago?? **
2 Cups Chopped Roasted Skinless Chicken Breasts ** OR Just Cut Up Buehlers Roasted or Smoked Chicken ?? **
1/2 tsp Black Pepper
1/4 tsp Salt - ** May not need with real chicken broth **
1/4 Cup Chopped Fresh Parsley

Instructions

Cook rice according to package instructions, omitting salt and fat
Heat a large Dutch Oven over medium high heat, coat with cooking spray. Add onion and garlic; saute 3 min.
Add broth and potato, bring to a boil. Cover, reduce heat and simmer 5 min or until potato is tender
Combine milk and flower, stirring well with a whisk. Add the milk mixture to the potato mixture; cook 5 min or until slightly thick, stirring constantly.
Remove from heat, add cheese, stirring until cheese melts. Stir in rice, chicken, pepper and salt. Garnish with parsley
