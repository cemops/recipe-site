Lemon Chicken Orzo Soup


Ingredients

1 Onion, Chopped
1/2 Cup Carrot, Shredded
12 Cups Chicken Broth (1 large box of broth = 6 cups)
3 Cups Chicken, Chopped (breast and thighs of 1 buehlers rotisserie chicken)
2 Cups Kale, ribs removed and leaves finely chopped
******
1 Cup Orzo ( cook orzo Separately so it doesn't Explode in the soup.)
******
1/4 Cup Lemon juice, plus 2 Lemon Slices
1 TBS EVOO

Instructions

Saute onion in olive oil until translucent, 2 - 3 min.
Add carrots and saute another minute.
Add Broth and bring to a boil.
Lower heat to medium, and add orzo.
After 6 min., add chicken and kale, cook for 1 - 2 min more.
Remove from heat, stir in lemon juice and add lemon slices to soup.
* If storing leftover soup, remove lemon slices - if left in soup, it will get bitter.
