Grilled Catalina Chicken Salad


Ingredients

4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
1 (6 oz) fresh baby spinach
2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
1 small cucumber, thinly sliced (about 1 1/2 cups)
3/4 Cup Catalina salad dressing, divided
Salt and Pepper

Instructions

Sprinkle chicken evenly with salt and pepper. Brush with 1/4 Cup dressing.
Grill, covered, 5 min on each side until done. Cut into 1" cubes
Combine Spinach, Tomato, and Cucumber in a large bowl. Add chicken and 1/2 cup dressing, toss well.,
