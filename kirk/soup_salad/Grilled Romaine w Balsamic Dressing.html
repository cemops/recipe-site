Grilled Romaine w/ Balsamic Dressing


Ingredients

Balsamic Dressing:

1/4 Cup EVOO
3 TBS Balsamic Vinegar
3 TBS Crumbled Gorgonzola
6 Leaves Fresh Basil, Chopped
1 Clove Garlic, Chopped
Kosher Salt & Freshly Ground Pepper

Grilled Romaine:
2 Romaine Hearts, Halved
Canola Oil
1/4 Cup Crumbled Gorgonzola, for Garnish
4 Leaves Fresh Basil, Torn for Garnish

Instructions

In a blender, combine the olive oil, balsamic vinegar, Gorgonzola, basil, garlic, a pinch of salt and pepper and 2 TBS water. Blend until the dressing is creamy and the garlic and basil are finely chopped.
Preheat the grill to medium heat.
Lightly brush the cut sides of the romaine with canola oil. Place the romaine on the grill cut-side down and cook until grill marks form and the leaves are slightly charred, 1 - 3 min. Arrange on a platter cut-side up.
Drizzle the romaine with the dressing, sprinkle with the Gorgonzola and torn basil and serve.
