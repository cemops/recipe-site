Chicken Caesar Salad


Ingredients:

Caesar Salad Dressing

3 slices country-style bread, cut into 1/2 in. cubes (optional)

4 boneless, skinless chicken breast halves, or chicken tenders

1 large Romaine lettuce or 3 hearts of romaine, chopped

1 Cup freshly shaved Parmesian cheese

Instructions:

1. Prepare the Homemade Caesar Dressing.

2. Marinate the chicken with the Caesar Dressing.

3. Grill the chicken until fully cooked.

4. Add Bread cubes, if using to the grilling grate for the last 2 - 3 minutes. You could also do the bread in the oven with italian seasoning.

5. Combine the salad with the dressing, grilled chicken, and cheese.
