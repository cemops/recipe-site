Tomato Bisque


Ingredients

2 lbs ripe tomatoes
1 medium onion, sliced thin
1 Tbsp butter
1 bay leaf
1 heaping Tbsp brown sugar
2 whole cloves
1 tsp salt
1/2 tsp pepper
2 tsp finely chopped fresh Basil
1 pt. light cream
1 cup milk
2 Tbsp chopped chives
Croutons

Instructions

Skin and seed the tomatoes.
Saute the onion in the butter for 1 min. Chop the tomatoes and add to the onions.
Add the bay leaf, sugar, cloves, salt, pepper and basil
Simmer about 25 min. until the tomatoes are thoroughly cooked, stir occasionally.
Remove the bay leaves and cloves.
Puree the tomato mixture in a food processor, and force through a coarse sieve.
Add the cream and milk.
Heat through but do not boil. Serve topped with croutons and chopped chives.
