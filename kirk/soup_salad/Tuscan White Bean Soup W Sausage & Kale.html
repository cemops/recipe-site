Tuscan White Bean Soup W/ Sausage & Kale


Ingredients

1/4 Cup EVVO
1 LB Italian Sausage - Cut Into Bite Size Pieces
1 Onion - chopped
1 Carrot - chopped
1 Stalk Celery - chopped
2 Cloves Garlic - Minced
1/2 Lb Kale, Stems removed, Chopped
4 Cups Chicken Broth
1 - 28 oz Can Cannelloni Beans, Rinsed and drained
1 tsp Rosemary, dried
1 Bay Leaf
1/4 tsp Pepper
Salt to taste
1/2 Cup Shredded Parmesian

Instructions

In a large Dutch Oven or thick bottomed soup pot, cook sausage in 1/4 Cup Olive Oil on medium heat until browned on all sides.
Add onion, carrot, celery and garlic. Continue to saute for another 3 min.
Add Kale. Saute until Kale begins to wilt.
Add Chicken broth, beans, rosemary and bay leaf.
Bring to a boil, then bring to a simmer, cover and cook on a low broil (simmer) for 30 min, stirring occasionally.
