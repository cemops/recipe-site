Chicken & Kale Ceasar Style Salad - Blue Apron


Ingredients

Makes: 2 Servings
Prep Time = 15 Min  /  Cook Time = 15 - 25 Min

2 Boneless, Skinless Chicken Thighs
3 Radishes
1 Bunch Kale
1 Clove Garlic
3 Tbs Mayonannaise
2 Tbs Flour
2 Tbs Roasted Almonds
2 Tbs Red Wine Vinegar
1/4 Cup Grated Pecorino Cheese

Instructions

Prepare The Ingredients

Peel and mince the garlic - mash into a paste
Remove the kale stems, roughly chop the leaves
Halve the radishes lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise
Roughly chop the Almonds
Chop the chicken into bite size pieces and transfer into a medium bowl

Make the Dressing and Massage the Kale

In a bowl, combine the mayo, 2/3 of the vinegar, half of the cheese, and as much of the garlic paste as you want
Drizzle with olive oil and stir to combine, season with salt and pepper
In a large bowl, combine the Kale and 3/4 of the Dressing, season with salt and pepper and toss to coat.
Using your hands, vigorously message the kale for 3 - 4 min.

Marinate the Radishes

In a bowl, combine the radishes and remaining vinegar. Drizzle with olive oil and toss to coat; season with salt and pepper to taste.
Set aside to marinate, stirring occasionally, for at least 10 min.

Coat and Cook the chicken:

Season the chopped chicken with salt and pepper; toss to coat. Add the flour; toss to thoroughly coat.
In a medium pan, heat a thin layer of oil on medium - high until hot. Add the coated chicken in a single layer. Cook, turning occasionally, 7 - 9 min. or until browned on all sides.
Transfer to a paper towel lined plate and immediately salt and pepper.

Finish the salad and Plate your dish

To the bowl of kale, add cooked chicken, marinated radishes (drained), almonds and remaining dressing.
Toss to combine and season with salt and pepper
Plate and garnish with remaining cheese.
