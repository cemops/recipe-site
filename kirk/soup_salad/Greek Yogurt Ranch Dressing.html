Greek Yogurt Ranch Dressing


Ingredients

3/4 Cup Plain Greek Yogurt
1/4 - 1/2 Cup Buttermilk
1 Clove Garlic, Finely Minced OR 1 tsp Garlic Powder
1/4 Cup Freshly Minced Parsley OR 1 TBS Dried Parsley
2 TBS Onion, Diced OR 2 tsp Onion Powder
1 tsp Salt
1/4 tsp Black Pepper
2 tsp Dijon Mustard
Juice of 1/2 Lemon

Instructions

Combine all ingredients into 2 Cup glass measuring cup.
Use immersion blender to combine.
Season to taste.
