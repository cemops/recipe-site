Honey - Lime Chicken Salad w/ tomato and avocado


Ingredients

MARINADE:

3 Tbsp fresh lime juice
3 Tbsp vegetable oil
1 Tbsp honey
1/2 tsp kosher salt
1/2 tsp granulated garlic
1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
1/4 tsp ground cayenne pepper

INGREDIENTS:

4 boneless, skinless chicken breast halves OR Chicken Tenders
2 Avocados
1 Tbsp fresh lime juice
1 pint cherry tomatoes, cut into halves
2 Tbsp mined fresh chives

Makes 4 - 6 servings

Instructions

In a small bowl, whisk the marinade ingredients until the honey is dissolved.
Place the chicken in a resealable bag, add the marinade and refrigerate for 1 - 2 hours, turning occasionally.
Remove the chicken and discard the marinade. Cook over medium - high heat, 10 - 12 min, turning once. Remove from the grill and let rest for 2 - 3 min before cutting into 1/2 in pieces.
Cut the avocado into 1/2 in pieces and toss into medium bow with lime juice. Add the chicken, tomatoes, and chives.. mix well. Adjust seasoning to your taste and serve at room temperature or slightly chilled.
