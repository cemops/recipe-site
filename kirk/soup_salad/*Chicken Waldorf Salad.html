Chicken Waldorf Salad


Ingredients

DRESSING:

3/4 Cup Mayonnaise
1/4 Cup Olive Oil
2 TBS White Balsamic Vinegar OR Italian White Wine Vinegar
Zest of 1 Lemon
2 TBS Lemon Juice
2 TBS Honey
1/4 tsp Ground Black Pepper

SALAD:
1 Bunch Red Kale, stems removed and torn into small pieces
1 LB Chicken Breast, grilled with salt and pepper (or roasted chicken)
1 Cup Candied Walnuts (1 Cup chopped walnuts, 1/4 Cup Sugar, 1 TBS Butter; Heat in a medium hot pan for 5 min, stirring often & constant at the end. Spread onto parchment paper and separate to cool)
2 - 3 Celery Ribs, roughly chopped
1 Cup Grapes, cut in half
2 Apples (green and red), cut into cubes

Instructions

In a large bowl, mix together the dressing ingredients until smooth.
Toss torn kale with salad dressing. Top with chicken, walnuts, celery, grapes and apples.
If making ahead, store prepared salad without dressing the kale. Dress salad right before serving.
