Spring Bowtie Pasta Salad


Ingredients

8 oz ( I just used the 12 oz box ) bowtie pasta
1/4 lb fresh asparagus spears (12 - 15 spears), trimmed and cut into 2 inch pieces
1 1/4 Cup frozen peas
1/4 Cup shaved fresh Parmesan cheese
1/4 Cup fat-free lime-basil vinaigrette (or stir 1 Tbs lime juice in 1/4 Cup olive oil vinaigrette)
1/4 tsp salt
1/4 tsp pepper

Instructions

Cook pasta according to directions without the salt and fat.
Add the Asparagus and Peas during the last 2 min of cooking.
Drain pasta mixture; rinse under cold water.
Combine mixture and remaining ingredients in a medium bowl, tossing well.
