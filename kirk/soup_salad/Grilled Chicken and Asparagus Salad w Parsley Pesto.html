Grilled Chicken and Asparagus Salad w/ Parsley Pesto


Ingredients

1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast - or chicken tenders
1/3 Cup plus 2 1/2 TBS Olive Oil
Salt
Fresh Ground Pepper
1 lb Asparagus
1 Clove Garlic
2 TBS Water
1 1/2 Cups loosely packed parsley
1 TBS Lemon Juice
3 Heads Bibb Lettuce - OR - 1 Leaf Lettuce and 1 Romaine Lettuce

Instructions

Combine the garlic, water, parsley, lemon juice, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/3 Cup olive oil. Puree until smooth.
Marinate the chicken with olive oil, salt and pepper.
Prep and cut the asparagus into 2" pieces. Coat with olive oil, salt and pepper.
Grill the chicken and asparagus for 10 min, turning as necessary.
In a large bowl, toss the lettuce, chicken, and asparagus with half of the vinaigrette.
Put the salad on plates, drizzle the remaining vinaigrette over the salad on plates.
