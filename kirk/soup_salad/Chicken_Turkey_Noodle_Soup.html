Chicken / Turkey Noodle Soup

Serves 5


Ingredients

2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
2 medium carrots, peeled and chopped
2 stalks celery, sliced
1 medium yellow onion, chopped
1 leek, cleaned and chopped (whites & light green parts only)
Kosher salt, to taste
Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
3 cloves garlic, minced
4 cups turkey stock (or low-sodium chicken broth)
3 cups water
Bouquet garni: thyme, rosemary, bay leaf
1 tsp dried oregano
Pinch crushed red pepper flakes
1 (12-oz) package egg noodles
3 cups cooked shredded turkey

Instructions

In a large pot over medium heat, heat olive oil. Add carrots, celery, onion, and leek; cook until soft (~5 minutes). Season with salt and pepper.
Add garlic and cook until fragrant (~1 minute).
Add broth, water, thyme, rosemary, bay leaf, oregano, and red pepper flakes. Bring to a boil.
Add egg noodles, cover, and cook until pasta is tender (~7 minutes). Discard thyme, rosemary, and bay leaf.
Add turkey and simmer until warmed through (~5 minutes). Adjust salt and pepper to taste.
