Crab and Avocado Salad


Ingredients

1 Avocado
3 TBS Mayonnaise
3 Cups Watercress, Chopped (can substitute arugula)
1 tsp Cumin
1/2 tsp Paprika
16 oz Canned Crab (last time got cheep stuff, next time try lump crab)
2 Stalk Medium Celery
2 TBS Fresh Lime Juice

Instructions

In a large bowl, mix mayo, lime juice, cumin and paprika
Add crabmeat and celery. Mix well; add salt and freshly ground pepper to taste.
Gently stir in avocado cubes.
Divide watercress on plates; top with salad
