Chopped Chicken Salad


Ingredients:

6 Boneless, skinless chicken thighs (about 3 oz each)

Marinade:

2 TBS Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO)
2 TBS Freshly minced rosemary leaves
1 TBS Fresh lemon juice
1 TBS Minced garlic
1 1/2 tsp Kosher salt
1/4 tsp Ground black pepper

Dressing:

1/2 cup Mayonnaise
3 TBS Dijon mustard
1/8 cup Honey
2 TBS Fresh lemon juice
Kosher salt, to taste
Ground black pepper, to taste

Salad:

2 Hearts of romaine, chopped
1 cup Cherry tomatoes, quartered
2 Large avocados, diced
1/2 Small red onion, finely diced
4 Slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
3 Hard-boiled eggs, cut into wedges

Directions:

In a small bowl, whisk together all marinade ingredients.
Marinate the chicken in the mixture for at least 1 hour, turning occasionally.
Preheat grill to medium heat. Place chicken, smooth (skin) side down first, on grill.
Grill chicken for 8-10 minutes, turning once or twice, until fully cooked.
Remove chicken from grill and let rest for 3-5 minutes.
Assemble salad with romaine, tomatoes, avocados, red onion, bacon, and egg wedges.
In a separate bowl, whisk all dressing ingredients until smooth.
Slice chicken and serve over the salad, drizzling with dressing as desired.
