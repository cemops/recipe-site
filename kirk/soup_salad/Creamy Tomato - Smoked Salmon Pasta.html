Creamy Tomato - Smoked Salmon Pasta


Ingredients

1 (8oz) package pasta (fresh)
1 TBS Butter
1 TBS EVOO
3 Shallots, minced
1 (28 oz) can peeled Italian Tomatoes, coarsely chopped, juices reserved
1 TBS Italian Herb Seasoning
Salt to Taste
White Pepper to Taste
1 1/2 Cups Heavy Whipping Cream
10 oz Smoked Salmon
1/2 Cup Dry White Wine
1/2 Cup Dry Cherry
1 TBS Chopped Basil
3 TBS grated Parmesan Cheese

Instructions

Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook pasta according to pasta instructions. Aldente
Melt butter and olive oil in a pot over medium heat and cook shallots until soft and translucent, about 5 min. Add tomatoes and 1/2 of the juice. Season with Italian herbs, salt and white pepper. Cover and simmer 10 min. Remove pot from heat and puree until smooth. Stir in cream.
Return pot to stove and bring to a boil. Add white wine, sherry and smoked salmon. Season with salt, white pepper and basil and heat until warmed through, but do not boil.
Serve pasta with sauce and freshly grated Parmesan Cheese.
