Smoked Ham and Bean Soup


Ingredients

1 Lb dry great northern beans
8 Cups water
1/2 tsp salt
1 ham hock (leftover from smoked ham)
1 Cup chopped carrots
1/2 stalk celery, chopped
1 Cup chopped onion
1 tsp minced garlic
1 tsp ground mustard
2 bay leaves
2 + Cups chopped ham
1/2 tsp ground white pepper

Instructions

Soak beans in cold water for 2 hours ** Last time did overnight **
Drain and rinse beans, sorting out any broken or discolored ones. In a large pot over high heat, bring the water to a boil. ** Last time needed to boil at least 30 min then...** Add the salt and beans and remove from heat. Let beans sit in the hot water for at least 60 min.
Return the pot to high heat and place the ham bone, carrots, celery, onion, garlic, mustard and bay leaves in the pot. Stir well, bring to a boil, reduce heat to low and simmer for 30 more min. Season with white pepper to taste.
