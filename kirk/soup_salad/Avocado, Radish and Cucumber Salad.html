Avocado, Radish and Cucumber Salad


Ingredients

2 Medium Sized Radishes, chopped
1 Large Cucumber, cut into quarters lengthwise, then chopped
1/4 Cup Red Onion, chopped
1 Avocado, cut into small chunks
1/4 Cup Chopped Fresh Parsley
Juice of 1/2 Lemon
1/2 tsp Dried Dill
3 TBS EVOO
Salt, to taste

Instructions

Add all ingredients into a large bowl. Toss well and serve.
