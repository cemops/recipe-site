Warm Kale Salad


Ingredients

1 1/2 Packages Kale
1/2 Red Onion - Thinly Sliced
1 Clove Garlic - Thinly sliced
1 Avocado - Sliced
4 Eggs - Soft Boiled or poached
4 - 6 Radishes - chopped
1/4 Lemon
Salt & Pepper
Optional:
Cannelloni Beans
Toasted Walnuts
Dried Cranberries

Instructions

Soft Boil or poach the Eggs.
In a nonstick skillet, heat1 tsp of oil over low heat. Add onions and garlic and sauté until onions are soft and caramelized, about 15 minutes.
Add kale and cook, stirring, until kale is warm and wilted, about 2 minutes. Add a squeeze of lemon, toss, and then plate kale.
Add egg, avocado and radishes to the kale.
