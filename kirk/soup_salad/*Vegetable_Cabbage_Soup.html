Vegetable Cabbage Soup


Ingredients

2 TBS EVOO
1 Lg Onion, Chopped
2 Carrots, Chopped
2 Stalks Celery, Chopped
1/2 tsp Chili Powder
Kosher Salt
Fresh Ground Pepper
1 - 15 oz Can White Beans, Drained and Rinsed
2 Cloves Garlic, Minced
1 tsp Thyme Leaves
4 Cups Chicken Broth
2 Cups Water
1/2 Large Head Cabbage, Chopped
1 - 15 oz Can Chopped Fire Roasted Tomatoes (Hunts ?)
Pinch Red Pepper Flakes
2 tsp Freshly Chopped Parsley, Plus More For Garnish

Instructions

In a large pot (or Dutch Oven) over medium heat, heat olive oil. Add onion, carrots, and celery, and season w/ salt, pepper, and chili powder.
Cook, stirring often, until vegetables are soft, about 5 - 6 min. (not likely, probably longer.... idiots)
Stir in beans, garlic, and thyme and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.  Add broth and water, and bring to a simmer.
Stir in tomatoes and cabbage and simmer until cabbage is wilted, about 6 min (again, probably longer.,...)
Remove from heat and stir in red pepper flakes and parsley. Season to taste with salt and pepper. Garnish with parsley if using.
