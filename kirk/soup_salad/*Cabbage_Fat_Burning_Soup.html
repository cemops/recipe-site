Cabbage Fat Burning Soup


Ingredients:

3 Carrots, chopped

3 Onions, chopped

2 (16 oz) cans whole peeled tomatoes, with liquid

1 Large head cabbage, chopped

1 (1 oz) envelope dry onion soup mix

1 (15 oz) can cut green beans, drained

2 quarts tomato juice

2 green bell peppers, diced

10 stalks celery, chopped

1 (14 oz) can beef broth

Instructions:

1. Place all ingredients into the largest stock pot available.

2. Simmer over medium heat until the vegetables are cooked and tender.

3. Serve warm and enjoy this healthy, flavorful soup!
