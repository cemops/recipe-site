Potato Chowder / with Cheddar Cheese Toasts - Blue Apron


Ingredients

Makes: 2 Servings
Prep Time: 15 Minute    Cook Time: 35 - 45 Minutes

1 Small Baguette
3/4 Cup Milk
2 Stalks Celery
2 Russet Potatoes
1 Carrot
1 Yellow Onion
1 Bunch Chives
2 oz Cheddar Cheese
2 Tbs All Purpose Flour
1 Tbs Chowder Spice Blend (Mustard Powder, Onion Powder, Sweet Paprika, Garlic Powder, Cayenne Pepper, Smoked Paprika, Dried Parsley Flakes)

Instructions

Prepare The Ingredients

Pre Heat the oven to 475
Wash and dry the produce
Halve the baguette, lengthwise
Peel the carrot, cut in half lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise.
Small dice the celery
Peel and medium dice the potatoes
Peel and small dice the onion
Small dice the cheese
thinly slice the chives

Cook The Vegetables

In a large pot, heat 2 Tsp of olive oil on medium-high until hot.
Add the Carrot, Celery, Potatoes and Onion; Season with salt and pepper.
Cook, stirring occasionally, 7 - 9 min, or until slightly softened.

Make the Cheese Sauce

While the vegetables cook, in a small pt, heat 2 Tbs of oil on medium-high until hot.
Add the flour and half the spice blend. Coo, whisking frequently, 30 sec - 1 min.
Slowly whisk in the milk: cook, whisking frequently, 2 to 3 min, or until slightly thickened.
Add the cheese and cook, whisking frequently, 1 to 3 min, or until thoroughly combined.
Season with salt and pepper to taste. Turn off heat

Start The Chowder

To the pot of vegetables; add the remaining spice blend. Cook, stirring frequently, 30 sec to 1 min.
Add 3 1/2 cups of water; season with salt and pepper.
Heat to boiling on high. Once boiling, reduce the heat to medium and cook, stirring occasionally, 15 - 17 min, or until the potatoes are tender when pierced with a fork.
Turn off the heat. Using a spoon, carefully mash the potatoes against the side of the pot to achieve your desired consistency; stir to thoroughly combine.

Make The Cheese Toasts

While the chowder cooks, place the baguette on a sheet pan, cut sides up.
Spread 2/3 of the cheese sauce onto the cut sides of the baguette.
Bake 8 to 10 minutes, or until the cheese and baguette are browned around the edges.

Finish The Chowder and Plate Your Dish

Add the remaining cheese sauce to the pot of chowder. Cook on medium-high, stirring occasionally, 3 -5 min or until thoroughly combined and heated through.
Season with salt and pepper to taste. Divide between 2 bowels, garnish with a drizzle of olive oil, season with pepper.
