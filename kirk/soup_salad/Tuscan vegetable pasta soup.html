Tuscan vegetable pasta soup


Ingredients

Serves: 6 - 8

Prep Time: 10 Min | Cook Time: 20 Min

2 TBS EVOO
4 Cloves Garlic, minced
1 Medium Yellow Onion, diced
1/2 Cup Carrot, chopped
1/2 Cup Celery, chopped
1 Medium Zucchini, sliced and quartered
1 - 15 oz Can Diced Tomatoes
6 Cups low sodium Vegetable Stock
2 Tbs Tomato Paste
6 - 8 oz Whole Wheat Pasta
1 - 15 oz Can White Beans
2 Large Handfuls Baby Spinach
6 Dorot Basil Cubes or 6 tsp Minced Basil
Salt and Pepper, to Taste
Fresh Chopped Parsley, for garnish

Instructions

Drizzle olive oil in a large pot and add in diced onion and garlic. Saute over medium high heat for 2-3 min, or until onions are translucent. Add in carrots, celery and zucchini and cook for an additional 5-6 min, stirring occasionally. Add in diced tomatoes, salt, pepper, and cook for 1-2 min.
Pour in vegetable stock and tomato paste, and bring soup to a boil. Once soup is boiling, add in pasta of choice. Cook for 10 min. or until pasta is al dente.
Add in spinach, white beans, Dorot basil cubes (or 6 tsp basil), and salt and pepper. Stir to combine, then remove from heat. Once spinach is wilted and herbs have dissolved, pour into large bowls and top with fresh parsley.
