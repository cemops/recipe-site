Caesar Salad Dressing


Ingredients (Full Batch)



2 small garlic cloves, minced

1 tsp anchovy paste

2 Tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice (1 lemon)

1 tsp Dijon mustard

1 tsp Worcestershire sauce

1 cup mayonnaise

1/2 cup freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano

1/4 tsp salt

1/4 tsp ground black pepper

Instructions



1. Whisk garlic, anchovy paste, lemon juice, Dijon, and Worcestershire.

2. Add mayonnaise, Parmigiano-Reggiano, salt, and pepper.

3. Whisk until smooth and creamy.

4. Taste and adjust seasoning.

5. Refrigerate up to one week.


Single-Serving House Caesar (1 Salad)

Designed for one salad · No leftovers · Uses real anchovies


Ingredients



1 small pinch garlic, very finely minced

About 1/4 Ortiz anchovy, smashed to a paste

Fresh lemon juice, added drop by drop

1 small dollop Dijon mustard (pea-sized)

2 small dashes Worcestershire sauce

3 heaping spoonfuls mayonnaise

2 generous spoonfuls Parmigiano-Reggiano, plus more to taste

Freshly ground black pepper

(Optional) 1/2 tsp olive oil from anchovy tin

Method



1. Smash garlic and anchovy into a smooth paste.

2. Stir in Dijon and Worcestershire.

3. Mix in mayonnaise until creamy.

4. Fold in Parmesan.

5. Add lemon a few drops at a time, tasting as you go.

6. Finish with black pepper and anchovy oil if using.


Chef’s Notes & Tips

• Use real anchovies. High-quality anchovies add depth without harsh saltiness.

• Lemon control matters. Caesar should be bright, not citrus-forward.

• Fixes:
Too sharp → Parmesan first, then mayo
Too thick → tiny splash of water or lemon
Flat → black pepper or Worcestershire

• Scaling: The single-serving version is designed for one salad with no waste.
