Luscious Lima Bean Soup


Ingredients

1 Lb Dry Lima Beans
4 Cups Water - for Lima Beans
5 Carrots, Chopped
1 Leek, Bulb Only, Chopped
2 Stalks Celery, Chopped
2 TBS Shallots, Minced
8 Cups Water - 4 Cups for Broth, 4 Cups four Soup
Olive Oil

Instructions

Brink 4 Cups water to a boil, add lima beans and boil for 2 - 3 min. Remove from heat and soak overnight.
In a soup pot, saute veggies in olive oil until onions and celery are translucent. Add lima beans and saute for another 2 - 3 min.
Bring 4 Cups of water to a boil and add Veggie - Better than Bouillon
Add broth to veggies and beans, add remaining 4 Cups of water. Simmer over a low flame for 1 1/2 hours.
