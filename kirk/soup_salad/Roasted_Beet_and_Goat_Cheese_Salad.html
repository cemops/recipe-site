Roasted Beet and Goat Cheese Salad


Ingredients

6 Medium Beets, scrubbed (I like to do 3 Red and 3 Yellow)
6 Cups Arugula Salad
1 Avocado, Sliced
4 Oz. Goat Cheese, Crumbled
1/2 Cup Chopped Toasted Walnuts

Dressing

1/2 Cup EVOO
1/4 Cup Balsamic Vinegar
1 TBS Maple Syrup
2 tsp Dijon Mustard
Kosher Salt
Freshly Ground Black Pepper

Instructions

Preheat oven to 400 degrees. Wrap each beet in foil and place on baking sheet. Roast until tender, about 1 hour. Let rest then peel and cut into wedges.
Make Vinaigrette: In a jar, shake together olive oil, vinegar, maple syrup, and dijon until fully combined, season w/ salt and pepper.
Place arugula in a large serving bowl and lightly dress with vinaigrette. Top w/ beets, avocado, goat cheese, and walnuts. Toss gently and add more vinaigrette as needed
