Spinach Salad with Chicken

Serves 2


Ingredients

4 Cups fresh baby spinach
6 oz grilled chicken breast strips
12 cherry tomatoes or 1 chopped roma tomato
2 Tbs chopped walnuts, toasted
2 Tbs crumbled blue cheese
4 Tbs light dressing (e.g., raspberry-walnut vinaigrette)

Instructions

Combine spinach, chicken strips, and tomatoes in a small bowl.
Sprinkle with toasted walnuts and crumbled blue cheese.
Drizzle with dressing and serve immediately.
