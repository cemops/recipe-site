Garden Minestrone


Ingredients:

2 tsp EVOO
1 Cup Onion, Chopped
2 tsp Fresh Oregano, Chopped
4 Garlic Cloves, Minced
3 Cups Yellow Squash, Chopped
3 Cups Zucchini, Chopped
1 Cup Carrot, Chopped
1 Cup Fresh Corn
4 Cups Tomato, Chopped
3 - 14 oz Cans Chicken broth
1/2 Cup Pasta
1 - 15 oz can Great Northern Beans, rinsed and drained
1 - 6 oz Package Spinach
3/4 tsp Salt
1/2 tsp Freshly Ground Pepper
1 Cup Grated Asiago Cheese
Coarsely Ground Pepper

Instructions:

Heat oil in a dutch oven over medium high heat. Add onion and saute 3 min or until softened. Add oregano and garlic, saute 1 min.
Stir in squash, zucchini, carrot and corn; saute 5 min or until veggies are tender. Remove from heat.
Place 3 Cups tomato and 1 can broth in blender; process until smooth. Add tomato mixture to pan; return pan to heat.
Stir in remaining 1 Cup tomato and remaining 2 cans broth; bring mixture to a boil. Reduce heat, and simmer 20 min.
Add pasta and beans to pan; cook 10 min or until pasta is tender, stirring occasionally. Remove from heat. Stir in Spinach, salt, and 1/2 teaspoon pepper. Ladle soup into individual bowls; top with cheese. Garnish with coarsely ground black pepper.
