Easy Marry Me Chicken Soup

Serves 8 • Cozy, Creamy Comfort Soup


Ingredients

4 chicken breasts (or shredded rotisserie chicken – see note below)
2 tbsp olive oil
3 tbsp all-purpose seasoning
1 tbsp sun-dried tomato oil
1/2 yellow onion, diced
4 garlic cloves, chopped
1/2 cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
2 tbsp tomato paste
6 cups unsalted chicken broth
2 cups pasta shells
5 oz baby spinach
1 cup heavy cream
1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
1 tbsp Italian seasoning
1/2 tbsp crushed red pepper flakes
Salt & pepper, to taste

Instructions

Season chicken breasts with all-purpose seasoning.
Heat a Dutch oven or large pot with olive oil over medium heat.
Cook chicken until internal temperature reaches 160°F; remove and set aside.
Add sun-dried tomato oil and diced onion; sauté until translucent.
Add chopped sun-dried tomatoes and garlic; sauté 1–2 minutes.
Stir in tomato paste until well combined.
Pour in chicken broth, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes; bring to a boil.
Add pasta shells and cook until al dente.
Shred or chop chicken and wash spinach.
Add cream, Parmesan, chicken, and spinach; stir well.
Cook 10 more minutes until spinach is wilted and soup thickens.
Adjust seasoning and serve with extra Parmesan and parsley.

Chef’s Note

You can substitute a store-bought rotisserie chicken for the chicken breasts. Simply shred the meat and add it when the recipe calls for adding the cooked chicken. This is a great shortcut for busy weeknights.
