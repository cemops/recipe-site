Greek Chicken Nachos


Ingredients

2 (6 in) whole-wheat pitas (Original recipe calls for 3)
2 1/2 TBS EVOO - Divided
2 TBS Red Whine Vinegar
1/4 tsp Black Pepper
4 Cups Shredded Romaine Lettuce
1 Cup Grape Tomatoes, Halved Lengthwise
1 Cup English Cucumber, Chopped
10 Pitted Kalamata / Black Olives, Halved Lengthwise
Chicken Breast of 1 Whole Rotisserie Chicken, Shredded
3 oz Part - Skim Mozzarella Cheese, Shredded (about 3/4 Cup)
2 oz Feta Cheese, Crumbled (about 1/2 Cup)
2 TBS Coarsely Chopped Fresh Oregano

Instructions

Preheat oven to 400 degrees
Split each pita in half horizontally. Cut each pita into bite size pieces and place on a foil lined baking sheet, spray with olive oil, and bake for 8 min. or until browned. Let cool for 10 min.
Combine oil, vinegar, and pepper in a medium bowl, stirring with a whisk.
Add lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, and olives to bowl; toss to coat
Arrange pita chips on baking sheet, top with chicken and cheese and bake at 400 degrees for 3+ min until the cheese is melted.
Top with lettuce mixture and chopped oregano.
