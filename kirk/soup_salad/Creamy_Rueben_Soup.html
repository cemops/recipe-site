Creamy Reuben Soup

Serves 6


Ingredients

2 tbsp butter
1 medium onion, diced
2 cloves garlic, minced
3 tbsp all-purpose flour
4 cups chicken broth
1½ cups half-and-half or whole milk
2 cups cooked corned beef, chopped
1½ cups sauerkraut, drained
1½ cups shredded Swiss cheese
1 tbsp Dijon mustard
1 tsp caraway seeds (optional)
Salt & freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions

In a large pot over medium heat, melt butter. Add onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
Sprinkle in flour and cook, stirring constantly, for 1–2 minutes.
Gradually whisk in chicken broth until smooth.
Stir in half-and-half and bring to a gentle simmer.
Add corned beef, sauerkraut, Dijon mustard, and caraway seeds. Simmer 10 minutes.
Reduce heat to low and stir in Swiss cheese until melted and smooth.
Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot.
