Strawberry Blueberry Salad with Balsamic Vinaigrette


Ingredients:

Salad

8 cups Mixed salad greens
1 cup Strawberries, washed and chopped
1 cup Blueberries, washed
4 oz Feta cheese, Crumbled goat cheese or blue cheese
½ cup Walnuts or pecans, chopped and lightly toasted (optional)

Balsamic Vinaigrette

½ cup Olive oil
2¼ tablespoons Balsamic vinegar
1 clove Garlic, minced
1 teaspoon Oregano
1 teaspoon Salt
½ teaspoon Black pepper

Instructions:

The Salad

Add mixed salad greens to a large bowl.
Remove leaves from strawberries and cut into thirds.
Add strawberries and blueberries to the bowl.
Add crumbled cheese over the top.
Lightly toast chopped walnuts or pecans in a skillet and add to the salad (if using).
Toss salad with 2 to 3 tablespoons of balsamic vinaigrette.

Balsamic Vinaigrette

Combine olive oil, balsamic vinegar, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper in a mason jar.
Seal and shake well until emulsified.
