Greek Spring Soup


Ingredients:

6 Cups Chicken Broth (3 cans)

1 1/2 Cups Shredded Chicken (Rotisserie from Buehlers)

2 Tbs Olive Oil

3/4 Cup Diced Onion (1/2 medium onion)

1 Bay Leaf

1/3 Cup Arborio Rice

1 Large Egg

Juice of 1/2 Lemon

1 Cup Chopped Asparagus

1 Cup Diced Carrots

1/2 Cup Fresh Chopped Dill - Divided

Kosher Salt and Ground Pepper to taste

Instructions:

- In a large stock pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Saute onions until soft and translucent, about 5 min. Add chicken broth, 1/4 Cup dill, bay leaf and bring to a boil.

- Add rice and reduce heat to medium low, and simmer about 10 min. Add carrots and asparagus and cook an additional 10 - 15 min, or until rice is cooked and veggies are tender. Add chicken and continue to simmer on low while preparing egg and lemon juice mixture.

- In a separate bowl, combine egg, lemon juice and 2 Tbs water.

- Slowly whisk 1/2 Cup of the hot stock into the egg mixture, stirring constantly, to keep the eggs from curdling.

- Slowly whisk the egg/lemon broth back into the stock pot. Soup will thicken slightly. Turn off heat and remove bay leaf.

- Add remaining fresh dill, and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
