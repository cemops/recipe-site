Béchamel (White Sauce)

Yield: About 1 Cup


Ingredients

2 tablespoons butter
2 tablespoons flour
1 1/4 cups milk, heated
Salt & freshly ground pepper

Instructions

Melt butter in heavy-bottom saucepan.
Add flour and cook, stirring constantly, about 2 minutes (do NOT brown).
Add hot milk and continue stirring as sauce thickens; bring to a boil.
Season with salt & pepper; reduce heat and cook 2–3 minutes more.
To cool for later use, cover with wax paper or a thin film of milk to prevent skin.

Notes

Cheese Sauce: Stir in 1/2 cup grated Cheddar during the last 2 minutes of cooking. Add a pinch of cayenne pepper.

How hot should the milk be? Warm gently on low heat until tiny bubbles form at the edges, then remove from heat.
