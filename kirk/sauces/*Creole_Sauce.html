Creole Sauce

Makes about 2 ½ cups

Ingredients

Seasoning Mix

2 Whole bay leaves
¾ tsp Dried oregano leaves
½ tsp Salt
½ tsp White pepper
½ tsp Ground red pepper
½ tsp Sweet paprika
½ tsp Black pepper
½ tsp Dried thyme leaves
½ tsp Dried sweet basil leaves

Main Ingredients

4 TBS Unsalted butter
1 Cup Peeled and chopped tomatoes
¾ Cup Chopped onions
¾ Cup Chopped celery
¾ Cup Chopped green bell peppers
1½ tsp Minced garlic
1¼ Cups Chicken stock
1 Cup Canned tomato sauce
1 tsp Sugar
½ tsp Tabasco sauce

Instructions

Combine all seasoning mix ingredients in a small bowl and set aside.
Melt butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
Add tomatoes, onions, celery, and bell peppers to the skillet; stir to coat.
Add minced garlic and seasoning mix, stirring thoroughly.
Sauté until onions are transparent, about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Add chicken stock, tomato sauce, sugar, and Tabasco; bring to a boil.
Reduce heat and simmer uncovered until vegetables are tender and flavors are married, about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
Remove bay leaves before serving.




Serving Suggestions

This classic Creole sauce is rich, savory, and full of Louisiana flavor. Serve it over:

  • Grilled chicken
  • Sautéed or grilled shrimp
  • Steamed white rice
