Sweet Rub For Grilled / Smoked Pork and Chicken


Ingredients

1/4 Cup Dark Brown Sugar
1 TBS Coarse Sea Salt
2 tsp Cracked Black Pepper
2 tsp Smoked Paprika
2 tsp Garlic Powder
2 tsp Onion Powder
1 tsp Ground Mustard
1/2 tsp Cayenne Pepper

Instructions

Combine all ingredients in a small bowl, stir well using a fork to break up any clumps.
Store BBQ rub in an airtight container.
