Remoulade Sauce


Ingredients:

1 1/4 Cups Mayonnaise

1/4 Cup Mustard (Creole Mustard if Possible)

1 TBS Sweet Paprika

1 - 2 tsp Cajun or Creole Seasoning

2 tsp Prepared Horseradish

1 tsp Pickle Juice

1 tsp Tabasco

1 Large Clove Garlic, Smashed and Minced

Half Ingredients:

1/2 Cup Mayonnaise

2 TBS Mustard (Creole Mustard if Possible)

1/2 TBS Sweet Paprika

1/2 - 1 tsp Cajun or Creole Seasoning

1 tsp Prepared Horseradish

1/2 tsp Pickle Juice

1/2 tsp Tabasco

1 Small Clove Garlic, Smashed and Minced



Instructions:

Mix all ingredients together in a medium bowl. The Remoulade is better if let set for a few hours to let the flavors meld. Keep refrigerated.
