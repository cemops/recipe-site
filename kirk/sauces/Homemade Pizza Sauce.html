Homemade Pizza Sauce


Ingredients

1 can (28 ounces) whole, peeled San Marzano tomatoes
1 teaspoon salt (or to taste, start with ½ teaspoon and then adjust as needed)
1/4 teaspoon coarsely ground black pepper
** 1 teaspoon dried basil (optional) (or 2 tablespoons minced fresh basil) - I didn't use the last time!!
1/2 - 1 teaspoon dried oregano (optional) (or 1 tablespoon minced fresh oregano)
1 - 2 teaspoons granulated garlic powder (sandy, not the fine powder)
(or 5 cloves of fresh garlic, minced or crushed)
1 tablespoon red wine vinegar, balsamic vinegar, or lemon juice, or a combination of both - I used balsamic last time!!

Instructions

Drain the tomatoes briefly to remove some of the water.
Mash the tomatoes gently to a finely chopped consistency and drain again much longer.
Stir all the ingredients together, adding the salt gradually, to taste.
In an authentic Napoletana marinara pizza, made with true San Marzano sauce, you would use only oregano, and not in the sauce but as a garnish after the bake. Do not cook this sauce–the tomatoes are already cooked when they go in the can and they will cook again on the pizza. This sauce will keep for 1 week in the refrigerator.
