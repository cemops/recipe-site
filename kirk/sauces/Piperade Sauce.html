Piperade Sauce


Ingredients

2 tsp Olive Oil
1 Large Onion
1 Large Red Pepper, cut into thin strips
2 Garlic Cloves, crushed
Salt & Pepper
1/2 tsp Piment d'Espelette, chille powder, or hot paprikia
8 ripe Cherry Tomoatoes, halved
1 - 2 tsp sweet vermouth

Instructions

Heat oil in a frying pan, add the onion and cook over medium hea for 2 - 3 min, until pale or softened
Add the red bell pepper and garlic. Season and add the Piment d'Espelette
Stir in the tomatoes, vermouth. Cook until the pepper and tomatoes are softened and the sauce is thick thick and mushy , stirring frequently.
Add a little water if the sauce starts drying out
