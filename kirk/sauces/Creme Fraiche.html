Creme Fraiche


Ingredients

2 TBS Cultured Buttermilk
Remaining Heavy Whipping Cream to Make 1 Cup
Cheesecloth

Instructions

Clean 4 mason jars and lids
Add 2 TBS of the buttermilk to each jar
Fill remainder of each jar with cream and stir
Cover with cheesecloth and screw on lid without the cover
Place in oven on a cooking sheet for 24 hours.
Remove cheesecloth, stir and place cover and lid on and refrigerate 24 hours
Mark and freeze 3 of the jars
