Blue Cheese Butter

Make at least 1 day ahead for best flavor


Ingredients

1/2 cup butter, softened
1/2 cup crumbled blue cheese (2 oz)
1 Tbsp snipped fresh parsley
1 Tbsp snipped fresh basil OR 1 tsp dried basil (crushed)
1 clove garlic, minced

Instructions

Mix all ingredients together.
Refrigerate at least 1 day to allow flavors to meld.
Before serving, set out the butter for at least a couple hours to soften.
