Teriyaki Sauce


Ingredients

1 Cup Soy Sauce
1 Cup Sake
2 TBS Brown Sugar
2 Cloves Garlic
Fresh Ginger

Instructions

Add soy sauce to medium sauce pan
Mix Sake and brown sugar then add to the sa dbuce pan
Add garlic and ginger
Bring to a boil, mixing frequently.
Reduce heat, cover and simmer for 20 - 30 min.
