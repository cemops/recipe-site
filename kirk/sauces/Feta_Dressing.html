Creamy Feta Dressing


Ingredients:

1/2 Cup Mayonnaise or Plain Greek Yogurt (yogurt was better last time)

1/2 Cup Sour Cream or Creme Fraiche

6 Oz Feta Cheese, Crumbled

2 TBS Milk (skipped the milk last time, made it thicker)

1 Clove Garlic, Roughly Chopped

2 TBS Chopped Fresh Dill

1/4 tsp Dried Oregano

1/4 tsp Salt

1/4 tsp Ground Black Pepper

Instructions:

- Combine all ingredients in a food processor (or bowl for immersion blender). Process until well-blended and creamy.

- Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.

Notes:

- Make ahead: This dressing will keep nicely refrigerated for 4 days. However, it will thicken up a bit in the fridge; before serving, whisk well then add a bit of milk, little by little, to the desired consistency.
