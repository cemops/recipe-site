Creamy Mushroom Wild Rice Skillet


Ingredients:

2 tablespoons olive oil
1 small onion, diced
2 cloves garlic, minced
8 ounces mushrooms, sliced
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon pepper
1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning
2 cups chicken or vegetable broth
1 cup wild rice**
1/4 cup heavy cream
1/2 cup shredded Parmesan cheese

Directions:

Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
Add diced onions and sliced mushrooms. Cook until vegetables are soft.
Add minced garlic and stir for 1 minute.
Season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
Add broth and rice. Bring to a boil.
Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes.
Remove from heat and let stand 5 minutes.
Stir in heavy cream and Parmesan cheese before serving.





Notes:

**Cooking times for wild rice can vary. This recipe uses a wild rice blend that cooks in 15 minutes. Check your package instructions. You may substitute any long grain rice such as white, basmati, or jasmine.
