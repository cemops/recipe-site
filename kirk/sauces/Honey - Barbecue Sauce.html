Honey - Barbecue Sauce


Ingredients

Prep:  5 Min.
Cook:  5 Min.
Cut in half for 2 Servings

INGREDIENTS - FULL:

2 Cups Ketchup
1/2 Cup white Vinegar
1/2 Cup Honey (they used gallberry honey)
1/2 Cup Water
2 tsp dried crushed green pepper
1 Tbs minced onion
2 Tbs Worcestershire sauce
1/4 tsp ground black pepper
Dash of garlic powder
Dash of ground red pepper

INGREDIENTS - HALF:

1 Cups Ketchup
1/4 Cup white Vinegar
1/4 Cup Honey (they used gallberry honey)
1/4 Cup Water
1 tsp dried crushed green pepper
1/2 Tbs minced onion
1 Tbs Worcestershire sauce
1/8 tsp ground black pepper
Dash of garlic powder
Dash of ground red pepper

Instructions

Bring all ingredients to a boil in a large saucepan over medium - high heat, stirring often. Serve with pork or poultry. Makes 3 1/2 Cups.
