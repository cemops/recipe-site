Blue Cheese Dressing


Ingredients

1 Cup Sour Cream
1 Cup Mayo
2/3 Cup Buttermilk
1 tsp Worcestershire
1/2 tsp Sugar
1/2 tsp Dry Mustard
1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
1/2 tsp White Pepper
1/2 tsp Salt
4 oz Crumbled Blue Cheese

Instructions

Combine all ingredients EXCEPT blue cheese
Whisk together thoroughly
Add the blue cheese cover and refrigerate overnight
Taste and adjust salt and white pepper as needed

**Last time I halved the recipe in it plenty for our steak salad and a couple of lunch salads**
