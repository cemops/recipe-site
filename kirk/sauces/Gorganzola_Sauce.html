Gorgonzola Sauce

Classic Creamy Steak Sauce


Ingredients

1 tbsp unsalted butter
2 tbsp minced shallot
1 cup sour cream
4 oz crumbled gorgonzola cheese (about 1 cup)
Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Instructions

In a medium saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter.
Add the minced shallot and cook until softened, about 2 minutes.
Stir in the sour cream and crumbled gorgonzola cheese.
Cook, stirring gently, until the sauce begins to simmer and the cheese is melted, about 3 minutes.
Remove from heat, season with black pepper to taste, and cover to keep warm.

Serving Notes

  • Serve warm over grilled or pan-seared steaks.
  • Excellent with roasted potatoes, pork chops, or grilled vegetables.
  • If sauce thickens too much, thin with a splash of cream or milk.
