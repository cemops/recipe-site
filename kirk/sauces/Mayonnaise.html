Mayonnaise


Ingredients

Makes about 2 cups

1 large egg, or 1 small egg plus 1 egg yoke
1 - 1 1/2 cups vegetable oil (can use as little as 3/4 Cup oil up to 1 1/2 Cups)
1 TBS cider vinegar
1 tsp Tabasco sauce
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp white pepper

Instructions

NEW WAY:

Combine all ingredients into the immersion blender mixer
Place immersion blender to the bottom of the mixer and blend for a few seconds
Slowly bring immersion blender up through the mixture, mixing the contents as you go, about 1 min.
Be sure there are no oil puddles and that the ingredients are fully combined.

OLD WAY

Place the egg in a food processor; blend about 30 seconds.
With the machine on, slowly add the oil in a thin, steady stream.
When the mixture becomes thick and creamy, add the vinegar and blend about 30 seconds
Add the Tabasco, salt and white pepper, and process until mixture is well blended, about 1 min, pushing the sides down once or twice with a rubber spatula.
Refrigerate at least 30 min before serving.
