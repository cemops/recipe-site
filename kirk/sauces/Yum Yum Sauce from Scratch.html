Yum Yum Sauce from Scratch


Ingredients

3/4 Cup Vegetable Oil
1 Egg
1/2 tsp Dried Mustard
1/2 tsp Salt
2 tsp Sugar
Dash Cayenne Pepper
1 1/2 TBS White Vinegar
1 1/2 tsp Tomato Paste
1 TBS Melted Butter
1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
1/4 tsp Paprika
1/4 Cup Water

Instructions

MAYO:

Add Oil, egg, mustard, vinegar, salt, cayenne, sugar into immersion blender container and blend for 1 minute from the bottom up.
Refrigerate for at least 30 min for the mayo to "set up"
Whisk in the remaining ingredients (except the water) until smooth, add water as necessary to achieve the desired consistency.
Refrigerate overnight to allow the flavors to blend.
Bring to room temperature before serving.
