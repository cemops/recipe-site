Turkey Chili


Ingredients:

Turkey:

2 lb Ground Turkey

1 1/2 tsp Cumin

1 1/2 tsp Coriander

1 1/2 tsp Worcestershire Sauce

Salt & Pepper

Chili:

1 Green Pepper, Chopped

1 Large Onion, Chopped

2 Celery Stalks, Chopped

2 TBS Worcestershire Sauce

2 TBS Franks or Tabasco Sauce (wait until done and add then)

2 TBS Brown Sugar

1 Large Can Diced Tomatoes

1 Large Can Chili Hot Beans (or mild)

1/2 - 1 Bottle of Killian's or other dark beer

2 Cap Fulls of Bourbon

1 Small Can Stewed Tomatoes (Last time I added "Mexican Style")

Instructions:

- Sauté turkey with Worcestershire, cumin, coriander, salt and pepper until cooked through.

- In a large pot, add EVOO and butter and sauté green pepper, onion, celery.

- Add remaining ingredients, bring to a boil, reduce heat to low and simmer for at least an hour.
