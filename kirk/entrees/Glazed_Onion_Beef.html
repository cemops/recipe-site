Glazed Onion - Sauteed Beef (Blue Apron)

Makes: 2 Servings

Prep Time: 10 Min | Cook Time: 30 Min


Ingredients:

10 oz thinly sliced beef

1/2 Yellow Onion - thinly sliced

2 Tbs Tomato Paste

1 Tbs White Wine Vinegar

1/2 Bunch Thyme

1 Bunch Chives

Directions:

Caramelize the Onions: In a large pan, heat 2 tsp of olive oil on medium until hot. Add the sliced onion. Cook, stirring occasionally, 8 - 10 min. or until lightly browned. Add 1 Tbs of water, cook stirring frequently, 1 to 2 min, or until very tender. Add the tomato paste and thyme; cook, stirring frequently, 1 to 2 min, or until dark red. Add the vinegar and 1/4 cup of water. Cook, stirring frequently, 30 seconds to 1 min, or until well combined. Transfer to a bowl.

Cook the Beef: Using your hands, separate the beef; pat dry with paper towels. Transfer to a bowl; season with salt and pepper. Toss to coat. In a large pan, heat 1 tsp of olive oil on high until hot. Add the beef in a single layer, cook without stirring, 1 - 2 min. Add the caramelized onions, cook, stirring constantly, 30 seconds to 1 minute. Garnish with chives.
