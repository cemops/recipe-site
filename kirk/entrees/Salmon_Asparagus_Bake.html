Healthy Salmon & Asparagus Bake


Ingredients:

Salmon Filets

Bunch of Asparagus

Some thawed peas

Minced Garlic

Minced Parsley

Seafood Magic

Lemon Juice

Lemon Slices

Instructions:

- Preheat oven to 400 degrees

- Then change to high broiler

- Rinse and pat dry salmon filets

- Place on a cooking sheet (lined with foil)

- Rub garlic then parsley on filets

- Season with Seafood Magic

- Sprinkle with lemon juice

- Spray with olive oil spray

- Season Asparagus with olive oil and veggie magic

- Spread asparagus and peas in a single layer around the salmon

- Place lemon slices over salmon and asparagus

- Place in oven for approx. 8 - 10 min

** Could also add red potatoes cut smaller **
