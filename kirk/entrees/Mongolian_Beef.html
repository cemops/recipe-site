Mongolian Beef

Sweet, savory, and spicy beef that's perfect over rice or noodles.


Ingredients:

1 Tbs dark sesame oil
2 tsp minced garlic
4 green onions, cut into 1-inch pieces
1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced
1/4 cup water
2 Tbs low sodium soy sauce
4 tsp corn starch
2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
2 tsp brown sugar
Toasted sesame seeds (optional)

Directions:

Heat oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat.
Add garlic and green onions; stir frequently for 1 minute.
Add beef and cook 5 minutes or until browned, stirring frequently.
In a small bowl, combine water, soy sauce, corn starch, red pepper flakes, and brown sugar.
Pour sauce over beef and cook 1–2 minutes until thickened and beef is cooked through.
Garnish with sesame seeds if desired.
