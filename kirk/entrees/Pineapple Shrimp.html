Pineapple Shrimp


Ingredients

For the Sauce:

1/2 Cup Pineapple Juice
1/2 Cup Chicken Broth
1/4 Cup Soy Sauce
2 TBS Siracha
2 TBS Light Brown Sugar
2 TBS Rice Wine Vinegar
1 TBS Minced Ginger
2 tsp Cornstarch

For the Shrimp:

1 lb Medium Shrimp, Peeled, Deveined, and Patted Dry
Kosher Salt
Freshly Ground Pepper
4 tsp EVOO
1 Red Bell Pepper, Chopped
2 Cups Fresh Pineapple Cubes

Instructions

In a saucepan, combine the pineapple juice, chicken broth, soy sauce, Sriracha, brown sugar, vinegar, ginger, and cornstarch over medium heat. Let simmer until thickened and reduced by a third, about 7 min.
Season the shrimp with salt and pepper. In a large skillet, heat 3 tsp olive oil over medium heat. Add the shrimp and cook, flipping once, until pink, 2 min total. Transfer to a plate.
Add the teaspoon of oil to the same pan. Add the bell pepper and cook until just cooked, about 3 min. Add the pineapple and the sauce and turn the heat up to high. Once the mixture is simmering, add the shrimp and stir to combine. Serve right away with green onions on top.
