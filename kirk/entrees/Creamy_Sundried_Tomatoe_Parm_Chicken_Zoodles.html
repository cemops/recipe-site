Creamy Sundried Tomato + Parm Chicken Zoodles


Ingredients:

1 TBS Butter (w/ Olive Oil)

1 1/2 lb Boneless Skinless Chicken Thigh Filets - cut into strips

7 oz jarred Sun Dried Tomatoes in oil - chopped

4 Cloves Garlic - peeled and smashed

1 1/4 Cup Heavy Cream (or Half and Half)

1 Cup Shaved Parmesan Cheese

Salt to taste

Dried Basil

Red Chilli Flakes

Zoodles or 2 Large Zucchini made into zoodles or sliced into "linguine"

Instructions:

- Heat the butter over medium-high heat. Add the chicken strips and sprinkle with salt. Pan fry until chicken is golden brown and cooked through.

- Add the sun-dried tomatoes with 1 TBS of the oil from the jar, and the garlic. Saute until fragrant. (Add some red cooking wine at the end to deglaze the pan.)

- Lower the heat, add the cream and Parmesan cheese, simmer while stirring until the cheese is melted. Sprinkle with salt, basil, and red chilli flakes.

- Stir through the zoodles and continue to simmer until the zoodles have softened (about 5 - 8 minutes).
