Monkfish with Curried Mussels

Serves 2–4


Ingredients

1 large monkfish tail, filleted in two
1/2 lb mussels, cleaned and beard removed
2 sprigs thyme
2 bay leaves
1/2 cup dry white wine (or as needed)
1 carrot, peeled and sliced
1 small leek, sliced
1 small celery stalk, sliced
1 clove garlic, minced
1 tsp curry powder
2 pinches turmeric powder
1 tbsp curry powder + 1 tbsp salt (for dusting fish)
1/2 cup heavy whipping cream
Handful of cilantro, chopped
Butter
Olive oil
*Ask the fishmonger to remove gray membrane*

Instructions

Clean monkfish fillets and cut each in half. Dust with curry/salt mix and set aside 5 minutes.
Heat a wide pan until hot; add thyme, bay leaves, and mussels.
Add wine, cover immediately, and cook ~30 seconds until mussels open.
Strain off & reserve juices; remove mussels from shells and set aside.
Sauté carrot, leek, celery, garlic with 1 tsp curry powder, turmeric, and thyme until soft.
Strain in mussel juices; reduce.
Pat monkfish dry and pan-fry in hot olive oil until golden (3–4 minutes).
Add mussels to vegetables; warm through.
Add cream and bring to a boil; mix in cilantro.
Divide mussel mixture into warm bowls; slice monkfish thickly and place on top.
