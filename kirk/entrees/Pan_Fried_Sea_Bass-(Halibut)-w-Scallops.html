Panfried Sea Bass (Halibut) and Scallops with Sauce Beurre Blanc

Prep: 15 min | Cook: 25 min | Servings: 2


Ingredients

Sauce

1 TBS very finely chopped shallot
2 TBS white wine vinegar
2 TBS dry white wine
2 TBS water
1/4 lb (1 stick) cold unsalted butter, diced
Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Fish & Scallops

2 Sea Bass fillets, scaled, rinsed, and drained (or halibut)
Salt and freshly ground black pepper
2 TBS butter
1 TBS olive oil
6 scallops

Instructions

To make the sauce, place shallot, vinegar, wine, and water into a small heavy-based pan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer until ~1 TBS of syrupy liquid remains.
Reduce heat to lowest setting and whisk in butter one piece at a time. The sauce should be pale and custard-like. Season with salt and pepper.
Meanwhile, season fish and scallops with salt and pepper.
Heat half of the remaining butter and oil in a heavy-based frying pan. Add fish skin-side down. Press with spatula to prevent curling. Cook 2–3 min until skin is crispy and flesh turns opaque, leaving a thin pink strip in center.
Carefully turn fish and cook 1–2 more minutes until done.
In another small frying pan, heat remaining butter and oil. Add scallops and cook 4–5 min, turning once.
Spoon sauce beurre blanc onto warmed plates, arrange sugar peas, set fish fillet on top, garnish with scallops, and serve immediately.

Tips for Perfect Sauce Beurre Blanc

  • Use cold butter straight from the fridge and cut into small cubes for smooth emulsification.
  • Whisk constantly and add butter gradually to prevent the sauce from separating.
  • Keep the heat very low once you start adding butter; too much heat will cause the sauce to split.
  • If the sauce begins to separate, remove from heat and whisk in a tablespoon of cold water to bring it back together.
  • Prepare the sauce just before serving; beurre blanc does not reheat well.
  • For extra flavor, add a teaspoon of fresh lemon juice at the end to brighten the sauce.
