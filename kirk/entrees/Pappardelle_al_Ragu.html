Pappardelle al Ragu

Servings: 4–6 | Prep: 20 min | Cook: 2 hours


Ingredients

2 Tbs Butter
2 Tbs Olive Oil
4 oz Pancetta, diced
1 small onion, finely chopped
1 celery stalk, finely chopped
1 carrot, finely chopped
2 garlic cloves, crushed
1 lb lean ground beef
1/2 cup beef stock (plus more if needed)
2 Tbs Tomato paste
14 oz can diced tomatoes (fire roasted optional)
Salt & Pepper, to taste
1/3 cup whole milk, warmed
1 lb package of pappardelle (wide noodles)
Parmesan cheese, grated to serve

Instructions

Melt butter with oil in a deep, heavy-bottomed saucepan. Cook pancetta 1–2 min.
Add onion, celery, carrot, and garlic. Cook, stirring occasionally, 10 min until softened but not browned.
Optional: Cook meat separately to drain excess fat, then stir in and cook until evenly browned (~10 min).
Add stock, tomato paste, and diced tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper. Bring to a boil.
Reduce heat to very low. Cover and simmer gently 1.5 hours, stirring occasionally, adding more stock if necessary.
Stir in warmed milk, cover, and simmer another 30 min.
Cook pappardelle according to package directions. Serve with sauce and grated Parmesan.

Tips for Perfect Ragu

  • Cooking the meat separately can help reduce excess grease for a cleaner sauce.
  • Maintain very low heat during simmering to prevent sauce from scorching.
  • Stir occasionally to keep the ragu smooth and prevent sticking.
  • Use whole milk for a richer finish; add gradually to avoid curdling.
  • Fire-roasted diced tomatoes add extra depth of flavor.
  • Resting the sauce for a few minutes before serving helps flavors meld beautifully.
