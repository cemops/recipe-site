Italian Deviled Chicken


Ingredients:

4 Skinless, boneless chicken breasts cut into 1-inch cubes

1/2 cup olive oil

Juice and finely grated rind of 1 lemon

2 garlic cloves, finely chopped

2 tsp finely chopped dried red chiles

Salt & pepper

Fresh flat-leaf parsley sprigs to garnish

Instructions:

- Place the chicken in a large, shallow, nonmetallic dish (or gallon zip lock bag).

- Place the oil, lemon juice and rind, garlic and chiles in a measuring cup and stir until well blended. Season to taste with salt and pepper.

- Pour the mixture over the chicken and stir gently to coat.

- Cover with plastic wrap and let marinate in the fridge for up to 8 hours.

- Preheat the grill. Drain the chicken, reserving the marinade.

- Thread the chicken onto 8 metal or presoaked wooden skewers and cook over medium-high heat, turning and brushing frequently with the reserved marinade, for 6 - 10 min.

- Transfer to a large serving dish, garnish with parsley sprigs, serve immediately.
