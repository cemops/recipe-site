Smoked Pork Loin w/ Sauerkraut and Apples


Ingredients

1 - 2 to 2 1/2 lbs Pork Loin Roast
1 Lb Bag Sauerkraut (last time I used a 2 LB bag)
2 Cooking Apples, Peeled, Cored, and Sliced (last time I used Golden Delicious Apples)
1 Sweet Onion, Thinly Sliced
1 Cup Dark Beer (last time I used Big Voodoo Daddy Dark Beer)
2 TBS Butter, melted
2 Bay Leaves
1/3 Cup Brown Sugar (last time I used a little more and was still not enough)
Paul Predhomes Pork Magic

Instructions

The night before - trim the pork and season with Pork Magic. Cover and refrigerate overnight. Remove in the morning to bring pork to room temp.
Pre - heat the smoker, with dry chips / chunks at 225 degrees for 15 min.
Place the roast directly on the grill grates and smoke for 1 hour.
Pre - heat the oven to 350 degrees.
In the large cast iron skillet; layer the sauerkraut, apples, onions, brown sugar, beer, butter, and bay leaves. (Try placing in oven 15 min before pork is to be moved to skillet)
Lay the smoked pork loin directly on top of the sauerkraut mixture and cover.
Roast the pork for an additional hour or until the internal temp of the pork reaches 160 degress.
Transfer pork to the cutting board and let rest for 10 min. Gently stir the sauerkraut mixture and arrange on a serving platter. Slice the pork and layer on top of the sauerkraut mixture.
ENJOY!!!!!
