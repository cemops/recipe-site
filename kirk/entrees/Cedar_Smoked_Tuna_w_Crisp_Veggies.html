Cedar Smoked Tuna / Salmon with Crisp Vegetables


Ingredients:

2 Tbs balsamic vinegar

2 Tbs fresh lemon juice

1 Tbs whole grain mustard

kosher salt

ground black pepper

EVOO

1 pound asparagus

Radishes

4 tuna (salmon) steaks, each about 8 ounces and 1 inch thick

1/2 tsp paprika

4 scallions (white and light green parts only) thinly sliced

2 cups tightly packed baby arugula

Instructions:

- In a small bowl whisk the vinegar, lemon juice, mustard, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1/2 tsp pepper. Then slowly whisk in 1/4 cup oil until the dressing is emulsified. Set aside.

- ** Jaque Pepin says to use a vegetable peeler to peel the lower half of the asparagus instead of breaking them saving more asparagus **

- Lightly coat the asparagus with oil and season evenly with salt and pepper.

- Brush the tuna / salmon steaks on both sides with oil and season evenly with 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper and the paprika.

- Place the soaked plank over direct medium heat and close the lid. After the plank starts to smoke and char (5 - 10 min) turn the plank over. Close the lid and cook until the fish is firm but still juicy, about 10 min.

- Grill the asparagus over direct medium heat, with the lid closed as much as possible, until crisp tender, 6 to 8 min, turning once. Remove and cut into 1 inch pieces.

- In a large bowl combine the asparagus with the scallions, radishes, and arugula. Drizzle with the dressing and toss to coat. Put the vegetables on the serving platter with the fish and serve right away.
