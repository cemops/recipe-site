Garden Vegetable Grilled Pizza


Ingredients:

1 1/2 cups fresh baby spinach

1 tsp olive oil

1 recipe Homemade pizza crust OR Boboli bread

6 Tbs grated Parmesan cheese

6 cloves garlic, thinly sliced

1 1/2 cups chunky tomato sauce

12 oz bite-size fresh mozzarella cheese balls, drained, thinly sliced, and patted dry

1 1/2 cups grilled corn kernels

1 1/2 cups grilled eggplant

6 slices of bacon, crisp cooked, crumbled

1 1/2 lb assorted heirloom tomatoes, cut up

small fresh basil leaves

Ground Pepper

Sea Salt

Directions:

1. In a small bowl combine spinach, 1 tsp oil and a dash of salt.

2. Using Boboli crust, brush with olive oil and grill on both sides for 2 min.

3. Sprinkle 1 Tbs of Parmesan cheese and one clove garlic over crust; top with 1/4 cup of tomato sauce. Add 1/4 cup of spinach mixture, 2 oz of the mozzarella cheese, 1/4 cup of corn, 1/4 cup of eggplant and 1 slice of the crumbled bacon. Grill pizza 3 to 5 min more or until crust is crisp and cheese is melted.

4. Before serving, top pizzas with tomato, basil, and pepper (Optional sea salt).
