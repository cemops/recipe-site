Tilapia with Jasmine Rice


Ingredients:

Tilapia fillet

Fat-free Italian salad dressing (I use Wishbone)

1/4 teaspoon salt

1/4 teaspoon black pepper

1/4 teaspoon Old Bay Seasoning

1/4 teaspoon cumin

2 1/2 cups water

1 tablespoon margarine or butter

1 cup jasmine rice (uncooked)

Instructions:

- Pour your dressing straight from the bottle into the pan, ensuring the bottom is covered but not too much.

- Place the fillets on top of the dressing in the pan.

- Sprinkle with all 4 seasonings. Don't leave out the cumin; it is a wonderful addition to the fish.

- Saute the fillets on medium-low heat for approximately 4-5 minutes each side until fish flakes easily with a fork.

- While the fish is slowly cooking, cook the rice using 2 1/2 cups water and 1 cup rice, with butter. Bring to a boil and simmer for 15 minutes on low.

- Serve the fillet on top of the Jasmine rice. Add more salt, pepper, and cumin according to taste.
