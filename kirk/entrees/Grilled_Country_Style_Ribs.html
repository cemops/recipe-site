Grilled Country Style Ribs


Ingredients:

3 lbs country style pork ribs

1/4 cup olive oil

3 cloves garlic, minced

2 Tbsp brown sugar

1 Tbsp chili powder

1 tsp salt

1/2 tsp pepper

Instructions:

Combine olive oil, garlic, brown sugar, chili powder, salt and pepper to make a marinade.

Place ribs in a shallow dish, pour marinade over top and turn to coat. Cover and marinate 1–2 hours.

Preheat gas grill to 400°F. Clean and oil the grates.

Sear ribs over direct heat 4–5 minutes per side.

Move ribs to indirect heat, close lid and cook until internal temperature reaches 195°F.

Remove from grill, tent with foil, and rest for 5 minutes before serving.
