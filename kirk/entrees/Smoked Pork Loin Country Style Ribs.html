Smoked Pork Loin Country Style Ribs


Ingredients

Pork Loin Country Style Ribs
Pork Magic
BBQ Sauce

Instructions

** Can be done 2 ways **
Smoke them for the entire 4 - 5 hours
Smoke them for 2 hours then reverse sear them on the grill w/ BBQ Sauce
Baste the ribs with Olive Oil and Pork Magic. At least 2 hours but preferably overnight.
Prepare the smoker with Hickory wood chips, water and apple juice
Preheat the smoker to 225 degrees.
Smoke for 2 hours and/or internal temp reaches 165 degrees
Remove from smoker, baste with BBQ sauce and place over direct, medium high heat
Turn after 3 - 5 min, basting agian
Remove and let rest for 10 min.
