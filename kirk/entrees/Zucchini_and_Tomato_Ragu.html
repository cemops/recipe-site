Zucchini and Tomato Ragu


Ingredients:

6 TBS EVOO

1 Onion, Roughly Chopped

2 Garlic Cloves, Lightly Crushed

1 Medium Zucchini, Sliced

1 Medium Squash, Sliced

1 Tomato, Chopped (1 Cup)

1/2 Cup Mozz Cheese, Torn or Small Balls

1/4 Cup Parsley, Roughly Chopped

1/4 Cup Basil Leaves, Torn

Kosher Salt, Pepper

Instructions:

- Heat the oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and cook, stirring often, until the onion is translucent, 8 - 10 min.

- Add the zucchini and squash, season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently, until the zucchini and squash turn golden brown, about 2 min.

- Stir in the tomatoes and cook until they have just begun to soften and the zucchini and squash are al dente, about 2 min. Serve topped with the Mozz, Parsley and Basil.
