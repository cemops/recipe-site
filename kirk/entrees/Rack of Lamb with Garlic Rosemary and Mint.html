Rack of Lamb with Garlic Rosemary and Mint


Ingredients

1 - 8-bone rack of lamb, French cut
2 Tbsp of minced garlic
2 Tbsp of dried rosemary
Ground pepper
1 small bottle of mint sauce

Instructions

Cut the 8 bone rack into two 4 bone racks
Place on plate and put 1 Tbsp of both the garlic and rosemary on each rack.
Sprinkle with ground pepper
Pour 1/2 the bottle of mint sauce over each rack and let sit for 2 hours
Preheat BBQ to 375 and place both racks bone side down for 30 min. for medium
Remove from grill and let stand for 8 - 10 min
