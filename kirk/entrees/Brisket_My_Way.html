Brisket - My Way


Ingredients:

Brisket Rub:

1/4 Cup Kosher Salt

1/4 Cup Sugar

1/4 Cup Black Pepper

3/4 Cup Paprika

2 Tbs Garlic Powder

2 Tbs Garlic Salt

2 Tbs Onion Powder

2 Tbs Chili Powder

2 tsp Ground Red Pepper

Brisket Mopping Sauce:

1 (12 oz) Bottle Beer

1 Cup Apple Cider Vinegar

1 Onion, Minced

4 Garlic Cloves, Minced

1/2 Cup Water

1/2 Cup Worcestershire Sauce

1/4 Cup Vegetable Oil

2 Tbs Brisket Rub

Brisket Hot Sauce:

1 1/2 Cups Cider Vinegar

1 Cup Ketchup

1/2 tsp Ground Red Pepper

1/4 Cup Worcestershire Sauce

1 tsp Salt

1/2 tsp Black Pepper

1/2 tsp Onion Powder

1/2 Tbs Garlic Powder

1/2 Tbs Ground Cumin

2 Tbs Unsalted Butter, Melted

1/2 Cup Firmly Packed Brown Sugar

Instructions:

- Sprinkle each side of beef with 1/4 cup Brisket Rub. Rub thoroughly into meat. Wrap brisket in plastic wrap and chill for 8 hours.

- Add water / apple juice to water container. Add dry wood chunks to smoker diffuser.

- Spray grates with grilling spray. Prepare smoker and preheat grill to 225°F.

- Place brisket on grates. Smoke for 45 minutes. Mop the top of brisket, and check water and wood every 45 minutes until internal temperature reaches 170°F.

- Wrap brisket in heavy-duty foil. Mop liberally with mopping sauce. Wrap tightly and return to grill.

- Remove brisket when internal temperature reaches 190°F. Let stand for 1 hour before serving.
