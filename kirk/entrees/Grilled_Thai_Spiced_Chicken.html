Grilled Thai-Spiced Chicken

Prep Time: 5 Minutes | Cook Time: 10 Minutes


Ingredients:

6 (3 oz) skinless, boneless chicken thighs

1/2 tsp salt

1/4 tsp black pepper

4 tsp red curry paste

Cooking Spray

1 Tbs chopped fresh cilantro

Directions:

- Prepare Grill

- Sprinkle chicken with salt and pepper, spread curry paste evenly over chicken

- Place chicken on grill rack coated with cooking spray; cover and grill 5 minutes on each side or until chicken is done.

- Sprinkle with cilantro
