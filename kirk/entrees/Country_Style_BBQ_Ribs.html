Country Style Barbecue Ribs


Ingredients:

4 lb bone in country style pork ribs

2 tsp salt, divided

1 medium onion, chopped

1 Cup brown sugar

1 Cup apple butter

1 Cup Ketchup

1/2 cup lemon juice

1/2 cup orange juice

1 Tbs A.1 Steak sauce

1 tsp coarse ground pepper

1 tsp minced garlic

1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce

Instructions:

- Cut ribs apart, if necessary, and trim; sprinkle 1 tsp salt over ribs, set aside.

- Stir together remaining 1 tsp salt, chopped onion, and remaining ingredients until blended. Pour half of the mixture into a 5-qt slow cooker (or use a dutch oven on the grill at 200 degrees for 5 hours, ensuring a nice simmer).

- Place ribs in slow cooker; pour remaining mixture over ribs.

- Cover and cook on HIGH for 6-7 hours.
