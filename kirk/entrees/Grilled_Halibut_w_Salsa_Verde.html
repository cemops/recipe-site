Grilled Halibut with Salsa Verde


Ingredients:

Halibut:

4 Halibut Steaks

2 - 3 Tbsp Olive Oil

Juice of 1/2 lemon

1/4 tsp Sea Salt

1/4 tsp Pepper

Salsa Verde:

2 Anchovy fillets, rinsed and drained

1/2 Cup Breadcrumbs

2/3 Cup fresh flat leaf (Italian) parsley

2/3 Cup fresh basil

2 Tbsp capers, rinsed

1 garlic clove, crushed

2 Tbsp lemon juice

1/2 Cup olive oil

Salt and Pepper

Instructions:

1. Combine oil, lemon juice, sea salt, and pepper in a shallow dish. Add halibut and turn to coat. Cover with plastic and marinate in the refrigerator for 1 hour, turning halfway through.

2. Combine all salsa ingredients in a food processor and briefly pulse 4 - 5 times to a rough paste. Pour into a bowl and set aside.

3. Grill the halibut in fish baskets over medium-high heat for 4 - 6 min each side, brushing with reserved marinade.

4. Serve on warmed plates with salsa verde on the side and lemon wedges.
