Slow Cooker Chicken Marsala

Serves 4


Ingredients

4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
1 cup sliced cremini mushrooms
3/4 cup Marsala wine
1/2 cup low-sodium chicken broth
2 tbsp olive oil
1 small onion, finely chopped
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp water (slurry)
1 tsp dried thyme (or fresh sprigs)
Salt & pepper to taste
Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

Instructions

Season chicken with salt and pepper.
In a skillet over medium heat, sear chicken in olive oil until lightly golden, 2–3 minutes per side.
Place chicken in slow cooker. Top with mushrooms, onion, garlic, and thyme.
Pour Marsala wine and chicken broth over the chicken.
Cover and cook on LOW for 5–6 hours or HIGH for 2.5–3 hours.
Remove chicken. Stir cornstarch slurry into sauce and cook on HIGH for 10–15 minutes to thicken.
Return chicken to slow cooker, coat with sauce, garnish with parsley, and serve warm.
