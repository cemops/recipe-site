Balsamic-Glazed Onion Cheeseburgers

Makes: 2 Servings

Prep Time: 15 min | Cook Time: 30 - 40 min


Ingredients:

10 oz Ground Beef (or 1 lb if cooking from home)

2 Potato Buns

2 Cloves Garlic

1 Yellow Onion

1 Russet Potato

2 oz Monterey Jack Cheese

2 Tbs Balsamic vinegar

1 tsp Whole Dried Rosemary

Directions:

Prepare The Ingredients: Preheat the oven to 475°F or light the grill. Wash and dry the produce, halve the buns. Cut potato into 1" wedges. Small dice the onion. Smash the garlic into a paste. Thinly slice the cheese.

Roast the Potato Wedges: Olive oil, salt, and pepper the potatoes. Inside: place skin side down on sheet and roast 30 to 32 min. or until browned. Outside: start right before putting Jelayne's burger on for 4 min per side.

Cook and Glaze the Onion: In a small pot, heat a drizzle of olive oil on medium-high heat. Add the onion, salt, and pepper. Cover, cook for 5 - 7 min, stirring occasionally. Add vinegar, cook, uncovered, stirring occasionally, for 2 - 3 min, or until most of the liquid has cooked off. Remove from heat and season with salt and pepper, set aside in a warm place.

Form the Patties: In a medium bowl combine the ground beef, garlic paste, and as much of the rosemary as you want, season with salt and pepper. Gently mix to combine and form into (2 patties from Blue Apron OR 4 patties from home).

Cook the Patties: Inside: Cook the patties in a pan and loosely cover with foil for 2 - 4 min. Flip, cover with cheese, cover with foil for another 2 - 4 min. Outside: Dude, you know what you are doing with a burger.....

Toast the Buns and Plate the Dish: Toast the buns (in the pan if inside | on the grill if outside). Place the onions on the bottom bun. Then place the meat with the cheese. Then the top bun (optional).
