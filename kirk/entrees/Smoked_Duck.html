Smoked Duck


Ingredients:

1 5 - 6 Lb duck (purchased 3 days before to thaw in refrigerator)

Apple and/or Cherry Wood Chips

1 Orange and/or mandarin oranges

1 Onion

Glaze:

1 Tbs Honey

1 Tbs Molasses

2 Tbs Orange Juice

1 Tbs Balsamic Vinegar

Zest of 1 Orange

Pinch Salt and Pepper

Dry Brine:

2 Tbs Honey

2 Tbs Soy Sauce

Salt and Pepper

Instructions:

- Thaw the duck in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

Day Before:

- Trim the skin around the neck leaving a 1" flap, and remove the tips of the wings.

- Remove the giblets and pat the duck dry with paper towels.

- Slash the skin all the way through the fat layer without going through to the meat, in about 1" intervals to help the fat render out.

- Brush with dry brine and season with salt and pepper on all sides.

- Stuff the duck with quartered orange and onion.

- Place on a metal cooling grate over a baking sheet and refrigerate overnight, uncovered.

Cook Day:

- Prepare smoker to cook at 300 - 325 degrees.

- Glaze the duck, liberally.

- Set High alarm to 157 degrees on the meat channel.

- Set high alarm to 330 and low alarm to 295 on smoker channel.

- Smoke for 2 1/2 hours.

- Remove and rest duck for 20 min before slicing.

- If the duck starts to get too dark, cover with foil.
