Roasted Pork Tenderloin w/ Sauerkraut


Ingredients

Pork Tenderloin
Sauerkraut
Pork Magic
Olive Oil

Instructions

Day Before:
   Trim Tenderloin
   Cover w/ Pork Magic
   Place on a grate and refrigerate overnight
Day Of:
   4 hours prior to dinner: set out tenderloin to obtain room temp
   3 hours prior to dinner: pre heat oven to 350 degrees
   2.5 hours prior to dinner: sear tenderloin in cast iron skillet on all four sides, wrap in foil and set aside.
   De-glaze the skillet with white wine
   2 hours prior to dinner: place sauerkraut in skillet, sprinkle with brown sugar, cover, and cook
   30 min prior to dinner: place 'smoke' thermometer in pork, add to skillet and place back in the oven until internal temp reaches 145 degrees
