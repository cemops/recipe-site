Smoked Meatballs

Serves 6–8


Ingredients

2 lb ground beef (or 1 lb beef + 1 lb ground pork)
2 large eggs, whisked
1 medium red onion, diced small
1 carrot, diced small
1 red bell pepper, diced small
1 tbsp garlic, minced
1.5 tsp Italian seasoning, dried
½ tsp black pepper
1 tsp salt
24 oz pasta sauce (your choice)

Instructions

Preheat smoker to 225°F.
In a large mixing bowl, combine ground meat, eggs, onion, carrot, red pepper, garlic, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Mix thoroughly.
Form meatballs (~3” each) using a ¼ cup scoop or large spoon. Place on a rimmed sheet pan or cast iron skillet, leaving space between each.
Smoke meatballs immediately or cover and refrigerate for up to 1 day before smoking.
Place meatballs in the smoker on a rack. Smoke at 225°F for 1 hour.
Preheat smoker to 325°F. Pour half of the pasta sauce into a large baking dish, place meatballs on top, then pour remaining sauce over them.
Continue smoking meatballs in pasta sauce for about 45 minutes, until internal temp reaches 160°F.
Serve when cool enough to enjoy. Perfect for sandwiches or pasta dishes.
