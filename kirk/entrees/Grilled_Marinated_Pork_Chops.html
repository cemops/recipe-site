Grilled Pork Chops


Ingredients:

6 bone-in pork loin chops (about 1-inch thick)
1/2 cup reduced sodium soy sauce
1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
2 tablespoons olive oil
2 tablespoons light brown sugar
2 teaspoons smoked paprika
1 teaspoon garlic powder
1 teaspoon dried thyme
Salt, for seasoning
Ground black pepper, for seasoning
Fresh thyme, for garnish

Directions:

Place the pork chops in a 13x9-inch baking dish. Set aside.
In a bowl, mix soy sauce, vinegar, olive oil, brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, and thyme.
Pour marinade over pork chops. Cover and refrigerate 1 hour to overnight, flipping halfway through.
Remove chops from fridge 30 min before grilling. Preheat grill to 400–450°F and oil grates.
Grill uncovered 5–7 min per side or until internal temp reaches 145°F. Discard leftover marinade.
Remove chops, tent with foil, and rest 10 minutes.
Season with salt and pepper before serving. Garnish with fresh thyme if desired.
