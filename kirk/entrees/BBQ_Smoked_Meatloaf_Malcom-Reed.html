BBQ Smoked Meatloaf - Malcom Reed


Ingredients:

2 lb ground beef

1 lb ground pork

1 Celery Rib

1 Red Pepper

4 Cloves Garlic

4 Green Onion, whites and light green only

8 oz Bread Crumbs

2 Eggs w/ 1/4 Cup Half and Half

Black Pepper

Kosher Salt

BBQ Rub

1 Tbs Worcestershire

Instructions:

- Combine ingredients in a large bowl.

- Line 2 foil loaf pans with Saran Wrap and fill each with meat mixture. Let set for at least 30 min.

- Remove to smoker grate, remove plastic wrap and smoke for 2 - 3 hours at 275 degrees until internal temp reaches 155 degrees.

- Baste with sweet BBQ Sauce and let cook for an additional 15 min and internal temp reaches 160 degrees.

- Remove and let rest for 15 min.
