Baked Chicken Ricotta Meatballs with Spinach

Serve with pasta, crusty bread, salad, or roasted veggies for a complete meal.


Ingredients:

1 lb ground chicken
1 cup Ricotta cheese
1 cup fresh spinach, finely chopped
½ cup bread crumbs or panko
¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
1 egg
2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tsp dried oregano
1 tsp salt
½ tsp black pepper
Italian seasoning (to taste)
¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
1 jar marinara sauce (about 24 oz)
Olive oil for drizzling

Directions:

Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
In a large bowl, mix ground chicken, ricotta, spinach, breadcrumbs, parmesan, egg, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper, and parsley.
Form mixture into 1.5" meatballs and place on parchment-lined baking sheet.
Drizzle with olive oil and bake 20–25 minutes, until golden and cooked through.
Broil for an additional 2–3 minutes for browning.
Meanwhile, heat marinara sauce in a saucepan over medium heat.
Serve meatballs warm with marinara, garnished with extra parsley and parmesan if desired.





Notes:

These meatballs pair wonderfully with pasta, crusty bread, or a fresh salad. Last time, I served them with Oven Roasted Zucchini and Squash for a light and flavorful side.
