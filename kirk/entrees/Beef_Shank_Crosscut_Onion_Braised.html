Beef Shank Cross Cut Onion Braised


Ingredients:

Red Onion

Yellow Onion

White Onion

Carrot

Celery

Garlic

Rosemary

Bay leaf

Thyme bundle (bouquet garni)

2 cups beef broth

Instructions:

Brown the Beef Shanks

- Salt and Pepper the beef shanks on both sides.

- Brown the beef shanks in vegetable oil on high heat.

- Remove them to the Dutch Oven.

- Add the Garlic, Celery, Carrots, Rosemary, Bay Leaf, Bouquet Garni, Black Pepper to beef.

Sauté the Onions

- Add the onions to the skillet drippings.

- Add large pinch of salt.

- Simmer for approximately 10 min until soft.

- Add 2 cups of beef broth.

- Stir and deglaze the skillet.

Combine and Simmer

- Pour the onions over the beef in the Dutch Oven.

- Cover and cook over VERY low heat for 3 – 4 hours.

Boil Cauliflower

- Boil cauliflower.

- Process until smooth.

Reduce Beef

- Remove beef bones (with marrow intact).

- Increase heat to Medium – High.

- Skim off any fat that pools up to the top.

- Reduce down for about 10 min.

Serve

- Serve warm topping each serving with feta cheese.
