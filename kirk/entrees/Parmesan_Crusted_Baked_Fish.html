Parmesan Crusted Baked Fish


Ingredients:

3/4 Cup Panko Breadcrumbs

3/4 Cup Parmesan Cheese, Finely Grated

3 TBS Unsalted Butter, Room Temp

3 TBS Mayonnaise

3 Green Onions, Green Tops Only, Thinly Sliced

1 tsp Worcestershire Sauce

1/2 tsp Hot Pepper Sauce

1 1/2 TBS Lemon Juice Freshly Squeezed (about 1/2 lemon)

Salt and Pepper to Taste

4 White Fish Filets: About 6 oz each

2 TBS Fresh Parsley, Chopped

Instructions:

- Preheat oven to 425 degrees. Lightly butter or spray baking dish for the filets.

- In a medium bowl, mix together the cheese, breadcrumbs, butter, mayo, green onions, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce, and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper.

- Pat the fish fillets completely dry with paper towels, season generously with salt and pepper. Arrange in baking dish.

- Spread 3 TBS of the cheese mixture over each fillet.

- Place in preheated oven and bake until bubbly and almost cooked through, about 8 - 10 min.

- Move fillets to broiler for 2 - 3 min to brown and crisp the tops.

- Remove from oven, garnish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.

Notes:

Butter mixture can be made 24 hours ahead and kept refrigerated.

Butter Mixture can be cut in half for 2 filets.
