Cedar Planked Halibut with Charred Tomato Avocado Salsa


Ingredients:

Halibut:

4 Halibut Steaks or Filets about 1 1/2 inches thick

Marinade / Glaze:

4 TBS Honey

4 TBS Balsamic Vinegar

1/2 tsp Black Pepper

1/2 tsp Sea Salt

Charred Tomato Avocado Salsa:

2 Large Beefsteak Tomatoes

1 Large Avocado

Zest and Juice of 1 Small Lime

Salt and Pepper to Taste

1/2 Small Red Onion, Finely Chopped

1 - 2 Cloves Minced Garlic

1/2 Finely Minced Jalapeno Pepper

Instructions:

For the Halibut:

- Soak a cedar plank in water for 2 - 4 hours.

- Wash the fish and pat it dry with paper towels.

- Stir together the honey, balsamic vinegar, salt and pepper and allow the fish to marinate for a min of 20 min, turning it occasionally.

- Place the soaked cedar plank on a medium hot grill and close the lid until it begins to smoke.

- Brush a little canola oil on top of the plank and place the marinated fish on it before closing the lid for about 20 min.

- Adjust the heat to keep the internal temp about 375-400 degrees F. Keep a water bottle on hand in case of any flare ups.

- After 20 min. brush the remaining marinade on a couple of times at five min. intervals until the fish is fully cooked.

To Make The Salsa:

- Cut the tomatoes into quarters and scoop the center flesh from them.

- Char the outside of the tomatoes for only a min. or two on a very hot grill. You don't want to cook the tomatoes through too much. As soon as they have grill marks, take them off the grill.

- Toss all ingredients together well and cover with plastic wrap until ready to serve.
