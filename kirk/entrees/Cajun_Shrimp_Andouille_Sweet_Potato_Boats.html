Spicy Cajun Shrimp & Andouille Sweet Potato Boats

Meal Prep Version – 4 Lunches (2 stuffed sweet potatoes, 4 servings)


Ingredients

2 medium sweet potatoes
½ lb raw shrimp, peeled, deveined, chopped
3 oz andouille sausage, diced
1–2 cloves garlic, minced
1 tbsp olive oil
½ tbsp butter
1 tsp Cajun seasoning
Pinch–⅛ tsp cayenne pepper
1½ tbsp whipped cream cheese
1–1½ tbsp whole milk Greek yogurt
Kosher salt & black pepper
Olive oil (for potato skins)

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425°F. Wash and dry sweet potatoes. Rub skins with olive oil and salt. Bake 45–55 min until fork tender.
Slice in half, scoop out ⅓–½ of flesh, brush lightly with olive oil. Return to oven 5–8 min for sturdier boats.
Heat olive oil & butter in skillet over medium heat. Add garlic 30 sec. Add sausage 2–3 min. Add shrimp & Cajun seasoning, cook until opaque (1–2 min).
Stir in cream cheese, Greek yogurt, cayenne, salt & pepper. Optional: mash a spoonful of sweet potato for stability.
Spoon mixture into sweet potato halves. Bake 400–425°F for 8–10 min to lightly set. Cool slightly before portioning.
Store in airtight containers up to 4 days. Reheat gently to avoid overcooking shrimp.
