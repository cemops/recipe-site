Blackened Catfish w/ Veracruz Sauce - Blue Apron


Ingredients

Makes: 2 Servings
Prep Time: 15 Min    Cook Time: 30 - 40 Min.

Catfish Filets
1 14-oz Can Whole Peeled Tomatoes
2 Cloves Garlic
2 Scallions
1 Stalk Celery
1 Yukon Gold Potato
3 Tbs Rice Flour
1 1/2 Tbs Golden Raisins
1 oz Castelvetrano Olives
1/4 tsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes
1 1/2 Tbs Veracruz Spice Blend (Smoked Paprika, Ground Oregano, Ground Coriander, Ground Cumin)

Instructions

Prepare The Ingredients

Medium dice the potato
Thinly slice, on an angle, separating the white bottoms and green tops
Small dice the celery
Peel and roughly chop the garlic
Using the flat side of your knife, smash the olives; remove and discard the pits. Roughly chop the olives
Roughly chop the raisins
Place the tomatoes in a bowl; gently break apart with your hands

Cook The Potatoes

In a medium pan, heat 1 Tbs of olive oil on medium-high until hot.
Add the potatoes and season with salt and pepper
Cook, stirring occasionally 13 - 15 min, or until golden brown and softened.
Transfer to a plate and set aside in a warm place, Wipe the pan.

Make The Sauce

In a medium pot, heat 2 tsp of olive oil on medium-high until hot.
Add the celery, garlic and white bottoms of the scallions; season with salt and pepper.
Cook stirring frequently, 2 - 3 min, or until softened and fragrant.
Add the olives, raisins and the red pepper flakes.
Cook, stirring occasionally, 30 sec to 1 min, or until fragrant.
Add the tomatoes, and season with salt and pepper.
Cook, stirring occasionally, 12 - 14 min, or until thickened and saucy
Turn off hte heat and stir in the cooked potato, season with salt and pepper to taste

Coat The Catfish

Place the flour and spice blend on a plate; stir to combine
Pat the catfish fillets dry with a paper towel; season with salt and pepper on both sides.
Thoroughly coat 1 side of each seasoned fillet in the flour-spiced blend mixture.

Cook The Catfish

In the same pan, heat 2 tsp of olive oil on medium-high until hot
Add the coated catfish fillets, coated sides down, and cook 3 - 5 min, or until browned.
Flip and cook 2 - 3 min, or until lightly browned and cooked through. Remove from heat.

Plate Your Dish

Divide the sauce between 2 dishes. Top with the cooked catfish fillets. Garnish with the green tops of the scallions.
