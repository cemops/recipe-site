Hot Dog Nuggets


Ingredients:

5 Hot Dogs

1 Can Crescent Rolls

Ketchup and Mustard for Dipping

Instructions:

- Preheat oven to 400 degrees

- Slice the hot dogs into thin pieces, about 1/2 inch.

- Roll out the crescent dough

- Cut into 8 strips. Then cut lengthwise into 8 more strips. You should end up with 64 - 1.5 inch by 1.5 inch pieces.

- Wrap the hot dogs in the crescent pieces and place on the parchment paper lined baking sheet.

- Bake for 8 - 9 Min
