Turkey Sausage Sauté with Cabbage and Scallions


Ingredients:

4 oz Turkey Sausage

1/4 Cup Scallions, Chopped

1 Cup Cabbage, Chopped

Instructions:

- Sauté sausage in a skillet over medium-high heat, about 1 minute.

- Add scallions and cabbage. Sauté together until scallions and cabbage are tender and sausage is cooked through.

- Add salt and pepper to taste.
