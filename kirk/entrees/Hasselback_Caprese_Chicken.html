Hasselback Caprese Chicken


Ingredients:

2 Boneless, skinless chicken breasts

1/2 tsp Salt, divided

1/2 tsp Ground Pepper, divided

1 Medium Tomato, sliced

Fresh Mozzarella, halved and sliced

1/4 Cup Prepared Pesto

8 Cups Broccoli Florets

Instructions:

- Preheat oven to 375 degrees.

- Make crosswise cuts every 1/2 inch along both chicken breasts. Sprinkle chicken with salt and pepper. Fill the cuts alternately with tomato and mozzarella slices. Brush with pesto.

- Toss broccoli, oil, salt and pepper in a large bowl with remaining tomato.

- Place chicken and broccoli on one pan and cook 25 min.
