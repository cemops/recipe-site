Smoked Boneless Leg of Lamb

Recipe

Ingredients

Lamb:

5–6 lb Boneless Lamb Leg (or two 2.5 lb legs)

Olive oil, salt & pepper

Fresh thyme, rosemary, garlic (minced) — reserve some for baste

Veal Magic

Baste:

1 cup red wine vinegar

Reserved thyme, rosemary & garlic

Salt & pepper

1/2 cup vegetable oil (stir in)

Beet Sauce:

2 cups Greek yogurt

1/2 cup beet juice

1/2 cup chopped dill fronds

2 TBS fresh mint, finely chopped

Salt & pepper

Instructions

The Night Before:

Cover lamb with olive oil, salt & pepper

Mince herbs & garlic and spread over lamb

Refrigerate overnight

The Day Of:

Remove lamb several hours ahead to bring to room temperature

Preheat smoker to 300°F using apple or cherry wood

Smoke lamb at 250–275°F for 2.5 hours

Baste every 45 minutes until internal temp reaches 130°F

Heat grill to medium-high

Reverse sear until internal temp reaches 140°F

Remove from grill & rest 10–15 minutes

Slice and serve immediately
