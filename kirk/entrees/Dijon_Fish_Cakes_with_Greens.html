Dijon Fish Cakes with Greens


Ingredients:

4 - 6 oz Tilapia Filets - or 1.5 lbs Cod

1/2 Cup Mayonnaise

2 Large Eggs

2 TBS Chopped Fresh Dill

1 TBS Dijon Mustard

1 tsp Dijon Mustard

3/4 Cup Panko

2 TBS Canola Oil

2 TBS Olive Oil

1 1/2 TBS Red Wine Vinegar

5 oz Salad Greens

Kosher Salt and Black Pepper

HALF RECIPE

2 - 3 oz Tilapia Filets - or 3/4 lbs Cod

1/4 Cup Mayonnaise

1 Large Eggs

1 TBS Chopped Fresh Dill

1/2 TBS Dijon Mustard

1 tsp Dijon Mustard

1/3 Cup (STRONG) Panko

2 TBS Canola Oil

2 TBS Olive Oil

1 1/2 TBS Red Wine Vinegar

5 oz Salad Greens

Kosher Salt and Black Pepper

Instructions:

- Heat oven to 400 degrees F. Place the tilapia on a rimmed baking sheet and season with salt and pepper. Bake until cooked through, about 10 - 12 min. Let cool and flake into small pieces.

- In a medium bowl, combine the mayonnaise, eggs, dill, and 1 TBS of mustard. Fold in the tilapia and panko. Form the mixture into 8 cakes and chill for at least 30 min. (or overnight if doing ahead)

- Heat the canola oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat. Cook the cakes until golden, 3 - 5 min per side.

- Meanwhile, in a large bowl whisk together the olive oil, vinegar, 1 tsp of mustard, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp pepper. Add the salad greens and toss to combine. Serve with the fish cakes.
