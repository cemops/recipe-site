Ahi Tuna with Wild Rice Salad


Ingredients:

Tuna Melt

1 lb sushi grade tuna

1 loaf Foccacia Bread

8 oz Gruyere Cheese

1 Avocado

Olive Oil

Wild Rice Salad

2 Cups Wild Rice - Cooked, Cooled

1/4 Cup red bell pepper

1 Mango, sliced

1/2 bunch Cilantro - roughly chopped

1/2 bunch Thai Basil - roughly chopped

1/2 bunch Scallion - sliced

1 bunch Watercress or Arugula

Wasabi Mayo

2 tsp Wasabi Paste

5 oz Mayo

Tuna Spice

2 tsp White Pepper

2 tsp Black Pepper

1 tsp Cumin

1 tsp Salt

1/2 tsp Sugar

Asian Vinaigrette

6 oz Canola Oil

2 oz Rice Wine Vinegar

1 tsp Honey

2 tsp Fish Sauce

1 tsp Thai Chili

1 Tbs Shallot - fine diced

1 Tbs Cilantro - fine chop

1 Lime - zest and juice

1 tsp Sesame Seeds

Instructions:

Tuna Melt

- Slice bread for 4 open-faced sandwiches, brush with oil and grill until lightly toasted and melt cheese.

- Rub tuna with tuna spice and sear tuna on high heat until rare.

- Slice Avocado.

- Slice Tuna Thin.

Wild Rice Salad

- Combine wild rice, scallions, mango, cilantro, and Thai basil with vinaigrette.

Assembly

- Spread Wasabi mayo on toasted cheese bread.

- Place avocado, watercress, and tuna on the bread.

- Sprinkle with Sesame seeds.

- Plate with Rice.
