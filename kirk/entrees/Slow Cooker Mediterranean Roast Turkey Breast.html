Slow Cooker Mediterranean Roast Turkey Breast


Ingredients

1 - 4 LB Boneless Turkey Breast, trimmed
1/2 cup Chicken Broth, divided
2 TBS fresh Lemon Juice
2 Cups Chopped Onion
1/2 Cup pitted Kalamata Olives
1/2 Cup oil-packed Sun Dried Tomatoes, thinly sliced
1 tsp Greek Seasoning
1/2 tsp Salt
1/4 tsp Black Pepper
3 TBS Flour

Instructions

Place turkey breast, 1/4 cup chicken broth, lemon juice, onion, kalamata olives, sun-dried tomatoes, Greek seasoning, salt, and pepper in the slow cooker. Cook on low for 7 hours.
Combine thle remaining 1/4 cup chicken broth and the flour in a small bowl; whisk until smooth. Stir into slow cooker. Cover and coo on Low for an additional 30 min.
