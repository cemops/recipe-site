Lobster Thermidor


Ingredients

1 1/2 lb cooked lobster (or 4 lobster tails)
2 tbsp butter
1 whole shallot, finely diced
1/2 cup dry white wine
1/2 cup heavy cream
1/2 tsp whole grain mustard
1 tbsp chives (or green onions)
1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
1 whole egg yolk
1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese
Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

Cut lobster in half lengthwise; remove meat, chop, and set aside.
Melt butter over medium heat; sauté shallot until tender.
Add white wine and reduce by 3/4.
Add heavy cream and reduce by 1/2.
Stir in mustard, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
Toss lobster meat with the cream sauce, egg yolk, parmesan, and chives.
Place lobster shells on a baking dish and fill with mixture; top with remaining parmesan.
Bake at 400°F until bubbling and golden brown.
