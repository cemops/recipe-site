Lemon-Pepper Chicken


Ingredients:

4 (or package of 3) skinless, boneless chicken breast

2 tsp lemon-pepper seasoning

1 tsp olive oil

Cooking spray

1/4 Cup fat-free, low-sodium chicken broth

1/4 Cup balsamic vinegar

Directions:

- Pound chicken breast to 1/2 inch thickness with a meat mallet, sprinkle both sides with seasoning.

- Heat oil in a large cast iron skillet over medium-high heat. Coat chicken with cooking spray.

- Add to skillet and cook 6 - 7 min on each side. Remove and keep warm.

- Add broth and vinegar to pan; cook 3 minutes, stirring occasionally and scraping pan to loosen browned bits. Drizzle sauce over chicken.
