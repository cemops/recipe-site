Tuna Steak with Mango and Cucumber Salsa


Ingredients:

1 Small Mango, Peeled and Finely Diced

1 Small Red Onion, Finely Diced

1/2 Cucumber, Diced

1/2 Small Bunch of Cilantro, roughly chopped

1/2 Small Bunch Basil, roughly chopped

2 1/2 TBS unsalted peanuts, roughly chopped (optional)

1 Red Chile, seeded and finely chopped (or Poblano)

2 tsp Fish Sauce (find a replacement)

Juice of 2 Limes

Olive Oil for Drizzling

2 Tuna Steaks

Salt and freshly ground pepper

2 Little Gem Lettuce, leaves separated and washed

Instructions:

- Mix the diced mango with the onion, cucumber, cilantro, basil, peanuts, and red chile. Pour in the fish sauce and lime juice and mix everything together. Taste and adjust the seasoning as necessary.

- Drizzle a little olive oil over the tuna steaks and rub it into both sides of the fish. Season with salt and pepper.

- Place a heavy-bottomed nonstick skillet (cast iron) over high heat. When hot, carefully put the fish into the pan and cook for 50 seconds on each side. Remove the fish from the pan and leave to rest for a couple of minutes.

- Gently toss the lettuce leaves through the salsa and pile up onto two plates. Slice the tuna steaks thickly, then place them on top of the leaves and serve.
