Chicken Stroganoff


Ingredients:

4 Bacon Strips, diced

1 Lb boneless, skinless chicken breast

1 Medium onion, chopped

2 Cans (4 1/2 oz each) sliced mushrooms, drained. OR, 2 8oz packages fresh mushrooms.

1 1/2 Cups chicken broth

2 Garlic cloves, minced

1/2 tsp Salt

1/8 tsp Paprika

Pepper to taste

2 TBS all-purpose flour

1 Cup sour cream

Hot Cooked noodles

Additional paprika, as needed

Instructions:

- In a skillet, cook bacon over medium heat until crisp. Remove to paper towels reserving 2 TBS of drippings.

- In the drippings, cook the chicken, onion and mushrooms until the chicken is no longer pink. If using fresh mushrooms, cook those first for about 10 min. then ad the chicken and onion.

- Add broth, garlic, salt, paprika, pepper and bacon. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, cover and simmer for 10 min.

- Combine flour and sour cream until smooth, add to the skillet.

- Bring to a boil, cook and stir for 2 min. or until thickened.

- Serve over noodles, sprinkle with paprika if desired.
