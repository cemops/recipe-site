Halibut with Sugar Snap Pea Salad

Serves 2


Ingredients

1 tbsp fresh lime juice
1 tsp grated fresh ginger
2 tbsp olive oil, divided
Kosher salt & freshly ground black pepper
12 oz sugar snap peas (about 4 cups), strings removed
1 small red onion, thinly sliced
1 tbsp sesame seeds, toasted (optional)
1 lb halibut fillet, cut into 4–6 oz pieces
1 lime, cut into wedges (for serving)

Instructions

In a large bowl, whisk together lime juice, ginger, 1 tbsp olive oil, and ¼ tsp each salt and pepper.
Add sugar snap peas, red onion, and sesame seeds (if using); toss to coat. Set aside.
Heat remaining 1 tbsp olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat.
Season halibut with ½ tsp salt and ¼ tsp pepper.
Cook halibut until opaque and flakes easily, about 3–5 minutes per side.
Serve halibut alongside snap pea salad with lime wedges.
