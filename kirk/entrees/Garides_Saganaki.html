Garides Saganaki (Frying Pan Shrimp)


Ingredients:

1 Tbs Olive Oil

1 Onion, finely chopped

2 Garlic Cloves, finely chopped

1 - 14 oz Can Low Sodium Chopped Tomatoes

2 Tbs Tomato Paste

3/4 Cup Dry White Wine

1/2 tsp Sugar

Salt & Freshly Ground Black Pepper

12 oz Large Shrimp - cooked

4.5 oz Feta Cheese

Thyme Leaves

Instructions:

1. Heat half of the oil in a large frying pan. Add the onion and cook over low heat for 8 min, or until soft and translucent, then stir in the garlic and cook for a few more seconds.

2. Add the tomatoes, 3/4 cup cold water, tomato paste, wine, sugar, and a little seasoning. Bring to a boil then reduce heat and simmer gently, stirring occasionally for 20 - 30 min until thick and pulpy.

3. Stir the shrimp into the sauce, remove from the heat and crumble the feta on top. Pop under a broiler until the cheese melts and turns golden brown, then sprinkle with thyme. Serve with crusty bread and crisp salad.
