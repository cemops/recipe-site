Soft Shell Crab on the Grill

Serves 3–6


Ingredients

6 soft-shell crabs
1/4 cup olive oil
Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Instructions

Rinse crabs under cold running water and use a small brush if necessary to remove dirt from shells.
Twist off and discard the apron. Fold back the pointed sides of the top shell to expose the gills; remove gills on both sides.
Using kitchen scissors, cut across the front of the crab about 1/4 inch behind the eyes and mouth, and squeeze out the small sac behind the mouth.
Preheat grill to medium-high heat with the lid down.
Brush crabs with some olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
Place crabs on the grill, cover, and cook, turning every few minutes and basting with remaining olive oil, until orange and cooked through, 7–9 minutes depending on size.
Remove from grill and serve hot.
