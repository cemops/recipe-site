Provencal Grilled Chicken and Herbed Penne

A fresh, herby pasta dish that's delicious warm or chilled.


Ingredients:

8 oz. penne pasta (or herb flavored)
4 medium skinless, boneless chicken breasts or tenders
1 medium zucchini, halved lengthwise
8 thick asparagus spears
1 Tbs olive oil (for grilling)
2 Tbs olive oil (for dressing)
1 Tbs Herbs de Provence
1/2 tsp salt
1 Tbs snipped fresh thyme
1/2 cup finely shredded Asiago cheese
Pepper to taste

Directions:

Cook pasta according to package directions; drain and return to pan.
Brush chicken, zucchini, and asparagus with 1 Tbs olive oil.
Sprinkle vegetables and chicken with Herbs de Provence and salt.
Grill chicken and vegetables over medium heat for 12–15 minutes or until done.
Transfer to cutting board; cool slightly and cut into 1-inch pieces.
Add chicken, vegetables, 2 Tbs olive oil, and thyme to pasta; toss to coat.
Serve warm or chilled. Top with Asiago cheese and season with pepper.
