One - Pan Chicken w/ Lima Beans, Leeks, Spinach


Ingredients

1 Whole chicken cut up, skin removed
1 TBS Olive oil
1 Head Garlic, cut in half horizontally
1 Leek, trimmed, halved lengthwise, and sliced
3/4 Cup dry white wine
1 1/2 Cups low sodium chicken broth
2 Thyme sprigs
2 - 15 oz cans lima beans, drained and rinsed (can sub. Fava Beans or Red or White Kidney Beans)
8 oz baby spinach leaves

Instructions

Preheat the oven to 350 degrees
Place a large roasting pan on oven (large cast iron skillet works) over medium high heat, add olive oil. Brown the chicken on all sides until nicely colored.
Add the garlic, cut side down, then add the leak slices and stir around in the oil.
Pour in the white wine and allow to bubble for 2 min, then add the stock. Use a wooden spoon to scrape up any bits stuck to the bottom.
Turn off the heat and add thyme sprigs, lima beans, and spinach, nestling them between the chicken pieces. Season w/ salt and pepper and mix everything together, then put into the oven.
Bake for 35 to 40 min, until the chicken is cooked through. Give the contents of the pan a stir occasionally to make sure everything is cooked evenly.
Remove pan from the oven and let rest for 5 min. before serving in warm shallow bowls.

Notes

** This recipe can be halved **

** If you want to leave the skin on: Turn chicken skin side up after browning and be careful not to pour the wine or the stock over the browned skin because it won't crisp up properly in the oven. **
