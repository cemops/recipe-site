Caribbean Chicken with Creamy Avocado Sauce


Ingredients:

4 - 6 oz skinless, boneless chicken breast halves

2 Tbs Caribbean rub

1/4 tsp Salt - Divided

Cooking Spray

2 tsp fresh lime juice

1/2 Cup Sour Cream

1 tsp fresh lime juice

1 ripe Avocado, peeled, seeded and quartered

Chopped fresh Cilantro (optional)

Instructions:

- Sprinkle chicken with Caribbean rub. Grill 6 - 7 min on each side or until chicken is done. Sprinkle 2 tsp of lime juice over each hot chicken.

- While chicken cooks; place sour cream, 1 tsp lime juice, remaining 1/8 tsp salt, and avocado in a blender and process until smooth.

- Serve over chicken, sprinkle with Cilantro.
