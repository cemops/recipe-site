Poorman’s Jambalaya


Seasoning Mix:

4 small whole bay leaves

1 tsp salt

1 tsp white pepper

1 tsp dry mustard

1 tsp ground red pepper

1 tsp gumbo file (file powder)

1/2 tsp ground cumin

1/2 tsp black pepper

1/2 tsp dried thyme leaves

Ingredients:

4 TBS margarine

6 ounces tasso (canned smoked ham)

6 ounces andouille smoked sausage

1 1/2 cups chopped onions

1 1/2 cups chopped celery

1 1/2 cups chopped green bell peppers

1 1/2 tsp minced garlic

2 cups uncooked rice

4 cups beef, pork or chicken stock

Instructions:

- Thoroughly combine the seasoning mix and set aside.

- In a large heavy skillet (preferably cast iron) melt the margarine over high heat. Add the tasso and andouille; cook 5 min, stirring occasionally.

- Add the onions, celery, bell peppers, seasoning mix and garlic. Stir well and continue cooking until browned, about 10 to 12 min, stirring occasionally and scraping the pan bottom well.

- Stir in the rice and cook 5 min, stirring and scraping pan bottom occasionally.

- Add the stock, stirring well. Bring to a boil; reduce heat and simmer until rice is tender but still a bit crunchy, about 20 min, stirring occasionally toward the end of the cooking time.

- Meanwhile heat the serving plates in a 250 degree oven. Remove bay leaves and serve immediately. 2 cups for main course, 1 cup as an appetizer. Serve with Creole Sauce.
