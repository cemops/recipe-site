Crunchy Cracker-Coated Chicken Tenders

Great with dipping sauces like BBQ or Honey Dijon Dippping Suace


Ingredients:

2 Large Egg Whites

1 TBS grated Parmesan Cheese

1 tsp dried Italian Seasoning

1/4 tsp salt

1/4 tsp black pepper

1 Cup crushed reduced-fat baked whole-grain wheat crackers (Triscuits)

1 1/4 lb Chicken Tenders

Cooking Spray

Instructions:

- Place a jelly-roll pan in oven, and pre-heat to 450 degrees.

- Combine first 5 ingredients in a shallow dish; stir well. Place crushed crackers in another shallow dish or plate.

- Dip chicken tenders in egg white mixture. Dredge chicken in crushed crackers, pressing firmly to coat; coat chicken tenders with cooking spray.

- Place chicken on pan in a single layer. Bake at 450 for 25 min. or until chicken is done.
