Beef Tenderloin & Marinade

Serves 4–6


Ingredients

1 Beef Tenderloin

Marinade

4 cloves garlic, slivered
1/2 cup olive oil
1/4 tsp Tabasco
1 cup port wine
1 cup soy sauce
Salt and pepper, to taste
Bouquet garni: 2 bay leaves, thyme

Instructions

Thaw and trim the tenderloin.
Marinade overnight.

Option 1: Smoke and Reverse Sear

Preheat smoker to 250°F.
Add water/apple juice and fruit wood chips to smoker.
Smoke at 250°F for ~1 hour until internal temp reaches 120°F.
Preheat grill to high heat.
Sear tenderloin on grill 3 minutes each side (~12 minutes total).
Remove and let rest for 15 minutes.

Option 2: Grill

Grill over direct high heat for 20 minutes, turning every 5 minutes.
Move to indirect heat for 20–25 minutes, turning occasionally until cooked to 130°F.
Remove and let rest for 15 minutes.
