Shrimp Diane

Makes 2 Servings


Ingredients:

1 3/4 lbs medium shrimp (with heads & tails if using for stock)

6 Tbs seafood stock

1 1/2 sticks unsalted butter (divided)

1/4 cup very finely chopped green onions

3/4 tsp salt

1/2 tsp minced garlic

1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

1/4 tsp white pepper

1/4 tsp black pepper

1/4 tsp dried sweet basil leaves

1/4 tsp dried thyme

1/4 tsp dried oregano

1/2 lb mushrooms, sliced 1/4"

3 Tbs finely chopped fresh parsley

French bread, pasta, or hot cooked rice (for serving)

Instructions:

Rinse and peel shrimp; reserve shells to make shrimp stock.

In a large skillet, melt 1 stick of butter over high heat.

Add green onions, salt, garlic, cayenne, basil, thyme, and oregano. Stir well.

Add shrimp and sauté ~1 minute until just pink, shaking the pan instead of stirring.

Add mushrooms and 1/4 cup stock. Then add remaining 4 Tbs butter in chunks.

Continue shaking the pan until everything is thoroughly combined and sauce is creamy.

Serve immediately with French bread, or over pasta or rice.
