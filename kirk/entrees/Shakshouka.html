Shakshouka


Ingredients

2 Tbs Olive Oil
1 Onion, Halved and Thinly Sliced
2 Red Peppers, Finely Sliced
2 Garlic Cloves, Finely Chopped
1 Jalepeno, Seeded and Finely Chopped
1 tsp Sugar
1 - 14 oz Can Diced Tomatoes
Salt and Freshly Ground Black Pepper
4 Large Eggs
Small Bunch Flat Leaf Parsely, Finely Chopped

Instructions

Heat the oil in a heavy bottomed (I used stainless steel pan) and cook onion and pepper for 2 - 3 min, until soft. Toss in the garlic and jalapeno and cook until the vegetables begin to color.
Add the sugar and tomatoes, and cook over medium heat for 3 - 4 min, until the mixture is slightly pulpy. Season well.
Using a wooden spoon, make 4 indentations in the tomato mixture and crack an egg into each one. Reduce the heat, cover the pan, and cook until the whites are set. Scatter with the parsley and serve with crusty fresh bread.
