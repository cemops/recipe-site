Ratatouille w/ Pasta


Ingredients

Onions x 1.5
Garlic x 5
Shallot
Zucchini x 3
Eggplant x 2 (or 1 Large one)
15 oz Can Diced Tomato
Cubanero Pepper
Red Pepper
Olive Oil
Salt and Pepper
** Pasta of Choice**  Penne or Rigotonni

Instructions

Cook pasta according to directions
Roughly cut all vegetables and add to pan
Cook 30 to 40 min.
Combine ratatouille and pasta
