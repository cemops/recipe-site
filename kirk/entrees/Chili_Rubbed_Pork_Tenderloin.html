Chile Rubbed Pork Tenderloin


Ingredients:

Pork Tenderloin
Olive Oil
Fresh Oregano Leaves

Rub:

1 TBS Brown Sugar, packed
1 TBS Chile Powder
1 tsp Ground Cumin
3/4 tsp Kosher Salt
1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
1/4 tsp Ground Black Pepper

Directions:

In a small bowl, combine all Rub ingredients and mix with a fork.
Coat the tenderloin with olive oil and apply the Rub evenly.
Let the tenderloin stand at room temperature for 15-30 minutes.
Grill over direct medium heat, turning every 5 minutes, for 20 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 150°F.
Remove from grill and rest for 3-5 minutes.
Season with fresh oregano and slice into 1/2-inch pieces.
