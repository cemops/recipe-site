Smoked Hamburger

Serves 2–4


Ingredients

1 lb ground beef (preferably chuck with ~20% fat)
Seasoning of choice (e.g., Canadian Steak or Cajun)
Cheese (optional)

Instructions

Preheat smoker to 225°F.
Season burgers with your choice of seasoning.
Place burgers on a rack before putting them in the smoker.
Smoke burgers for about 45 minutes, until internal temp reaches 135°F.
Add cheese on top and smoke for an additional 15 minutes, until internal temp reaches 155°F.
