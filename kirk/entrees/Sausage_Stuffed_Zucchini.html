Sausage Stuffed Zucchini

Serves 4


Puree Ingredients

4 large plum tomatoes (~1 lb total)
1 small onion, cut into 1/2 inch slices
2 small bell peppers (1 red, 1 yellow)
2 TBS fresh chopped basil & oregano leaves
2 garlic cloves
2 TBS red wine vinegar
1 tsp kosher salt
1/4 tsp black pepper
1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil (EVOO)

Zucchini & Sausage Ingredients

4 medium zucchini, cut in half lengthwise
1 lb Italian sausage, medium or sweet
1 cup grated Romano cheese
1/3 cup panko bread crumbs

Instructions

Grill tomatoes, onions, and peppers over direct medium heat until soft (tomatoes & onions ~8–10 min; peppers ~10–15 min). Remove vegetables as done. Cover peppers to steam for 10 min.
When cool enough to handle, peel tomatoes and remove stem ends. Peel, core, and seed the peppers.
In a food processor, combine tomatoes, onion, peppers, basil, oregano, garlic, vinegar, salt, and pepper. Puree until smooth, slowly adding olive oil.
Warm puree in a medium pan over low heat until slightly thickened, 3–5 minutes. Remove from heat.
Score zucchini where seeds meet the flesh and scoop out with a spoon.
In a medium bowl, gently combine sausage, cheese, bread crumbs, and 1/2 cup of puree. Fill zucchini boats with mixture.
Grill zucchini over direct medium heat about 15 min. Spread remaining puree over each zucchini after plating.
Serve with rice or pasta.
