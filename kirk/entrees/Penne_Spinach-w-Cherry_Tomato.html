Penne with Spinach & Cherry Tomatoes

Serves 2–3 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 15 min


Ingredients

8 oz pasta (penne)
1 veggie bouillon cube
10 oz cherry tomatoes, halved
10 oz baby spinach, roughly chopped
3 cloves garlic, minced
2 Tbs olive oil
12 Kalamata olives, roughly chopped
1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, finely grated
Salt & black pepper, to taste

Instructions

Bring a large pot of water to boil; add bouillon cube.
While water heats, prep cherry tomatoes, spinach, olives, and grate cheese.
Add pasta to boiling water and cook according to package instructions.
Heat 1 Tbs olive oil in a sauté pan with 1 clove garlic for 30 sec over medium-high, stirring constantly.
Add spinach gradually; once wilted, transfer to a large bowl.
Heat 1 Tbs olive oil with 2 cloves garlic in pan, cook briefly. Add cherry tomatoes, salt, and pepper; cook 3 minutes. Combine with cooked spinach.
Drain pasta and add to spinach-tomato mixture. Stir in chopped olives and 2/3 of Parmesan cheese. Serve immediately.
