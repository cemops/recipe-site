Shrimp and Artichoke Pasta


Ingredients:

9 ounces pasta, cooked according to package directions

1 lb large shrimp, cleaned and deveined

3/4 cup Zesty Italian salad dressing, divided

2 cups baby bella mushrooms, sliced

1 small onion, thinly sliced

1 (14 ounce) can artichoke hearts, drained, cut into quarters

1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped, plus more for garnish

1/4 cup parmesan cheese, grated

Instructions:

- In large skillet on medium-high heat, add mushrooms, onion, artichokes with 1/2 cup dressing and cook for about 3 minutes until veggies begin to soften. If using fresh mushrooms, start them for about 5 min. first, then add the onion and artichoke

- Add shrimp and parsley and cook for about 3 minutes or until shrimp turn pink and vegetables are tender, stirring frequently.

- Toss with hot cooked pasta and remaining 1/4 cup salad dressing.

- Sprinkle with cheese and parsley.
