Grilled Sea Scallops with Ponzu Glaze


Ingredients:

Scallops

Sea Scallops

Extra Virgin Olive Oil

Rosemary and Thyme sprigs

Ponzu Glaze

1/2 cup Soy Sauce

1/4 cup water

1/8 cup Oyster Sauce

2 Tbsp Brown Sugar

2 Tbsp Lime Juice

1 Tbsp White Vinegar

Instructions:

Ponzu Glaze

- Mix glaze ingredients together in a bowl. Simmer on medium heat to reduce by 1/4 if used as a sauce.

Scallops

- Skewer Sea Scallops on water-soaked Rosemary and Thyme sprigs. Brush Scallops with EVOO.

- Grill for approx. 3 - 4 min. on Med-high grill. Turn and brush with Ponzu Glaze.

- Grill another 4 min. until scallops feel slightly firm.

- Remove from grill and brush again with Ponzu Glaze.
