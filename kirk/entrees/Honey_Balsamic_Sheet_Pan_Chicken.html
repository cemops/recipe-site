Honey Balsamic Sheet Pan Chicken


Ingredients:

1/4 Cup Balsamic Vinegar

6 TBS Honey

3 TBS Olive Oil, divided

2 cloves Garlic, minced

1 tsp dried Thyme

3/4 tsp dried Rosemary

1/2 tsp Red Chili Flakes

3/4 tsp Kosher Salt

1/4 tsp Black Pepper

3 Chicken Breasts

4 Red Potatoes, 1/8 th

1 pint Cherry Tomatoes

1 lb (three handfuls) Green Beans, trimmed

Directions:

- Preheat oven to 425 degrees

- Combine vinegar, honey, 1 TBS olive oil, garlic, thyme, rosemary, chili flakes, salt and pepper

- Pour the marinade and chicken in a bag and marinate for 30 min.

- Place the potatoes on a sheet pan, drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, stir to coat. Bake for 25 min.

- Remove the sheet pan and toss the green beans and tomatoes with the potatoes.

- Place the chicken on the veggies and spread the marinade over both the chicken and veggies.

- Return the sheet to the oven and cook for 40 min. basting at least once. Let chicken stand 5 min. before serving.
