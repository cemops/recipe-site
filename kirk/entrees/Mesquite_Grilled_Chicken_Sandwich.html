Mesquite-Grilled Chicken Sandwich

Serves 4


Ingredients

2 cups mesquite wood chips
1/4 cup fat-free mayonnaise
1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (for mayo mix)
1/2 tsp finely shredded lime peel or lemon peel
4 medium skinless, boneless chicken breasts (thinly sliced if possible)
2 TBS Worcestershire sauce (for brushing chicken)
1/2 tsp garlic pepper
1/8 tsp salt
1/2 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
Whole wheat hamburger buns
Tomato slices
Avocado slices

Optional / Tips

Grilled onion, red/orange pepper, romaine lettuce
Substitute whole wheat flat buns

Instructions

At least 1 hour before grilling, soak mesquite wood chips.
Combine mayonnaise, 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce, and lime/lemon peel. Cover and refrigerate until serving.
If chicken is not thinly sliced, place each piece between plastic wrap and pound to ~1/2 inch thick.
Brush both sides of chicken with 2 TBS Worcestershire sauce; sprinkle with garlic pepper and salt.
Start grilling vegetables ~5 minutes before adding chicken.
Grill chicken over direct medium heat 5–7 minutes, turning once. Sprinkle each piece with mozzarella cheese.
Toast bread and romaine lettuce with ~2 minutes remaining.
Spread mayo on bread; add chicken, lettuce, tomato, and avocado. Serve with grilled vegetables on the side.
