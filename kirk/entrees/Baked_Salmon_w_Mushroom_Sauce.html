Baked Salmon with Mushroom Cream Sauce

Serves 4

Pairs well with Roasted Potato-Carrot | Broccoli-Onions


Ingredients

1 1/2 lbs fresh salmon, skin on or off
2 tbsp unsalted butter
2 cups sliced fresh mushrooms (cremini, button, or shiitake)
2 green onions, chopped
2 tbsp all-purpose flour
2 tsp chopped fresh parsley
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 cup milk (whole or 2%)
1/4 cup dry white wine (or 2 tbsp water + 2 tbsp lemon juice)

Instructions

Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
Cut salmon into 4 equal portions and pat dry.
Lightly butter a 13×9-inch baking dish and arrange salmon evenly.
Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat.
Add mushrooms and green onions; sauté 5–7 minutes until tender.
Stir in flour, parsley, and salt; cook 1 minute to form a roux.
Gradually whisk in milk; bring to a boil.
Cook and stir until thickened, about 2–3 minutes.
Stir in wine (or water and lemon juice) and simmer 1 minute.
Pour mushroom sauce evenly over the salmon.
Bake uncovered 20–25 minutes, until salmon flakes easily.
