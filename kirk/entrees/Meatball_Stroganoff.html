Meatball Stroganoff

Comfort-food classic with a creamy beefy sauce


Ingredients

3 cups egg noodles
1 tbsp olive oil
1 package (12 oz) frozen meatballs or 1 lb fresh meatballs
1 1/2 cups beef broth
1 tsp dried parsley flakes
1/4 tsp dried basil
1/4 tsp dried oregano
1/4 tsp salt
1/4 tsp black pepper
1 cup heavy whipping cream
3/4 cup sour cream

Instructions

Cook egg noodles according to package directions until al dente; drain and set aside.
In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium-high heat. Brown the meatballs, then remove from the pan.
Add beef broth to the skillet, stirring to loosen browned bits from the bottom.
Add parsley, basil, oregano, salt, and pepper. Bring to a boil and cook 5–7 minutes, until liquid reduces to about 1/2 cup.
Return meatballs to the pan. Add cooked noodles and heavy cream.
Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce heat and simmer, covered, for 3–5 minutes until slightly thickened.
Stir in sour cream and heat through. Do not boil after adding sour cream.

Notes

  • This recipe pairs perfectly with homemade meatballs.
  • Use your favorite meatball recipe here: Meatballs Recipe
  • If the sauce gets too thick, thin with a splash of beef broth or cream.
