Double Smoked - Cajun Glazed Ham


Ingredients:

8 - 12 lb pre-cooked, smoked spiral cut ham

2 Cups Apple Wood Chips

2 Cups Apple Juice, divided

Glaze:

1 Cup brown sugar

4 Jalapeños, seeded and diced

2 Garlic cloves, minced

2 TBS white wine vinegar

2 TBS spicy brown mustard

1 TBS paprika

1/2 tsp cayenne pepper

1/2 tsp dried thyme

1/2 tsp dried oregano

Instructions:

- Overnight: Bring ham to room temp, trim excess fat, rinse, and pat dry.

- 1-2 hours before smoking: Soak wood chips in water.

- Preheat smoker to 300°F.

- Place ham in a foil container, pour half the apple juice over the ham, and smoke uncovered at 220-250°F for 1 hour, spritzing with apple juice every 15 minutes.

- Preheat grill to 400°F for indirect heat (outside burners on medium-high, inside burners on low).

- Lower outside burners to medium, turn off inside burners, cover ham with foil, and bake at 300-330°F for 1 1/2 hours.

- Move ham to a Dutch oven, uncover, and baste with the glaze every 10 minutes for 30 minutes.

- If traveling: cover, transport, and serve with remaining glaze.

- Uncover ham and baste with glaze every 10 minutes for 20-30 minutes. Let rest for 10-15 minutes before carving.

- Serve with remaining glaze.
