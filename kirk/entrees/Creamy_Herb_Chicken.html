Creamy Herb Chicken


Ingredients:

For The Chicken:

4 Chicken Breasts

Onion Powder and Garlic Powder to Season

Herbes De Provence to Season

1 tsp Fresh Chopped Parsley

Salt and Pepper

For The Sauce:
4 Cloves of Garlic, minced

1 tsp Fresh Chopped Parsley

1/2 tsp Herbes De Provence

1 Cup Half and Half

1 tsp Corn Starch mixed with 1 TBS water, until smooth

Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

- Coat the chicken with the onion and garlic powder, herbs, salt, and pepper.

- Heat 1 TBS oil in a large skillet over medium heat and cook until chicken is no longer pink, about 5 minutes each side. Transfer to a plate and set aside.

- To the same skillet, heat another 2 TBS of oil and saute garlic, parsley, herbes de provence for about 1 minute, or until fragrant.

- Stir in milk, season with salt and pepper to taste.

- Bring to a boil; add the cornstarch mixture to the center of the skillet, quickly stirring, until sauce has thickened slightly.

- Reduce heat and simmer gently for a further minute to allow sauce to thicken more.

- Return chicken to skillet, sprinkle with extra herbs if desired. Serve immediately.
