Skinny Omelette Options

Skinny Omelette Base:

2 Eggs in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat, well beaten and swirled around the pan evenly. Cook for 15 seconds to 1 minute. Season according to filling.

With a spatula, remove from pan onto cutting board. Spread filling across omelette, roll, then slice in half with a deep diagonal cut.

Filling Options:

Standard:

  • Pesto
  • Chives
  • Feta
  • Salad Greens
  • Salt

Caprese:

  • Long sliced tomato
  • Long sliced mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt / Pepper

Ratatouille:

  • Leftover grilled vegetables
  • Asiago Cheese
  • Olive Oil
  • Balsamic Vinegar
  • Salt / Pepper
  • OR Leftover Ratatouille

Chicken Teriyaki:

  • Grilled Chicken Teriyaki, long/thin sliced
  • Grilled Teriyaki Vegetables
  • Sesame Seeds
  • Happy Sauce

Thai Vegetable:

  • Red Curry Paste
  • Thai Grilled Chicken
  • Bean Sprouts
  • Green Onions
  • Cabbage or Cellophane Noodles

Breakfast:

  • Bacon
  • Tomato
  • Spinach
  • Asiago Cheese
  • Salt / Pepper

Steak:

  • Leftover Steak
  • Asparagus or Spinach
  • Happy Sauce

Southwest:

  • Long, thin sliced Avocado
  • Cilantro
  • Tomato
  • Frozen corn, thawed
  • Lime Juice
  • Salt / Pepper

Italian:

  • Polenta - fried and cut into long strips
  • Olives

Creole:

  • Creole Filling - Paul Prudhomme; pg 131 OR any meat pie filling pages 130 - 134

Walnut / Grape Salad:

  • Spring Mix Lettuce
  • Grapes - quartered
  • Walnuts - chopped and toasted
