Italian Chicken Skillet


Ingredients:

4 Skinless, Boneless Chicken Breast Halves

2 Cloves Garlic, Chopped

1/2 Cup Red Cooking Wine

1 - 28 oz Italian-style Diced Tomatoes

8 oz Small Seashell Pasta

5 oz Fresh Spinach, Chopped

1 Cup Shredded Mozzarella Cheese

Instructions:

- Heat the olive oil in a large skillet with a lid over medium heat, and cook and stir the chicken and garlic until the chicken is no longer pink in the center, 5 - 8 min.

- Pour the wine and diced tomatoes with their juice into the skillet, and bring to a boil over high heat while scraping any browned bits of food off of the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon.

- Stir in the shell pasta, and return to a boil. Cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, until the shells have cooked al dente, about 10 min.

- Spread the spinach over the top of the pasta, cover, and simmer until the spinach leaves are cooked, about 5 min. Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese evenly over the skillet, and simmer until the cheese has melted and the pasta is bubbling, about 5 min.
