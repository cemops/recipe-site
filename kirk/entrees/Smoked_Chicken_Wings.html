Smoked Chicken Wings

Serves 4–6


Ingredients

1 package chicken wings (Gerber Party Pack)
Olive oil
Poultry Magic seasoning
Hickory wood chunks

Instructions

Prepare wings the day before cooking.
Pat wings dry.
Place wings in a large bowl and lightly coat with olive oil; mix to coat evenly.
Sprinkle Poultry Magic seasoning over wings and mix to coat.
Place wings on 2 oiled smoker racks.
Refrigerate uncovered overnight.
Preheat smoker to 250°F.
Smoke wings at 225–250°F for 1 1/2 – 2 hours, until internal temp reaches 160°F.
Remove wings and let rest at least 10 minutes before serving.
