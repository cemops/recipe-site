Quick & Easy Soy Honey Chicken W/ Broccoli


Ingredients

1 lb Boneless Skinless Chicken Breast (Cut into 1" pieces)
Salt & Pepper
1 tsp Garlic Powder
1/4 Cup Reduced Sodium Soy Sauce
3 Tbs Honey
2 Garlic Cloves - minced
2 tsp Cornstarch
3 Cups Broccoli / Cauliflower or both
2 tsp Sesame Seeds

Instructions

In a large pan heat olive oil over medium / high heat. Add chicken and sprinkle with a large pinch of salt and pepper and add garlic powder. Cook chicken for about 5 min. until no longer pink and begins to brown.
Make the sauce: In a bowl; whisk together the soy sauce, honey, garlic, and cornstarch. Set aside.
Once chicken is browned, add the broccoli and cook 3 - 5 min until broccoli turns bright green and just begins to soften.
If the mixture seems too dry, add a little more olive oil
Add the soy sauce mixture and cook for 1 min until the sauce thickens.
Garnish with sesame seeds and server immediately
