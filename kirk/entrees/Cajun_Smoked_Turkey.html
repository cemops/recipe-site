Cajun Smoked Turkey


Ingredients:

Turkey:

1 10-12 lb turkey

Either stuffing OR

Green Apples

Onion

Celery

Brine:

1 1/2 Cups Salt

1 Cup Brown Sugar

1 Cup Molasses

1 Cup Honey

1/2 Cup Poultry Magic

4 Bay leaves

Bunch of Thyme

1 Tbs Peppercorns

1 small Bag of Ice

Rub:

1/2 Cup Kosher Salt

1/2 Cup Granulated Garlic

1 Tbs Poultry Seasoning

Poultry Magic (second step)

Injection:

1 Stick Butter

1 Cup Chicken Stock

1 Tbs Hot Sauce

1 tsp Granulated Garlic

1 tsp Cajun Seasoning

Instructions:

- Refrigerate turkey up to 3 days ahead to thaw.

- 2 days ahead: Combine 1 Gal of water with remaining brine ingredients in a large pot and bring to a boil. Let cool and stand.

- Add turkey and brine with enough water to cover the bird and refrigerate overnight or 24 hours.

- Rinse, dry and stuff the turkey.

- Combine the Injection ingredients and inject the turkey.

- Spray with canola (vegetable) oil.

- Combine first three Rub ingredients and season.

- Then season with Poultry Magic.

- Tie up the legs.

- Smoke with Apple or Cherry wood for 3 1/2 hours, turning once after 1 1/2 hours at 275 - 300 degrees.

Chef's Notes

This can also be done using a boned in turkey breast, usually around 5 - 8 lbs. This allows you to enjoy cajun turkey year 'round because it takes so much less effort. Skip the brine but still rub and inject (just use less of both). Smoke for approx 3 hours at 225 degrees.
