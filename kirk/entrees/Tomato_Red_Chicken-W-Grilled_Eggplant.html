Tomato Red Chicken Breast with Grilled Eggplant


Marinade Ingredients:

3 TBS EVOO

2 TBS minced shallot

1 TBS red wine vinegar

2 tsp tomato paste

1 tsp crushed red pepper

1 tsp kosher salt

1/2 tsp dried oregano or basil

1/4 tsp black pepper

Chicken:

4 Boneless, skinless chicken breast halves

Directions:

- In a large bowl, whisk the marinade ingredients. Add the chicken breasts to the bowl and turn to coat them evenly. Allow the chicken to marinate at room temp for 20 to 30 min before grilling.

- Prepare the grill for direct cooking over medium heat (350 to 450 degrees).

- Remove the chicken from the bowl and discard the marinade. Grill the chicken, smooth (skin) side down first over direct medium heat, turning once, approx 8 - 12 min total. Let rest 3 - 5 min.

Eggplant:

- Grill eggplant for 4 min over direct medium heat.

- Turn them over, top each slice with about 1 tsp freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, continue to grill until the cheese is melted, about 4 min.
