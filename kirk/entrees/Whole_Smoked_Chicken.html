Whole Smoked Chicken


Ingredients

1 Whole (3 - 5 lb.) Chicken
1 Lemon
1 medium Yellow Onion
3 Whole Fresh Garlic Cloves
Bunch of 'Poultry Herbs' (Thyme, Rosemary, Sage)
Apple or Cherry Wood Chips

Rub

1 TBS Garlic, crushed
1 tsp Onion Powder
1 tsp Salt
1 tsp Pepper

Brine

1/2 Cup Kosher Salt
1 Cup Brown Sugar
1 gallon water
Soy Sauce
Honey
Whole Peppercorns

Instructions

- Combine brine ingredients until salt is dissolved, add chicken and brine overnight.
- Prepare the smoker: Clean Grates, soak wood chips, Water & Apple Cider Vinegar. Preheat the grill at 225 degrees for 10 - 15 min.
- Remove chicken from brine, pat dry and stuff with lemon, onion, garlic and herbs. Tie the legs together.
- Place the chicken (with wings tucked underneath) on the grates and smoke for 2 1/2 - 3 hours. Chicken is done at 160 degrees. (Last time it took 4 + hours)
- Let rest for 15 - 20 min.
- ** Last time I used sauce from 'Ultimate BBQ Chicken' recipe in Evernote, Put a thin coat on at 150 degrees **
