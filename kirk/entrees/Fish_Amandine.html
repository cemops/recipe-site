Fish Amandine


Ingredients:

4 4-oz fresh or frozen skinless tilapia, trout, or halibut fillets

1/4 Cup buttermilk

1/2 Cup panko bread crumbs or fine dry bread crumbs

2 Tbs fresh parsley

1/2 tsp dry mustard

1/4 tsp salt

1/4 cup sliced almonds, coarsely chopped

2 Tbs grated Parmesan Cheese

1 Tbs butter

1/8 tsp crushed red pepper

1 lemon, cut into wedges to put on plates

Directions:

- Preheat oven to 450°F. Grease a shallow baking pan; set aside. Rinse fish; pat dry with paper towels.

- Pour buttermilk into a shallow dish. In another shallow dish combine bread crumbs, parsley, dry mustard, and salt. Dip fish into buttermilk, then into crumb mixture, turning to coat. Place coated fish in the prepared baking pan.

- Sprinkle fish with almonds and Parmesan; drizzle with melted butter. Sprinkle with crushed red pepper. Bake 4 to 6 minutes per 1/2" thickness of fish or until fish flakes easily when tested.



Nutrition Per Serving:

209 cal.

9 g total fat (3 g sat. fat)

67 mg chol.

302 mg sodium

7 g carb. (1 g fiber, 1 g sugars)

26 g pro.
