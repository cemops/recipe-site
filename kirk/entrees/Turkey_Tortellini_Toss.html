Turkey Tortellini Toss


Ingredients:

1 Package Fresh 3 Cheese Tortellini

1 LB Ground Turkey

2 Medium Zucchini, halved lengthwise and sliced

2 Garlic Cloves, Minced

1 1/2 Cups Cherry Tomatoes, Halved

1 tsp Dried Oregano

1/4 tsp Salt

1/4 tsp Crushed Red Pepper Flakes

1 Cup Shredded Asiago Cheese, Divided

1 Tbs Olive Oil

Instructions:

- Cook Tortellini according to instructions.

- Meanwhile, in a large skillet, cook turkey, zucchini, garlic over medium heat, 7 - 9 min or until turkey is no longer pink, breaking up turkey into crumbles.

- Drain. Add tomatoes, oregano, salt, pepper and cook 2 minutes longer. Stir in 3/4 Cup Cheese.

- Add tortellini to skillet and toss to combine. Drizzle with oil and sprinkle with remaining cheese.
