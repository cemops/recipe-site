Braised Beef Short Ribs


Ingredients:

3 1/2 lbs Bone-In English Style Cut Ribs

3 TBS Olive Oil

1 Cup Onions, Diced (about 1 medium)

4 Garlic Cloves, Minced

1/2 Cup Red Wine

2 Cups Beef Stock

2 TBS Tomato Paste

1/2 tsp Dried Thyme

12 oz Mushrooms, Cleaned and Quartered

Parsley, Fresh Flat-Leaf, Chopped

Instructions:

- Remove ribs from the refrigerator to bring them to room temperature.

- Heat olive oil in a cast iron skillet. Sear the ribs on all sides until browned. Remove and set aside.

- Add onion and garlic to the skillet and sauté until lightly browned, about 3 minutes.

- Add red wine and cook until reduced by half.

- Place beef stock, tomato paste, and thyme into a slow cooker.

- Add the reduced onion mixture to the slow cooker and nestle the ribs into the liquid.

- Cook on low for 5 hours, then add the mushrooms.

- Continue cooking for 1 more hour.

- If desired, remove the liquid and reduce it in a skillet to make a sauce.

- Garnish with chopped parsley before serving.
