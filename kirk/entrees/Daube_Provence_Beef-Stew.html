Daube Provence - Beef Stew


Ingredients:

3 Lb Boneless Chuck Roast

2 Garlic Cloves, crushed

1 Large White Onion, chopped

1 Shallot, chopped

3 Bay Leaves

1 Carrot, chopped

1 Small Fennel Bulb, chopped

3 in Strip of Dried Orange Peel

10 Black Peppercorns

2 Sprigs Thyme or Oregano

2 Sprigs Flat-Leaf Parsley

2 Cups Red Wine

2 TBS Olive Oil

3 oz Bacon or Diced Pancetta

Salt and Freshly Ground Black Pepper

Instructions:

- To marinate the beef, cut the roast into 2 in chunks and place in a large bowl. Add the garlic, onion, shallot, bay leaves, carrot, fennel, orange peel, peppercorns, thyme, and parsley. Pour in the wine, stir well, cover, and refrigerate overnight.

- Remove the beef from the marinade and wipe well with paper towels. Strain the marinade through a sieve into a bowl. Reserve both the ingredients and the marinade.

- Pour the oil into a large flameproof pan, add the bacon, and stir over medium heat for 2-3 min, until sizzling. Add the meat, increase the heat a little, and cook for 10 min, until brown on all sides.

- Pour in the drained marinade ingredients. Cook and stir for 5 min, then pour in the marinade. Add a little boiling water - just enough to cover the ingredients - and bring to a boil.

- Reduce heat to low. Cover and cook 2 hours, stir and remove the parsley, thyme, and bay leaves. Taste and adjust the seasoning. Simmer, uncovered, for 15 min and serve hot.

- ** If you prefer a deeper flavor: leave until cold, then remove and discard the surface fat, and reheat, covered, over low heat **
