Mediterranean Cod Bake

Double the ingredients for leftovers


Ingredients

10 oz cod fillets
1/2 lb small red-skinned potatoes, quartered
1/2 yellow bell pepper, sliced
1/2 orange bell pepper, sliced
1 cup grape tomatoes, halved (or Roma tomatoes)
1/2 medium onion, sliced
1/4 cup Kalamata olives, sliced (or black olives)
Olive oil
Salt and black pepper, to taste
