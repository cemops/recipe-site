Crawfish Enchiladas con Queso


Ingredients:

1/2 lb (2 sticks) unsalted butter, in all

1 cup finely chopped onions

1 cup canned green chilies, drained and chopped

3/4 cup finely chopped green bell peppers

2 3/4 tsp salt, in all

2 3/4 tsp white pepper

1 1/2 tsp ground red pepper (cayenne), in all

3/4 tsp dried oregano, in all

1/2 tsp minced garlic

3 cups heavy cream

1 cup sour cream

8 cups grated Monterey Jack cheese, 2 lbs, in all

2 lbs peeled crawfish tails

2/3 cup very finely chopped green onions

1/2 cup vegetable oil

20 (6") corn tortillas

Instructions:

- In a large skillet, melt 1 stick of the butter. Add the onions, green chilies, bell peppers, 1 1/4 tsp of salt, 3/4 tsp of white pepper, 1/2 tsp red pepper, 1/4 tsp oregano, and the garlic. Saute over medium heat for 10 minutes, stirring often.

- Stir in the heavy cream and bring mixture to a rapid boil; then reduce the heat and simmer uncovered for 10 minutes, stirring constantly. Add the sour cream; with a metal whisk, beat continuously until the sour cream is dissolved, about 3 minutes. Add 3 cups of the cheese and stir until melted. Set the sauce aside.

- In a 4-quart saucepan, melt the remaining 1 stick butter. Add the crawfish, green onions, and the remaining 1 1/2 tsp salt, 2 tsp white pepper, 1 tsp red pepper, and 1/2 tsp oregano. Saute over medium heat for about 6 minutes, stirring occasionally. Add the cheese sauce to the crawfish mixture and stir well. Simmer until the flavors are well blended, about 6 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally; set aside.

- In a small skillet, heat the oil to about 325 degrees. Holding tortillas one at a time with metal tongs, dip each into the hot oil just long enough to soften, about 1 second on each side; drain on paper towels. Spoon about 1/2 cup crawfish sauce on each tortilla and roll up tortilla.

- For the prettiest presentation, broil the enchiladas: Put them all, seam side down, in a flameproof baking dish. Cover each enchilada from end to end with a generous amount of additional sauce, then sprinkle each with 1/4 cup of cheese. Place the baking dish under a broiler near the flame until the cheese melts and begins to turn brown, about 2 minutes.

- Or, bake the enchiladas in a 350-degree oven until the cheese melts, about 5 to 8 minutes. Serve immediately.
