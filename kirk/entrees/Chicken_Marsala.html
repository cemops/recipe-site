Chicken Marsala by Chef John


Ingredients:

2 Skin-on, Boneless Chicken Breasts (I used skinless last time)

1 tsp Salt and Ground Black Pepper to Taste

3 TBS Butter, Divided

2 TBS Olive Oil

5 White Mushrooms, Sliced

1 Shallot, Minced

1 TBS All-purpose Flour

1 Cup Marsala Wine

2 Cups Chicken Stock

2 TBS Chopped Fresh Parsley

1 tsp Cold Butter

Instructions:

- Season chicken breasts all over with salt and pepper.

- Melt 1 1/2 TBS butter and olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook chicken breasts skin side down until browned, about 5 min. Flip and cook until breasts are almost cooked through, about 5 more min. Transfer to a plate.

- Melt 1 1/2 TBS butter in same skillet over medium high heat. Saute mushrooms with a pinch of salt and a pinch of pepper until mushrooms are golden, 5 - 7 min. Add minced shallot; cook and stir until softened, 2 to 3 min. Sprinkle flour over the top and cook and stir until the bitterness of the flour cooks off, 3 to 4 min.

- Pour wine into skillet and bring to a boil; cook and stir until wine reduces and sauce thickens, 3 to 4 min. Add chicken stock; bring to a simmer and cook until slightly reduced, 3 - 5 min.

- Return chicken breasts to skillet, reduce heat to low, and cook chicken, turning once, until no longer pink in the center and juices run clear, about 10 min. Internal temp should read 165 degrees.

- Move chicken to one side of the skillet and tilt the skillet so that the sauce pools at the bottom. Stir parsley and 1 tsp cold butter into the sauce, stirring constantly, until sauce is shiny and butter is completely melted. Transfer chicken to plates and spoon mushrooms and sauce over the top.
