Salmon with Chipotle Butter


Ingredients:

Chipotle Butter:

6 Tbs unsalted butter, softened

3 canned chipotle chiles in adobo sauce, seeded and minced

1 tsp minced garlic

1/2 tsp Kosher salt


Salmon:

4 salmon filets (with skin)

2 Tbs vegetable oil

Kosher salt

Ground black pepper

1 lime, cut into wedges

Instructions:

In a medium bowl, combine butter, chipotle chiles, garlic, and 1/2 tsp salt. Mix well.

Prepare the grill for direct cooking over high heat (450–550°F).

Brush salmon filets generously on both sides with oil. Season evenly with salt and pepper.

Grill salmon skin side up, with the lid closed, 6–8 minutes or until they lift easily from the grate.

Turn filets over and cook 2–3 minutes more, to desired doneness.

Slide a spatula between the skin and flesh and remove salmon from grill.

Top each filet with chipotle butter and serve warm with lime wedges.
