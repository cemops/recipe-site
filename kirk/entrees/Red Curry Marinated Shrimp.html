Red Curry Marinated Shrimp


Ingredients

1 (14 - ounce) can unsweetened coconut milk
2 Tbs red curry paste
1/2 red onion, finely chopped
1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
1 lime, juiced
2 - 3 Tbs canola oil
Salt and freshly ground black pepper
2 Lbs shrimp, shell and tail left on

Instructions

Heat grill to high
Whisk together coconut milk, curry paste, onion, cilantro, lime juice, and oil in a large bowl
Season to taste with salt and pepper
Place shrimp in the bowl and toss to coat, cover and let marinate at room temp for 20 min.
Remove shrimp from marinade and grill 1 1/2 to 2 min per side
