Ultimate Barbecued Chicken


Brine:

2 Quarts water

2 Tbs kosher salt

1/4 Cup brown sugar

2 garlic cloves, smashed

4 Sprigs fresh thyme

6 Chicken Legs and Thighs, still connected, bone in, skin on, about 10 oz

Ultimate Barbecue Sauce:

1 Slice Bacon

1 Bunch fresh thyme

EVOO

1/2 Onion, chopped

2 Garlic cloves, chopped

2 Cups ketchup

1/4 Cup brown sugar

1/4 Cup molasses

2 Tbs red or white wine vinegar

1 Tbs dry mustard

1 tsp ground cumin

1 tsp paprika

Freshly ground black pepper

Instructions:

- For the brine, in a mixing bowl combine the water, salt, sugar, garlic, and thyme.

- Transfer the brine to a 2-gallon sized re-sealable plastic bag. Add the chicken, close the bag and refrigerate 2 hours to allow the salt and seasonings to penetrate the chicken.

- Meanwhile, make the sauce. Wrap the bacon around the bunch of thyme and tie with kitchen twine so you have a nice bundle.

- Heat 2 Tbs of oil in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add the thyme and cook slowly 3 to 4 min. to render the bacon fat and give the sauce a nice smoky taste.

- Add the onion and garlic and cook slowly without coloring for 5 min. Add the remaining ingredients, give the sauce a stir, and turn the heat down to low.

- Cook slowly for 20 min. to meld the flavors. Once the sauce is done cooking, remove about 1 1/2 Cups of the sauce and reserve for serving alongside the chicken at the table. The rest of the barbecue sauce will be used for basting the legs.

- Preheat outer burners of the grill to medium heat.

- Arrange the chicken pieces on the preheated grill and cook, turn once mid-way, and cook for a total of 10 min.

- Transfer the chicken to a double-foil base on the indirect heat section of the grill. Cook the chicken for 15 min.

- Brush liberally, coating every inch of the legs with the barbecue sauce and then cook for 25 - 30 more min, basting the chicken for a second time halfway through, serve with extra sauce.
