Beef Kabobs with Corn and Orzo Salad


Ingredients:

1 Recipe Corn and Orzo Salad

4 tsp chili powder

2 tsp garlic salt

1 tsp ground cumin

1 tsp oregano

1 lb boneless beef sirloin, trimmed and cut into 1-inch pieces

Lime - Optional

Fresh Cilantro sprigs - Optional

Corn and Orzo Salad:

2/3 Cup orzo pasta

2 Cups corn

15 oz can of kidney beans

1 small zucchini, halved lengthwise and sliced

1 medium orange or red pepper, cut into bite-size pieces

1/2 cup tomatoes

1/2 cup sliced red onion

Dressing:

1 tsp lime zest

1/4 cup lime juice

2 Tbs snipped fresh cilantro

2 Tbs olive oil

2 Tbs honey

1 fresh jalapeño, seeded and finely chopped

3 cloves garlic, minced

1/2 tsp salt

Directions:

- Prepare Corn and Orzo Salad, Chill.

- If using wooden skewers, soak in water 30 min. In a resealable bag combine next four ingredients. Add meat, shaking to coat. Thread onto skewers.

- Grill meat skewers, uncovered, over medium heat 8 to 12 min.

- To serve, arrange Corn and Orzo Salad on a large platter. Top with meat skewers and, if desired, lime slices and cilantro.

Corn and Orzo Salad Instructions:

- Cook 2/3 Cup orzo pasta according to package directions, adding 2 Cups corn the last minute of cooking. Drain in a colander, rinse with cold water, drain again.

- In a large bowl combine orzo mixture: 15 oz can of kidney beans, 1 small zucchini (halved lengthwise and sliced), 1 medium orange or red pepper, cut into bite-size pieces, 1/2 cup tomatoes, 1/2 cup sliced red onion.

Dressing Instructions:

- Combine 1 tsp lime zest, 1/4 cup lime juice, 2 Tbs each snipped fresh cilantro, olive oil, and honey, 1 fresh jalapeño, seeded and finely chopped, 3 cloves garlic, minced, and 1/2 tsp salt.
