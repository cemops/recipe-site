Curried Beef Kababs


Ingredients:

4 Large Scallions

1 tsp Canola Oil

1/2 tsp Garlic

2 tsp Curry Powder

1 TBS Cayenne Pepper

1/8 tsp Salt

12 oz Beef Top Sirloin

Instructions:

- Preheat grill.

- Combine oil, garlic, curry powder, and cayenne pepper in a large bowl; add beef and scallions.

- Alternate beef and scallion pieces on four skewers.

- Grill 8 - 10 minutes, turning occasionally, for medium, less for rare, more for well done.

- Season to taste with salt and serve.
