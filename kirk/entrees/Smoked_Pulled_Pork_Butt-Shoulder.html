Smoked Pulled Pork Butt / Shoulder

Two Methods: Last Time & Next Time


Ingredients — Last Time

4–6 lb bone-in pork shoulder roast (Boston Butt)
3 tbsp yellow mustard
1/4 cup BBQ Sweet Rub (Evernote)
1 tbsp BBQ Sweet Rub (Evernote)

Instructions — Last Time

Preheat smoker to 225°F.
Remove roast from package; pat dry thoroughly.
Coat entire pork shoulder with yellow mustard.
Season all sides with the rub.
Place pork fat-side up in the center of the smoker grate.
Smoke until internal temp reaches 195–205°F (15–20 hours).
Remove pork, wrap tightly in foil, and rest for 1 hour.
Pull pork, discarding fat and gristle; sprinkle remaining rub and serve.
To reheat: Add 2 tbsp water, cover, bake at 350°F for 30 min.

Ingredients — Next Time

For the Rub:

3 tbsp sea salt
2 tbsp black pepper
2 tbsp granulated garlic
2 tbsp granulated onion
2 tbsp paprika
2 tbsp cumin

Spritzer / Sauce:

1 cup apple juice
1/2 cup apple cider vinegar
1/2 cup water

For the Pork:

6–9 lb pork shoulder or butt, fat trimmed
4 tbsp yellow or Dijon mustard

Instructions — Next Time

Preheat smoker to 250°F.
Rub mustard on all sides of pork.
Season generously with rub.
Smoke pork for 3 hours.
Spritz pork every hour for the next 3 hours.
At 165–170°F internal temp (~6–7 hours), double-wrap tightly in foil.
Return to smoker bone-side up; cook to 200–205°F (2–4 hours).
Rest 60 minutes in foil (inside cooler optional).
Pull pork, discard large fat pieces, remove bone.

Chef’s Notes

Reheat at 325°F for 30 min with a little beef or chicken stock.
Important: Ensure the top pork skin has split before wrapping.
