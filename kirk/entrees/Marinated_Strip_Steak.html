Marinated Strip Steaks

With Gorgonzola Sauce · Serves 4


Ingredients

Marinade

2 cups beef broth
1 cup dry red wine
1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
2 tbsp tomato paste

Steaks

4 New York strip steaks
Extra-virgin olive oil
Kosher salt
Freshly ground black pepper

Gorgonzola Sauce

1 tbsp unsalted butter
2 tbsp minced shallot
1 cup sour cream
4 oz crumbled gorgonzola cheese (about 1 cup)

Instructions

In a large glass baking dish, whisk together beef broth, red wine, onion, and tomato paste until smooth.
Submerge steaks in the marinade, cover, and refrigerate for 2–4 hours.
Preheat grill to 450–550°F.
Remove steaks from marinade, pat dry, and discard marinade.
Lightly brush steaks with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and let rest at room temperature for 15–30 minutes.
Meanwhile, melt butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add shallot and cook until tender, about 2 minutes.
Stir in sour cream and gorgonzola; cook until the sauce begins to simmer and cheese is melted, about 3 minutes.
Remove sauce from heat, add ¼ tsp black pepper, cover, and keep warm.
Grill steaks to desired doneness. Rest briefly, then serve topped with warm gorgonzola sauce.
