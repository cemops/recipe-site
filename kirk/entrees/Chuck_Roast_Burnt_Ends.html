Chuck Roast Burnt Ends


Ingredients:

1 Chuck roast, approx. 3 lbs
Brisket rub
1 Cup BBQ sauce (recommend Kansas City style)
1/2 Cup Beef broth

Directions:

Cut chuck roast into 1" x 1" cubes. Season with rub and arrange on a rack with space between each piece.
Dry brine overnight in the refrigerator.
Remove racks at least 2 hours before cooking to reach room temperature.
Preheat smoker to 275°F and add fruity wood for smoke flavor.
Place racks in the smoker and cook for 2 hours.
Remove burnt ends from rack and arrange in a deep aluminum pan.
Mix BBQ sauce and beef broth, then pour over burnt ends evenly.
Cover pan with foil and return to smoker. Continue cooking for 2 hours or until burnt ends are tender.
