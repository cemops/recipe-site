Harissa Chicken Skewers - Blue Apron


Ingredients

Makes: 2 Servings
Prep Time: 10 Min.   Cook Time: 35 - 45 min

2 Boneless, Skinless Chicken Breasts
8 Wooden Skewers
3 oz Semi-Pearled Khorasan Wheat
2 Cloves Garlic
1 Persimmon
1/2 Bunch Kale
2 Tbs Red Wine Vinegar
1 Tbs Red Harissa Paste
1/4 Cup Labneh Cheese

Instructions

Cook the Khorasan Wheat:
Heat a medium pot of salted water to boiling on high.
Once boiling, add the Khorasan wheat and cook, uncovered, 16 - 18 min, or until tender.
Drain thoroughly and set aside in a warm place.
Prepare the Ingredients:
Peel and mince the garlic.
Remove and discard the kale stems; roughly chop the leaves.
Core and small dice the persimmon.
Cut each chicken breast lengthwise into 4 equal-sized strips; transfer to a medium bowl.
Assemble the Chicken Skewers:
To the bowl of chicken strips add: 1/4 of the labneh, as much of the harissa paste as you'd like, season with salt and pepper.
Toss to thoroughly coat.
Thread the seasoned chicken lengthwise onto the skewers.
Transfer onto a plate.
Cook The Chicken Skewers:
In a large (nonstick) heat 1 Tbs of olive oil on medium-high until hot.
Add the chicken skewers and cook 3 - 4 minutes per side, or until browned and cooked through.
Leave the pan.....
Make the Khorasan Wheat salad:
Add 1 Tbs of olive oil to the pan of reserved fond and heat on medium-high until hot.
Add the garlic and cook, stirring occasionally, 30 seconds to 1 minute, or until fragrant.
Add the kale; season with salt and pepper. Cook stirring occasionally, 30 seconds to 1 min, or until slightly wilted.
Add the vinegar and 1/2 cup water (be careful the water may splatter) Cook, stirring occasionally 4 - 5 min, or until the kale has wilted and most of the liquid has cooked off.
Add the persimmon and cooked khorasan wheat. Cook, stirring occasionally, 1 - 2 min. or until thoroughly combined and the persimmon is slightly softened. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
Season the Labneh and Plate your Dish:
Place the remaining labneh in a bowl; season with salt and pepper. Stir to incorporate.
Divide the salad and chicken and serve with the labneh on the side (and remaining harissa paste).
