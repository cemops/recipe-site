Teriyaki Tilapia with Herb Salad


Ingredients:

1 cup brown rice

4 6-ounce tilapia fillets

1/2 cup teriyaki sauce

1/4 cup fresh cilantro leaves

1/4 cup fresh mint leaves

2 scallions, thinly sliced

1 tablespoon fresh lime juice

1 teaspoon crushed red pepper

Directions:

1. Cook the rice according to the package directions.

2. Heat broiler. Place the tilapia on a baking sheet lined with foil and broil, basting occasionally with the teriyaki sauce, until cooked through, 7 to 9 minutes. Break into pieces.

3. Meanwhile, in a small bowl, combine the cilantro, mint, scallions, and lime juice.

4. Serve the tilapia over the rice and top with the herb salad and crushed red pepper.
