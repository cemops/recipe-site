Cajun Parmesan Salmon


Ingredients:

4 - 4 oz Salmon Fillets

1 Tbs Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO)

2 tsp Cajun Seasoning

Fresh Ground Black Pepper

2 Tbs Butter

3 Cloves Garlic, minced

1/3 Cup low-sodium Chicken Broth

Juice of 1 Lemon

1 Tbs Honey

1 Tbs freshly chopped Parsley

2 Tbs freshly chopped Parmesan Cheese

Lemon Slices, for serving

Instructions:

- Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

- Season salmon with 1 tsp Cajun Seasoning and pepper, then add to skillet, skin-side up. Cook until golden, about 6 minutes, then flip and cook for another 2 minutes. Transfer to a plate.

- Add butter and garlic to the skillet. When the butter has melted, stir in broth, lemon juice, honey, and the remaining 1 tsp Cajun Seasoning, parsley, and Parmesan.

- Bring the mixture to a simmer, reduce heat to medium, and add salmon back to the skillet. Simmer for 3-4 minutes until sauce reduces and salmon is cooked through.

- Add lemon slices to skillet and serve.
