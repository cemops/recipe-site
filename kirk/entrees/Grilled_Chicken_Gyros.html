Grilled Chicken Gyros


Ingredients:

Chicken and Marinade:

1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs

¼ cup olive oil

2 TBS lemon juice

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 tsp dried oregano

Salt and pepper, to taste

Tzatziki Sauce:
1 cup Greek yogurt

½ cucumber, grated

2 cloves garlic, minced

1 TBS lemon juice

1 TBS olive oil

Salt and pepper, to taste

Pita Bread Filling:
4 pita breads

1 small red onion, thinly sliced

1 tomato, chopped

¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions:

1. Marinate the chicken: Combine olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Add the chicken and marinate for at least 30 minutes.

2. Preheat grill or skillet. Cook chicken thighs 5-7 minutes per side. Let chicken rest and cool, then cut into thin slices.

3. Warm the pita breads and spread each with tzatziki sauce.

4. Add the sliced chicken, red onion, tomato, and parsley.

5. Roll up the gyros and serve immediately.
