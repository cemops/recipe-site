Bruschetta Chicken


Ingredients:

Boneless skinless chicken breasts, pounded to 1 1/2 inches

Roma Tomato, diced

Minced Shallot

Minced Garlic

Olive Oil

Balsamic Vinegar

Chopped Fresh Basil

Salt / Pepper

Shredded Mozzarella Cheese

Instructions:

- Marinate the chicken in olive oil, balsamic vinegar, minced garlic, salt, and pepper for 1 - 4 hours.

- Make bruschetta by combining diced Roma tomato, minced shallot, minced garlic, olive oil, balsamic vinegar, and chopped fresh basil.

- Preheat oven broiler.

- Fry the chicken in a skillet until fully cooked.

- Assemble the dish by placing bruschetta on top of the chicken, followed by shredded mozzarella cheese.

- Place the assembled chicken on serving plates and broil to melt the cheese.

- Serve and enjoy!
