Turkey & Veggie Bake


Ingredients:

2 Cups Fresh Mushrooms - sliced

3/4 Cup Red or Yellow Pepper, Chopped

1/2 Cup Onion, Chopped

2 Cloves Garlic, Minced

2 TBS Margarine or Veg Oil Spread

1/4 Cup Flour

3/4 tsp Salt

1/2 tsp dried thyme

1/4 tsp black pepper

2 Cups fat-free Milk

1 - 10 oz package frozen chopped spinach, thawed and well drained

2 Cups Cooked brown or white rice

2 Cups Cooked Turkey or Chicken

1/2 Cup Finely Shredded Parm Cheese

Instructions:

- In a 12 in skillet cook and stir mushrooms, sweet peppers, onion and garlic in hot butter or oil over medium heat until tender.

- Stir in flour, salt, thyme, and black pepper. Slowly stir in milk; cook and stir until thickened and bubbly. Stir in spinach, rice, turkey and 1/4 Cup of the Parm cheese.

- Spoon mixture into a 2-quart rectangular baking dish. Sprinkle with remaining Parm Cheese. Bake, covered, in a 350 degrees F oven for 20 min. Uncover and bake about 10 min more or until heated through. Let stand 15 min before serving.
