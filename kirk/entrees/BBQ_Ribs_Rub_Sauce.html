BBQ Ribs, Rub and Sauce


Ingredients:

RUB:
1/4 Cup Spanish Paprika
1/2 Tbs Chili Powder
1 Tbs Cayenne Pepper
1/8 Cup Black Pepper
1/2 Tbs Smoked Paprika
1/2 Tbs packed Brown Sugar
1 Tbs Oregano
1 Tbs Thyme
2 Tbs Kosher Salt
1/8 Cup Garlic Powder

MEMPHIS BLUES BBQ SAUCE:
1/2 10 - oz can tomato paste
1/4 Cup packed Brown Sugar
1/4 Cup Molasses
1/8 Cup Honey
1/8 Cup Soy Sauce
1/4 Cup French’s mustard
1 1/2 Tbs Garlic Powder
1 1/2 Tbs Onion Powder
1/8 Cup Worcestershire Sauce
1/8 Cup White Vinegar
1/2 Cup Ketchup
1 1/2 Tbs Hot Sauce
1/2 tsp Salt
1 Cup Water

Instructions:

RUB:
Sprinkle with Rub and marinate over night

MEMPHIS BLUES BBQ SAUCE:
Mix ingredients in a pot and cook over low heat, whisking thoroughly until smooth. Simmer for 30 min, careful not to burn it. Store in refrigerator for one week.

SMOKE:
Prepare approx 4 cups of chips (3/4 Hickory to 1/4 Apple or Cherry)
Soak the wood chips for a min. of 1 hour prior to smoking
3 Hours
Smoke bone down at 220 - 250 degrees
Baste with (1/2 apple juice and 1/2 apple cider vinegar) every 20 min after the first hour
2 Hours
Wrap ribs with foil, meat side down, with 3 Tbs butter for 2 hours
1 Hour
Remove ribs from wrap and cook until done..... 190 degrees
Can add sauce at this point ..... but VERY lightly
