Grilled Veggies Mediterranean Style


Ingredients

3-4 spring onions
2 peppers
1 zucchini
1 Eggplant
2 red onions
5 tomatoes
Olive oil
Don Marcos Tuscany spice mixture (TIP: Use McCormick seasoning for the Don Marco's if you live in the U.S.!)

Instructions

Cut the vegetables into small pieces and place in a medium sized bowl. Place the tomatoes aside.
Add a few tablespoons of olive oil to the Tuscany seasoning; there should be enough oil so that it is more like a basting sauce.
Coat the raw vegetables with the olive oil seasoning mixture; be sure to coat all the vegetables completely.
Add the tomatoes a few minutes before the mixture is done as they cook very fast!
Using a grilling basket cook over direct heat, covered until desired doneness is reached. Enjoy!
