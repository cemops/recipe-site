Spinach, Artichoke and Brie Crepes w/ Sweet Honey Sauce


Ingredients

WHOLE WHEAT CREPES:

2 Large Eggs
3/4 Cup Milk
1 Cup Flour
3 TBS Butter, Melted
1/4 tsp Salt
1/4 Cup Water
FILLING:
2 TBS Olive Oil
2 Cloves Garlic, Minced
1/4 tsp Salt
1/2 tsp Pepper
1/2 tsp Crushed Red Pepper ( optional )
1 (8 oz) bag Fresh Spinach
1 (12 oz ) Jar Marinated Artichoke Hearts, Drained and Chopped
1/4 Cup Parmesan Cheese Finely Grated
1 ( 8 oz ) Brie Wheel cut into Slices
** Add some roasted chopped chicken for some protein **
SWEET HONEY SAUCE:
1/4 Cup Olive Oil
3 TBS Honey
1/4 Cup Fresh Parsley, Chopped

Instructions

Start by making the crepes. Add all of the ingredients to a bowl and use an immersion blender to blend until smooth. Set aside for 30 min to allow the air bubbles to subside.
Heat large pan, add cooking spray to coat. Pour 1/3 Cup of batter into the center of the pan and swirl to spread evenly. Cook for 30 sec. and flip. Cook for another 10 sec. and remove to cool.
Heat a large skillet over medium heat and add 1 TBS of olive oil and garlic. Add the salt, pepper and crushed red pepper, if using, and cook for 2 min. Stir in Spinach and artichokes, cooking 5 - 6 minutes until spinach is fully wilted. Reduce heat to low and stir in parmesan cheese, then turn off heat.
Whisk together the olive oil and honey. Place in a small sauce pot and worm through. Keep warm until ready to use.
Wipe the skillet clean and heat over medium - low heat. Law a few slices of brie on one quarter of the crepe (basically make a triangle). Layer on the spinach and artichoke filling, chicken and another layer of brie.
Fold over the bottom of the crepe and over again to make a triangle. Cook for 2 - 3 min and flip and cook for another 2 - 3 min or until the brie is melty and gooey.
Remove the honey sauce from the heat and stir in the chopped parsley. Drizzle the crepe with the warm honey sauce and dig in!
