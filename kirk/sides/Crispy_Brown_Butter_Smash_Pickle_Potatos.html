Crispy Brown Butter Smashed Pickle Potatoes

Full Version & Weekday Dinner Version


Full Version – 3 servings (2 dinners + 1 leftover lunch)

Ingredients

1½ lbs red potatoes (18–22 small)
4 tbsp unsalted butter
¾ tsp kosher salt (+ extra for boiling)
½ tsp black pepper
½ tsp garlic powder
½ cup freshly shredded sharp cheddar cheese
½ cup finely chopped dill pickles
1 tbsp pickle juice
2 tbsp sour cream or Greek yogurt
1–2 tbsp fresh chopped dill (or 1 tsp dried)
Optional: finely chopped red onion
Optional: pinch crushed red pepper
Optional: small squeeze of lemon for brown butter

Instructions

Boil potatoes in salted water 15–20 min until fork-tender. Drain and steam dry 5–10 min. Pat dry.
Preheat oven 425°F, line sheet pan with parchment, preheat pan 5 min.
Place potatoes on hot pan, smash to ~¼ inch, wait 30 sec, press gently again.
Sprinkle with salt, pepper, garlic powder.
Melt butter until foaming stops and solids turn golden. Remove from heat, stir in dill and optional lemon.
Brush half the brown butter on potatoes, roast 20 min. Flip, brush remaining butter, roast 5–10 min until golden and crispy.
Sprinkle cheddar, broil 1–2 min until bubbly and lightly browned.
Mix sour cream, pickle juice, remaining dill. Spoon over potatoes, sprinkle pickles, optional onion or crushed red pepper.
Leftover tip: reheat 400°F or in air fryer 8–10 min. Add fresh pickle topping after reheating.

Weekday Dinner Version – 2 servings

Ingredients

4 medium red potatoes
3 tbsp unsalted butter
½ tsp kosher salt (+ extra for boiling)
¼ tsp black pepper
¼ tsp garlic powder
⅓ cup freshly shredded sharp cheddar cheese
⅓ cup finely chopped dill pickles
2 tsp pickle juice
1½ tbsp sour cream or Greek yogurt
1 tbsp fresh chopped dill
Optional: small squeeze of lemon for brown butter

Instructions

Same as full version, using smaller quantities. Roast times slightly shorter (~20–25 min). Broil cheese 1–2 min.
