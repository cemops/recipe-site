Rice Pilaf


Ingredients:

2 TBS Unsalted Butter
1/2 Cup Onion, finely chopped
3 Cloves Garlic, minced
1 tsp Kosher Salt
1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
1 1/2 Cups long-grain white rice
3 Cups Low Sodium Chicken Broth
1/4 Cup Parsley, chopped

Directions:

Rinse rice thoroughly until water runs clear. Drain well.
Melt butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Add rice and cook, stirring, until lightly golden.
Add onion, garlic, salt, and pepper. Cook until onion has softened.
Add chicken broth and bring to a boil. Cover with a tight lid and reduce heat to a simmer. Do not uncover while cooking.
Simmer for 18 minutes. Remove from heat and let rest (covered) for 10 minutes.
Fluff rice with a fork and gently stir in chopped parsley.





Notes:

1. Yields about 4½ cups of rice. Serving size: ¾ cup.
2. Brown Rice Pilaf: Use ½ cup more broth and cook for about 40 minutes.
3. Orzo Rice Pilaf: Use 1 cup rice + ½ cup orzo.
4. Vegetable Rice Pilaf: Add celery, bell pepper, carrots, peas, and use vegetable broth.
5. Leftover Ideas: Great in Chicken & Rice Enchiladas, Crispy Southwest Wraps, Creamy Wild Rice Soup, Breakfast Fried Rice, Salmon Bowls, Arroz Chaufa, or Eggroll in a Bowl.
