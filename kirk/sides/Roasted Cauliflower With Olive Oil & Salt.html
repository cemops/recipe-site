Roasted Cauliflower With Olive Oil & Salt


Ingredients

1 Large Head Cauliflower - Chopped
5 Cloves Garlic - Thinly Sliced
4 - 5 Sprigs Fresh Rosemary
Sea Salt
Cracked Pepper
EVOO
Balsamic Vinegar
Feta Cheese

Instructions

Preheat oven to 375 degrees
Combine all ingredients EXCEPT Feta cheese in a covered baking dish
Bake covered until the cauliflower is soft, about 40 min.  then uncover until it browns, about 10 min.
Remove, plate and cover with sauce in baking dish and feta cheese
Garnish with fresh rosemary and cherry tomato
