Pasta Limone

Serves 4


Ingredients

12 oz spaghetti, linguine, or bucatini (whole wheat)
2 leeks, thinly sliced
2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
Zest and juice of 1 lemon (try 1/2 lemon juice)
3 sprigs basil
1 tsp kosher salt (cut back if desired)
3/4 tsp black pepper
1 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Instructions

Combine pasta, 4 1/2 cups water, leeks, garlic, lemon zest, basil, salt, and pepper in a large skillet with 2-inch sides.
Bring water to a boil and cook, stirring occasionally, until water is nearly gone and pasta is fully cooked (8–10 min).
Add lemon juice and Parmesan, tossing to combine.
Season with additional salt and pepper to taste.
Garnish with basil leaves and lemon zest.

Serving Notes

Good with lemon pepper grilled chicken tenders.
Also great with garlic butter shrimp and black olives.
