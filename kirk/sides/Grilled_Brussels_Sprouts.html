Grilled Brussels Sprout Skewers


Ingredients:

1 pound Brussels sprouts, uniform in size
1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
1 tablespoon minced garlic
1 tablespoon soy sauce or tamari (for gluten free)
1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
Wood or metal skewers

Directions:

Soak wooden skewers in water for at least 1 hour, if using.
Trim Brussels sprouts and remove outer leaves. Microwave 3 min or blanch and ice-bath. Dry well.
Preheat grill to medium heat (~375°F) or use grill pan/broiler.
Toss sprouts with oil, garlic, soy sauce, balsamic, mustard, salt, and pepper.
Thread 4–5 Brussels sprouts per skewer, stem ends same direction, spacing 1/2-inch apart. Group similar sizes.
Grill skewers, covered, for 5 min. Flip and grill another 5 min, until browned and tender.
Remove from skewers, toss with reserved sauce, and serve warm.
