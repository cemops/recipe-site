Grits


Ingredients

1/2 Cup REAL Grits
1 Cup Seafood or Chicken Stock
1 Cup Milk (prefer whole milk)
1 tsp Smoked Paprika
1/2 Cup Shredded Parmesian

Instructions

Combine the broth, milk and paprika in a medium pot with a lid and bring to a boil. Gradually pour in the grits and stir vigorously.
Reduce heat to medium low, so the grits cook at a low boil. Keep the pot covered except to stir periodically to prevent clumping.
Remove from the heat when the grits are the desired consistency, about 6 - 8 min.
Add Parm. Cheese in 3 Parts
