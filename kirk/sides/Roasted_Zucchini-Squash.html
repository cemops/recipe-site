Oven Roasted Zucchini and Squash

A quick and healthy side dish that pairs perfectly with grilled meats or savory mains.


Ingredients:

2 large zucchini
2 large yellow squash
2 tsp garlic powder
½ tsp ground black pepper
Salt (to taste)
Grated Parmesan cheese (to taste)
Bread crumbs (to taste)
Olive oil

Directions:

Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
Slice zucchini and squash into ¼-inch rounds.
In a large bowl, toss slices with olive oil, garlic powder, and pepper.
Spread slices on a baking sheet in a single layer.
Sprinkle with Parmesan cheese and breadcrumbs.
Roast for 9–10 minutes until tender.
Broil for 2–3 minutes until golden and slightly crisp.
