Roasted Broccoli, Kale, Tomato


Ingredients

1 1/2 Containers of Kale - Stems removed and roughly chopped
1 Bag Broccoli Florets
2 Roma Tomatoes - Chopped
2 Cloves Garlic - Minced
Salt & Pepper
Parmesan Cheese

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425°
Toss kale, broccoli, tomato, garlic, olive oil, salt & pepper in a large bowl
Line a large cooking sheet and spray with oil
Layer the mixture on the sheet, the kale can be mounded slightly
Cook for 20 min.
