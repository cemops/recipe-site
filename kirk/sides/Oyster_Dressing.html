Oyster Dressing


Ingredients:

1 Pkg Pepperidge Farm Herb Season (14 oz)

1/4 Cup Unsalted Butter

3/4 Stick Butter

1 Cup Onion

1 Cup Celery

2 Cups Chicken Broth

1 Pint Oysters (4 Cups) processed

Celery Leaves

Salt

Pepper

Oregano

Instructions:

- Sauté: 1/4 cup unsalted butter, 1 cup celery, 1 cup onion

- Heat: 2 cups chicken broth, 3/4 stick butter

- Place the dressing in a large bowl. Add the oysters, sauté, and broth mixture and mix lightly.

- Add the celery leaves, salt, pepper, and oregano and complete mixing.

- Place in the 8 x 11.5 baking dish.

- ** Last time I poured some of the oyster juice around the edges and on top.

- Preheat oven to 350°F. Cover and cook 1 hour.
