Tomato Provencale


Ingredients

Medium - Large Firm Ripe Tomatoes x 4 (try using vine ripe tomatoes)
Bread Crumbs - 4 Heaping TBS Whole Wheat
Olive Oil - 4 TBS
2 - Garlic Cloves, Crushed
3 TBS Finely Chopped Flat Leafed Parsley
1 TBS Finely Chopped Oregano
2 - 3 Basil Leaves to Finish

Instructions

Halve the tomatoes. Using a grapefruit spoon, scoop out the flesh and seed of the tomato. Sprinkle with salt and place upside down on paper towels for at least 15 min.
Preheat the oven at 375 degrees. In a bowl, combine the bread crumbs, oil, garlic, and herbs. Season lightly.
Using the teaspoon, spread the mixture into the tomatoes, patting it in evenly.
Place the tomatoes in a gratin dish large enough to accommodate them side by side, and still leave a little space between them.
Sssssssssssx.    A for 25 - 30 min, or until the tomatoes are tender but still hold their shape and the filling is golden. Garnish with snipped basil leaves and serve warm or at warm temp.
