Sea Island Red Peas w/ Celery Leaf Salad


Ingredients

2 Cups dried Sea Island Red Peas or Black Eyed Peas
1 tsp kosher salt
2 cloves garlic
6 sprigs thyme
1 fresh bay leaf
1/4 Cup EVOO
1/2 Cup finely chopped celery
1/2 Cup celery leaves from inner leaves
1/2 Cup finely chopped onion
1/2 Cup finely chopped red bell pepper
1 TBS unsalted butter
1 tsp coriander seeds
1/2 Cup flat leaf parsley leaves
2 TBS thinly sliced chives
2 TBS finely grated lemon zest
Fresh cracked black pepper

Instructions

Place peas in a medium pt; cover with 6 cups water; let soak for 3 hours or overnight
Bring water with peas to a boil (do not drain); boil for 15 min.
Reduce heat to medium; gently simmer, adding water by 1/2 cupfuls as needed to keep peas covered, until peas are tender but still holding their shape, about 20 - 25
Remove from heat and stir in 1 tsp salt. DO AHEAD: Can be made 1 day ahead. Cover and chill. Rewarm before continuing, adding more water by 1/4 cupfuls if too thick.
Mince garlic w/ some salt into a coarse paste. Tie thyme and bay leave with kitchen twine to form bouquet garni.
Heat 1/4 Cup oil in a medium pot over medium heat
