Mediterranean Grit Cakes


Ingredients

GRITS:

1 Cup Grits
4 Cups Chicken Broth
2 tsp Garlic Powder
2 tsp Onion Powder
2 tsp Sea Salt
1/2 tsp White Pepper

TOPPINGS:

2 Whole Roasted Red Peppers - Cut Into Thin Slices
1 Avocado
1/2 Cup Roma Tomato - Finely Chopped
1/2 Cup Onion - Finely Chopped (I fried these in a pan w/ EVOO and butter to sweeten them up a little)
2 Cloves Garlic
1/2 tsp Salt
1 Lime - juiced
Cilantro

Instructions

Grits:

Boil the chicken broth, slowly add the grits stirring constantly. Add the remaining grits ingredients, lower heat and simmer 20 - 25 min or until grits are done.
Pour the grits into a greased pan and refrigerate for at least 1 hour to set.

Toppings:

Scoop avocado into a bow: add garlic, salt and lime juice and blend to a chuncky consistency
Saute onions in EVOO and butter until soft

Pan fry the Grit Cakes until golden
Remove from pan and add Red Peppers, Avocado Mixture, Onions, Tomato, and Cilantro
Drizzle with Balsamic Vinegar and serve immediately
