Brussel Sprouts


Ingredients

1/2 lb bacon, finely diced
1 medium yellow onion, diced
2 cloves garlic, minced
2 pounds Brussels sprouts, trimmed
Salt & Pepper
2 cups Chicken Broth
4 Tbsp butter

Instructions

In a heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat, fry the bacon until crisp. Remove the bacon and drain on paper towels.
Saute the onion and garlic in the bacon fat over low heat until soft, about 3 min. Add the Brussels sprouts and stir them around so that they are coated with the bacon fat. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
Add the broth and cook, covered, over low heat until the sprouts are easily pierced with a fork, about 12 to 15 min. Stir in the butter and transfer the sprouts to a serving dish. Garnish with bacon bits and serve.
