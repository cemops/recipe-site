Garlic-Chive Grilled Red Potatoes


Ingredients

6 red potatoes, medium sized
1/4 cup chopped fresh chives (can sub w/ green onion)
2 tablespoons olive oil
1 tablespoon butter
1/2 cup parmigiano-reggiano cheese, fresh grated
2 tablespoons garlic, minced
1 tablespoon kosher salt
1 teaspoon black pepper, fresh ground

Instructions

Slice potatoes in 1/4 inch slices. I used a serrated potato slicer, but you can use a regular knife if necessary.
Mix all ingredients together in a bowl.
Spoon into 16 inch aluminum foil strips. Fold and seal into packets individually.
Grill on medium heat 20-25 minutes, until cooked.
