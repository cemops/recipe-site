Spinach Artichoke Dip - Slow Cooker


Ingredients:

1 - 10 oz bag of fresh baby spinach, roughly chopped

1 - 13 oz can of quartered artichoke hearts, chopped and drained

1 - 8 oz brick of low fat cream cheese, cut into 1" cubes

1 cup light sour cream

1 cup shredded Mozzarella cheese

1/2 cup grated Parmesan

1/3 cup white onion, finely chopped

4 cloves garlic, minced

1/2 teaspoon black pepper

1/4 teaspoon sea salt

3 quart slow cooker

Instructions:

1. Combine all ingredients into a large mixing bowl and toss briefly.

2. Add to crock pot and cook on high for 45 min, then back down to low for 45 min.

3. Check and stir every so often so it doesn't overcook.
