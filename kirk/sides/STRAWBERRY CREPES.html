Strawberry Crepes


Ingredients

2 Cups Frozen Strawberries, thawed
2 TBS Sugar
1/2 tsp Orange Zest, Optional
3 Cups Fresh Strawberries, sliced
2 Large Eggs
2 TBS Butter, Melted and Slightly Cooled
2 Cups Milk
1 tsp Vanilla
1 TBS Sugar
1/2 tsp Salt
1 1/2 Cups All Purpose Flour

Instructions

STRAWBERRY FILLING:

Gently puree thawed strawberries. Stir in sugar, orange zest and freshly sliced strawberries. Serve strawberry filling at room temp.
CREPES:
Add ingredients to bowl and use an emulsion blender to blend until smooth.
Heat an 8" skillet over medium heat and lightly coat with cooking spray.
Pour 1/4 cup of batter in the center of the skillet and rotate to cover the bottom of the skillet in a thin layer.
Cook until light brown and the top begins to dry out, approximately 1 min.
Flip and cook and additional 30 seconds.
Repeat with remaining batter. Stack completed crepes on a plate. If the crepes are sticking together, put wax paper between the crepes. You can put crepes in a 200 degree oven to keep warm until ready to serve.
Fill each crepe with strawberry filling and roll up. Serve with whipped cream.
