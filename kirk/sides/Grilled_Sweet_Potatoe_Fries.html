Grilled Sweet Potato Wedges


Ingredients:

1 1/2 teaspoons salt, plus more for seasoning water

2 large sweet potatoes, washed

2 tablespoons olive oil

3 tablespoons brown sugar

1 teaspoon cayenne pepper, plus more if desired

1 1/2 teaspoons freshly grated black pepper

1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

1/4 teaspoon ground cloves

Instructions:

Preheat grill to medium-high heat and oil the grates.

In a large pot, add sweet potatoes and cover with cold salted water. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer 25–30 minutes until just tender.

Transfer potatoes to a bowl of cold water to stop cooking. Once cool, drain well and peel.

Cut each potato lengthwise into about 6 thick wedges.

Toss wedges with olive oil, brown sugar, cayenne, salt, pepper, cinnamon, and cloves.

Grill wedges about 3–4 minutes per side, until caramelized and heated through.

Serve as-is or with mint and cumin aioli, if desired.
