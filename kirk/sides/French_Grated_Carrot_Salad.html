French Grated Carrot Salad


Ingredients:

1 lb carrots, peeled

2 tsp Dijon mustard

1 tbsp freshly squeezed lemon juice

1 1/2 tbsp vegetable oil

1 1/2 tbsp olive oil

1-2 tsp honey

1/4 heaping tsp salt

1/4 tsp pepper

2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

2 scallions, thinly sliced

Instructions:

Grate the carrots and set aside.

In a salad bowl, combine Dijon mustard, lemon juice, honey, vegetable oil, olive oil, salt, and pepper.

Add grated carrots, fresh parsley, and scallions to the bowl and toss well.

Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.

Check seasoning before serving as flavors may mellow.
