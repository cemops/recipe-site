Pumpkin Packages with Chile & Lime


Ingredients

1 lb 9 oz pumpkin or squash
2 Tbs canola oil
2 Tbs Butter
1/2 tsp chili sauce
grated rind of 1 lime
2 tsp lime juice

Instructions

Halve the pumpkin and scoop out the seeds. Rinse the seeds and reserve
Peel and cut into wedges.
Heat the oil and butter together in a large pan. Stir in the chili sauce, lime rind and juice.
Stir in the pumpkin and seeds, toss to coat on all sides.
Divide the mixture among 4 double - thickness sheets of aluminum foil. Fold the enclose completely.
Cook for 15 - 25 min, or until tender.
