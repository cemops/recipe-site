Feta Skharas


Ingredients

Feta Cheese: 12 oz
1 Tomato - Chopped
1 Sweet Pepper
1 tsp Greek Oregano (Greek Seasoning)

Instructions

Place feta slices on a piece of foil large enough to wrap around the slices without overcrowding them.
Top the cheese with the chopped tomatoes, pepper slices, Greek seasoning, and a couple of drops of olive oil. Wrap the feta slices securely in the foil.
Heat the grill or oven to 375 degrees and cook for 15 - 17 min. Each slice should be soft in the center when you press your finger in it.
Transfer to a bowl or plate and serve.

Notes

For best results, bring the feta to room temp before grilling / baking
The pepper can be mild or hot, depending on your taste
Thyme is an acceptable substitute for oregano
Can also be roasted in the oven at 375 degrees for 15 - 20 min.
As an additional touch, serve feta with grilled or toasted bread
