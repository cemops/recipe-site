Roasted Potato Carrot, and Broccoli Onions

Two-pan method for perfectly caramelized vegetables


Ingredients

Potatoes, cut into ¾–1 inch chunks or wedges
Carrots, halved lengthwise if thick, cut into 2–3 inch pieces
Onion, cut into large wedges
Broccoli florets, stems peeled and sliced
Green onions, left whole or halved (added last)
Olive oil (about 1–1½ tbsp per pan)
Salt
Black pepper
Optional: garlic powder, paprika, rosemary, thyme

Instructions (Two-Pan Method)

Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C).
Use two large sheet pans to prevent overcrowding.
Cut vegetables to similar thickness for even cooking.
Toss each vegetable group separately with olive oil, salt, pepper, and optional seasonings.
Pan 1 – Hard Vegetables
Add potatoes and carrots to Pan 1 in a single layer, cut side down when possible.
Roast for 20 minutes.
Flip and continue roasting for 10–15 more minutes until tender and browned.
Pan 2 – Medium Vegetables
Add onions and broccoli to Pan 2.
Roast for 12–15 minutes, stirring once halfway.
Add green onions during the final 5 minutes if using.
Remove pans when vegetables are caramelized and tender.
Combine pans if desired, adjust seasoning, and serve.

Why Two Pans Works

  • Prevents steaming and promotes browning
  • Allows each vegetable group to cook perfectly
  • Easier stirring and flipping
