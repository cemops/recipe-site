Baked Tandoori Spiced Cauliflower


Ingredients

2 Garlic Cloves, Peeled
1/4 inch Piece Fresh Ginger, Peeled
1 TBS Tandoori Masala Spice Mix
1 tablespoon ground cumin
1 ½ teaspoons ground coriander
1 ½ teaspoons ground cardamom
1 ½ teaspoons ground black pepper
1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
½ teaspoon ground cloves
½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
1 tsp Cayenne Pepper (optional)
Juice of 1/2 Lemon
1/2 Cup Plain Yogurt
Salt
1 Medium Cauliflower, leaves removed and base trimmed flat
Olive Oil
Dried chili flakes, to serve (optional)

Instructions

Using a fine grater, grate the garlic and ginger into a large mixing bowl. Add the spices and lemon juice and mix into a paste. Add the yogurt and stir, season well with salt.
*** Try boiling the cauliflower for 20 + min. before following the rest of the recipe ***
Place the cauliflower, base side up, in the bowel. Using clean hands, spread the yogurt marinade all over the cauliflower, then leave to marinate for at least 20 min., or up to 12 hours.
When ready to cook, preheat the oven to 350 degrees.
Pat the Cauliflower, base side down, on a baking sheet rubbed with olive oil and pour over any extra marinade still in the bowl. Put the sheet into the oven and bake for 30 - 45 min, until golden and tender.
Put the cauliflower on a serving plate, sprinkle with chili flakes if using, and slice it into wedges like a cake to serve.
