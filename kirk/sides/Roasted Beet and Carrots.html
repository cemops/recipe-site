Roasted Beet and Carrots


Ingredients

Makes: 2 Servings

Prep Time: 15 min    Cook Time: 18 min

1 Beet
2 Carrots
1/2 Bunch Thyme

Instructions

Slice the carrots and beet into 1/2 inch strips
Remove the thyme from their stem
Place the beet and carrots on a sheet pan.
Drizzle with olive oil; season with salt and pepper and thyme. Toss to coat.
Arrange in a single, even layer and roast, stirring halfway through, 16 - 18 min.
