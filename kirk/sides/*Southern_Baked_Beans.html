Southern Baked Beans


Ingredients:

2 - 16 oz cans pork and beans

1 Large Onion - diced

3 Tbs Yellow Mustard

1/4 Cup Maple Syrup

1/4 Cup Light Brown Sugar

4 Tbs Ketchup

1 Tbs Lemon Juice

1/2 lb Bacon, cut into 1/2 inch pieces

Instructions:

Preheat oven to 350°F

In a Dutch Oven (cast iron skillet with lid), combine all ingredients with bacon on top

Bake covered for 45 - 60 minutes

OR smoke uncovered for 3 - 4 hours
