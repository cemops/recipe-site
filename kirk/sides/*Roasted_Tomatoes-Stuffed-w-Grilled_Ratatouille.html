Roasted Tomatoes - Stuffed with Grilled Ratatouille

Prep Time: 15 min    Grilling Time: 16 – 24 min


Ingredients:

4 large, ripe tomatoes
Kosher salt
1 medium red onion, sliced 1/3 inch thick
1 medium red bell pepper, stemmed, seeded, flat pieces
1 medium zucchini, sliced lengthwise 1/3 inch thick
Extra Virgin Olive Oil
Fresh ground black pepper
2/3 cup grated mozzarella cheese
1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh basil
1 teaspoon balsamic vinegar

Directions:

Slice 1/2 inch off top of each tomato and discard.
Scoop out the insides, leaving 1/2 inch of flesh.
Lightly salt inside of tomatoes and place cut side down on paper towels.
Brush or spray onion, pepper, and zucchini with oil.
Season vegetables with salt and pepper.
Grill vegetables over Direct Medium heat. Onions: 8–12 min; Peppers/Zucchini: 6–8 min.
Cut grilled vegetables into 1/3 inch pieces.
In a bowl, combine vegetables with cheese, basil, and vinegar.
Spoon mixture into tomatoes.
Grill tomatoes over Indirect Medium heat until cheese is melted and warm, 8–12 min.
