Chateau Potatoes


Ingredients:

2 tablespoons/30 mL vegetable oil

1 pound/450 g russet potatoes, peeled

3 tablespoons/45 mL melted butter

2 tablespoons/30 mL minced parsley

2 teaspoons/10 mL sea salt

1 teaspoon/5 mL black pepper

Directions:

1. *** Next time try some whole garlic cloves and rosemary in the butter, remove before serving ***

2. Preheat oven to 375 degrees.

3. Peel and cut potatoes into 1-inch rounds and place into a large bowl. (If preparing ahead of time; place potatoes in a large bowl of ice water and lemon juice)

4. Heat oil in a large cast iron skillet. Drizzle potatoes with melted butter. Season with salt and pepper and toss to coat.

5. Layer potato pieces in pan. Remove from stove top and place into oven. Bake potatoes for 30 minutes or until fork tender and golden brown on top.

6. Once cooked, remove from oven, garnish with chopped parsley and serve.
