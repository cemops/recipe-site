Brussels Sprouts – Balsamic Glazed


Ingredients:

2 ounces bacon, diced
1-2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
10 ounces Brussels sprouts, halved
1/4 cup balsamic vinegar
Fresh ground black pepper
2 tablespoons butter
Salt

Directions:

Cook bacon in olive oil over medium-low heat until crispy. Set aside.
Brown Brussels sprouts cut-side down in pan.
Simmer sprouts in water until tender, then remove.
Reduce balsamic vinegar until syrupy, stir in butter.
Toss sprouts and bacon with balsamic mixture, season, and serve.
