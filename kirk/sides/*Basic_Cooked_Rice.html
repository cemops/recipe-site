Basic Cooked Rice

Makes 6 Cups


If you make this ahead of time and store it, omit the bell peppers — they tend to sour quickly. Use chicken stock with chicken dishes, seafood stock with seafood dishes, and beef stock with beef dishes.



Ingredients:

2 cups uncooked rice (preferably converted)
2 1/2 cups stock
1 1/2 tbsp very finely chopped onions
1 1/2 tbsp very finely chopped celery
1 1/2 tbsp very finely chopped green bell pepper
1 1/2 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
1/2 tsp salt
1/8 tsp garlic powder
A pinch each of white pepper, cayenne pepper, black pepper

Directions:

Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
In a 5 × 9 × 2½ inch loaf pan, combine all ingredients and mix well.
Seal pan snugly with aluminum foil.
Bake until rice is tender, about 1 hour and 10 minutes.
Serve immediately — rice stays hot for ~45 minutes and warm for up to 2 hours.
To reheat leftover rice, use a double boiler or warm it in a skillet with unsalted butter.
