Broccoli Fried Rice


Ingredients

Steamed Rice (EASY - get from Buehlers)
Steamed Broccoli (EASY - get from Buehlers)
Egg
Sesame oil
Sesame Seed
Teriyaki Sauce
Any other stuff you want

Instructions

Steam the rice and broccoli
Fry the egg in the sesame oil
Melt unsalted butter
Add rice, cook
Add broccoli, sesame seeds etc.
