Pasta Limone


Ingredients

12 oz spaghetti, linguine or bucatini - Use Whole Wheat
2 Leeks, Thinly Sliced
2 Garlic Cloves, Thinly Sliced
Zest and Juice of 1 Lemon (Try 1/2 of lemon juice)
3 Sprigs Basil
1 tsp Kosher Salt - Cut back on the salt
3/4 tsp Black Pepper
1 Cup Grated Parm Cheese

Instructions

Combine pasta, 4 1/2 Cups Water, leeks, garlic, lemon zest, basil, salt & pepper in a large skillet with 2-inch sides.
Bring the water to a boil and cook, stirring occasionally, until the water is nearly gone and the pasta is fully cooked, 8 - 10 min.
Add the lemon juice and parmesan, and toss to combine. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Garnish with basil leaves and lemon zest.
** Good with lemon pepper grilled chicken tenders **
** Also great with Garlic Butter Shrimp and black olives**
