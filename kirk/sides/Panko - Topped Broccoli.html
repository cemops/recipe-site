Panko - Topped Broccoli


Ingredients

Prep: 3 Min | Cook: 10 Min
1 (12 oz) package of fresh broccoli florets ( about 5 cups )
1 Tbs Olive Oil
4 Garlic cloves - minced
3/4 Cup panko
1 Tbs chopped fresh flat leave parsley
1/4 tsp salt
1 1/2 tsp black pepper

Instructions

Cut large florets in half, arrange broccoli in a vegetable steamer. Steam 6 min or until crisp - tender.
In a large pan heat the oil and add garlic for 30 seconds
Stir in the panko; remove from heat.
Stir in parsley, salt and pepper.
Add broccoli and toss gently
