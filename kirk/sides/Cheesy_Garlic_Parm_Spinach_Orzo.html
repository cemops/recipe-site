Cheesy Garlic Parmesan Spinach Orzo


Ingredients:

1 1/2 Cups orzo pasta

2 Tbs olive oil

2 1/2 tsp minced garlic

Salt and pepper to taste

2 tsp flour

1 Cup milk

2/3 cup fresh spinach, roughly chopped

1/2 Cup shredded Parmesan cheese

Optional: fresh lemon zest, grated parmesan, cracked black pepper for topping

Half Recipe

3/4 Cup orzo pasta

1 Tbs olive oil

1 1/4 tsp minced garlic

Salt and pepper to taste

1 tsp flour

1/2 Cup milk

1/3 cup fresh spinach, roughly chopped

1/4 Cup shredded Parmesan cheese

Optional: fresh lemon zest, grated parmesan, cracked black pepper for topping

Directions:

- Cook orzo according to directions, drain and set aside.

- Heat olive oil in a medium sized pot over medium heat. Add garlic, salt and pepper and saute for 1 min until fragrant.

- Add flour to form and stir for 1 min. Add about 1/4 Cup of milk and whisk to work out any lumps. Add the remaining milk and whisk to combine. Bring to a simmer and cook for about 8 min or until thickened.

- Add chopped spinach to sauce. Cook additional 2 min. or until spinach is soft and dark. Remove sauce from heat and stir in Parmesan cheese. When cheese has melted add orzo pasta and stir to combine.

- Serve with extra Parmesan cheese and serve immediately.
