Grilled Carots


Ingredients

Carrots
Glaze:
2 Tbs Butter
Dash of Red wine vinegar
Nutmeg
Salt
Pepper

Instructions

Blanch carrots for 6 min. Drain and rinse in cold water to stop cooking
Combine remaining ingredients in sauce pan and melt
Cover carrots with 1/2 of glaze then grill on med. direct heat for 3 - 5 min turning every min. or so.
Drizzle with remaining glaze
