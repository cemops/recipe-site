Cole Slaw


Ingredients:

1 Bag Fresh Slaw Blend

Red Onion (thinly sliced)

Green Pepper (thinly sliced)

2 tsp Creole Mustard

1/2 tsp Roasted Garlic

1 1/2 Tbs Apple Cider Vinegar

1/2 Cup Mayonnaise

1/2 Cup Sour Cream

Parsley (chopped)

Lemon Juice (to taste)

Instructions:

In a large bowl, mix together the mayo, sour cream, Creole mustard, vinegar, garlic, and lemon juice to create the dressing.

Add the slaw blend, red onion, and green pepper to the bowl.

Toss all ingredients together until the slaw is fully coated with dressing.

Sprinkle with chopped parsley and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
