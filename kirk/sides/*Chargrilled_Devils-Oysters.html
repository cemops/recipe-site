Chargrilled Devils (Oysters)

Smoky, spicy, bacon-wrapped oysters

Serves 6


Ingredients

36 fresh oysters
18 bacon slices
1 tbsp paprika
1 tsp cayenne pepper

Sauce

1 fresh red chili, seeded & finely chopped
1 garlic clove, finely chopped
1 shallot, finely chopped
2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
2 tbsp lemon juice
Salt & black pepper, to taste

Instructions

Preheat grill to medium-high heat.
Open oysters, catching the juice from the shells in a bowl.
Cut oysters from bottom shells, set aside, and add any remaining juice to the bowl.
Add chili, garlic, shallot, parsley, and lemon juice to the bowl. Season with salt and pepper, mix well, cover, and chill until needed.
Cut each bacon slice in half crosswise.
Season oysters with paprika and cayenne.
Wrap each oyster in half a bacon slice and thread onto skewers.
Grill over direct heat, turning frequently, for about 5 minutes until bacon is browned and crispy.
Transfer to a platter and serve immediately with the chilled sauce.
