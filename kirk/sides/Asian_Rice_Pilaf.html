Asian Rice Pilaf


Ingredients:

3 Tbs Butter

1/2 Cup Sliced Carrots

1/2 Cup Sliced Celery

1/2 Cup Chopped Onion

1 Cup Uncooked Long Grain Rice (Last time Jasmatie Brown rice took too long)

2 Cups Hot Water

2 Tbs Soy Sauce

2 tsp Sugar

2 tsp Dried parsley flakes

2 tsp Chicken Bouillon Granules

1/2 tsp Ground Ginger

2-1/2 Cups Frozen Peas (about 10 oz) Thawed

Instructions:

- In a large saucepan, heat butter over medium-high heat; saute carrot, celery and onion until tender, 3 - 5 min. Stir in rice until coated.

- Stir in water, soy sauce, sugar and seasonings; bring to a boil. Reduce heat; simmer, covered, until liquid is absorbed and rice is tender, about 15 min.

- Gently stir in peas. Cover and heat through.
