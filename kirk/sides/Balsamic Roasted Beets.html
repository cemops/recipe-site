Balsamic Roasted Beets


Ingredients

3 Beets, Peeled & Diced (about 3 Cups)*
1 TBS EVOO
1/2 tsp Salt
1 TBS Balsamic Vinegar
1 TBS Honey

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425 degrees
Grease a large baking dish
Toss beets with 1 TBS olive oil and 1/4 tsp salt, spread onto greased baking dish in an even layer.
Bake for 15 - 20 min.
Stir and bake for 10 min. more until beets are soft.
Mix together balsamic and honey
After 30 min. remove beets from the oven and add the honey/vinegar mixture to the baking dish. Stir until evenly coated.
Return to the oven for 10 min., stirring once half way through. Roast until the balsamic/honey coats the beets.
Remove from the oven and serve warm

** Instead of peeling and cutting the beets, you could roast them whole in the following manner:

Wrap beets individually in foil and set on a baking sheet.
Roast in an oven set to 450 degrees until beets are soft to the touch, 50 - 70 min. depending on the size of the beets.
Remove the skin from the beets with your hands or paper towel. (at this point you can store the roasted beets until later when you're ready to add the rest of the ingredients, dice and roast)
Dice and toss with recipe ingredients.
Roast for 10 - 15 min. at 450 degrees until beets are soft and the balsamic mixture is thick and coats the beets.

NOTES:
The size of the beets are cut to make a difference in roasting time. Please be aware of this and check your beets after 15 min of cooking to ensure they are not getting overdone!
