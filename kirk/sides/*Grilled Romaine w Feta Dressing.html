Grilled Romaine w/ Feta Dressing


Ingredients

Romaine Lettuce
1/2 Cup Buttermilk (1 TBS Fresh Lemon Juice + 1 (Remaining Cup) Milk; Let Set 5 Min)
1/4 Cup Mayonnaise (See Homemade Mayo Recipe) ** Single serving of Greek Yogurt**
2 Oz Feta Cheese
Juice of 1/2 Lemon
1/2 tsp Ground Black Pepper
1 TBS Minced Fresh Chives
1 tsp Minced Fresh Thyme

Instructions

In a food processor combine the dressing ingredients (minus chives and thyme) and process until smooth. Add the chives and thyme and set aside
Slice the romaine lettuce lengthwise and oil, salt and pepper the cut side.
Grill the lettuce over direct medium heat with the lid closed until lettuce starts to wilt, turning once, about 2 min.
Serve immediately and drizzle with dressing

Notes

Could (should) use the feta dressing with Greek yogurt instead
Could still add the thyme to it as well
