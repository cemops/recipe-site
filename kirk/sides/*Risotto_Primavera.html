Risotto Primavera


Ingredients:

2 TBS Olive Oil plus extra for frying

4 TBS Butter (I used unsalted)

1 Onion, finely chopped

3 Cloves Garlic, finely chopped

10 oz (1 1/4 Cups) Arborio rice

1 Cup White Wine

3 Cups Hot low sodium Chicken Broth

4 1/2 oz fresh or frozen Fava Beans (or lima beans, peas, edamame etc)

Bunch of Asparagus, trimmed and cut into bite-sized pieces

2 small Zucchini, diced

1 oz, grated Parm Cheese, plus extra to serve

Instructions:

- Heat olive oil and half of the butter in a large heavy-bottomed (cast iron skillet) pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook 3 - 4 min until soft. Season, then add the garlic and cook for another min.

- Stir in the rice and turn it in the oily butter so all the grains are coated. Cook for a few seconds. Increase the heat, add the wine, and bubble for 1 - 2 min until it has been absorbed.

- Add a ladleful of hot stock at a time (keeping the rest simmering in a saucepan) and stir, cooking until it has been absorbed. Continue doing this for 30 - 40 min, or until the rice is cooked to al dente and is creamy.

- Meanwhile, add the fava beans (lima beans, peas etc) to a large pot of salted water and cook for 3 - 4 min. Drain well and set aside.

- Add oil to another pan and fry the asparagus and zucchini for 3 - 4 min until they have begun to color. (Last time: I combined the veggies while waiting for the risotto to finish.)

- Stir the veggies into the risotto, dot the remaining butter all over, stir it in. Then stir in the parm and season to taste.
