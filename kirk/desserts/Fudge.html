Fudge


Ingredients

3 Cups Granulated Sugar
3 TBS Cocoa
1 Cup Milk
1/2 tsp Cream of Tartar
1 tsp Butter
1 1/2 tsp of Vanilla

Instructions

Cook first three ingredients until it comes to a boil, add Tartar
Boil as slowly as possible and cook until it forms a ball
Remove from heat and set in pan of cold water
Add butter and vanilla
Do not beat until very thoroughly cold, then add nuts.
