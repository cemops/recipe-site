Double Streusel Apple Butter Bars

Sweet, spiced, and crumbly


Ingredients

2 cups all-purpose flour
1½ cups old-fashioned oats
½ cup raw pecans
1 cup brown sugar
1 tsp baking powder
1 tsp cinnamon
¼ tsp nutmeg
2 tsp vanilla extract
2 tbsp cold salted butter, thinly sliced
Apple butter (for filling)
Apples, sliced (for filling)

Instructions

Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9" × 13" baking dish with parchment paper.
In a food processor, pulse flour, oats, pecans, brown sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, and nutmeg until oats are mostly ground.
Add cold butter and pulse until a crumbly dough forms. If dry, add 1 tbsp water.
Press ⅓ of the dough into the prepared baking dish.
Bake for 30 minutes or until lightly golden.
Spread apple butter evenly over the baked crust.
Arrange apples evenly over the apple butter.
Sprinkle remaining ⅔ of streusel dough over the apples.
Lay butter slices over the streusel.
Bake 30–40 minutes until topping is lightly golden.
Cool completely before cutting into bars.
