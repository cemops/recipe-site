Brownies

Ingredients

  • 1/2 Cup Shortening
  • 1 Cup Sugar
  • 2 Eggs
  • 3/4 Cup Flour
  • 1/4 tsp Salt
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 2 Squares Unsweetened Chocolate
  • 1 tsp Vanilla

Instructions

  1. Cream the shortening, then add sugar and well-beaten eggs.
  2. Sift flour once, measure, then sift flour, salt, and baking powder together, and add to the creamed mixture.
  3. Add the melted unsweetened chocolate.
  4. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  5. Pour into a well-greased utility dish.
  6. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes.
