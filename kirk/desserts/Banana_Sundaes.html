Banana Sundaes

Ingredients

  • 3 Large firm bananas
  • 1 Tbs margarine or butter, melted
  • 2 tsp orange juice
  • 1/2 cup caramel ice-cream topping
  • 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 pint vanilla ice cream
  • 1/4 cup toasted coconut
  • 1/4 cup sliced almonds, toasted

Instructions

  1. Cut bananas in half lengthwise, then cut each piece in half crosswise.
  2. Stir together margarine or butter and 1 tsp of the orange juice. Brush mixture over all sides of banana pieces.
  3. Grill bananas over medium-hot heat for 4 minutes, turning halfway through grilling.
  4. Meanwhile, combine caramel topping and remaining orange juice in a skillet or saucepan. Heat on the grill until mixture boils, stirring frequently. Stir in cinnamon.
  5. Add bananas to the sauce and stir gently to coat.
  6. Spoon sauce and bananas over scoops of vanilla ice cream. Sprinkle with toasted coconut and almonds.
  7. Serve immediately and enjoy!
