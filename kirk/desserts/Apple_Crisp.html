Apple Crisp


Ingredients:

6 Golden Delicious apples, peeled and chopped
2 TBS Granulated Sugar
1 3/4 tsp Ground Cinnamon, divided
1 1/2 tsp Lemon Juice
1 Cup Light Brown Sugar
3/4 Cup Old Fashioned Oats
3/4 Cup All-Purpose Flour
1/2 Cup Cold Unsalted Butter, diced
Pinch of Kosher Salt

Directions:

Preheat oven to 350°F. Butter or spray an 8x8 baking dish.
Mix apples, granulated sugar, 3/4 tsp cinnamon, and lemon juice. Transfer to dish.
In a bowl, combine brown sugar, oats, flour, 1 tsp cinnamon, salt, and butter.
Cut butter into oat mixture until it forms pea-sized crumbs.
Spread topping over apples. Gently pat to even it out.
Bake 40-50 minutes until golden and bubbly.
Serve warm and enjoy!
