Grilled Peaches with Blackberry Sauce

Prep Time: 10 Minutes  |  Grill Time: 8–10 Minutes

Ingredients:

For the Sauce

6 oz fresh blackberries (about 1 cup)
1 to 3 Tbsp sugar

For the Peaches

1 Tbsp Unsalted Butter
1 Tbsp granulated sugar
4 medium peaches, halved and pitted

For Serving

1 Cup vanilla ice cream

Directions:

In a food processor, puree the blackberries with 1 Tbsp water.
Add 1 to 3 Tbsp sugar to the puree, to taste. Set aside.
Melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat.
Stir in granulated sugar until dissolved.
Brush peach halves all over with butter-sugar mixture.
Grill peaches over Direct Medium Heat, turning every 3 minutes, for 8–10 minutes total, until browned in spots and heated through.
Place grilled peaches in a bowl with vanilla ice cream.
Drizzle with blackberry sauce and serve immediately.
