Snow Cream

A classic winter treat


Ingredients

Option 1 (Classic)

2 cups milk
2 tbsp vanilla
½ cup sugar
2 quarts clean, fresh snow

Option 2 (Creamy)

1–3 cups clean, fresh snow
½ cup whipping cream
1 tbsp sugar
2–3 drops vanilla

Instructions

Collect fresh, clean snow in a large bowl.
In a separate bowl, mix milk (or cream), sugar, and vanilla until sugar dissolves.
Gradually pour liquid mixture over the snow while gently stirring.
Add snow or liquid as needed to reach desired consistency.
Serve immediately.
