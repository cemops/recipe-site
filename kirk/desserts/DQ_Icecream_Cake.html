Dairy Queen Ice Cream Cake

Ingredients

Chocolate Cookie Crunchies

  • 3/4 Cup Oreo crumbs (about 9 Oreos)
  • 1 1/2 TBS butter, melted

Ice Cream Layers

  • 1.5 quart container vanilla ice cream
  • 1.5 quart container chocolate ice cream

Chocolate Fudge

  • 6 oz bag semi-sweet chocolate chips
  • 3 TBS light corn syrup
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 Cup heavy whipping cream

Whipped Cream

  • 2 Cups heavy whipping cream, cold
  • 1 Cup powdered sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • Sprinkles (for decoration)

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a cookie sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat and set aside.
  2. Combine the Oreo crumbs and melted butter until well combined. Spread the crumbs evenly on the prepared cookie sheet.
  3. Bake for 8-10 minutes, then allow to cool. Break any large clumps into smaller pieces. Set aside.
  4. Line an 8×3 inch cake pan with plastic wrap, ensuring it covers the bottom and sides. Set aside.
  5. Soften the chocolate ice cream for about 20 minutes. Spread it evenly in the cake pan and freeze for 30 minutes.
  6. To make the fudge, combine chocolate chips, corn syrup, and vanilla extract in a bowl. Heat the cream until it just begins to boil, then pour it over the chocolate mixture. Whisk until smooth.
  7. Remove the cake pan from the freezer and pour the fudge over the chocolate ice cream. Freeze for 10 minutes.
  8. Add the cookie crumbles over the fudge layer and freeze for 2 hours.
  9. Soften the vanilla ice cream for about 20 minutes. Spread it over the cookie layer and freeze until firm (2-3 hours).
  10. Once frozen, remove the cake from the pan using the plastic wrap and place on a serving plate. Freeze again while preparing the whipped cream.
  11. To make whipped cream, whip together cold cream, powdered sugar, and vanilla until stiff peaks form. Frost the cake and decorate with sprinkles or desired toppings.
  12. Freeze until ready to serve. Best when stored covered and eaten within 4-5 days.
