Grandma Sandford's Ham Loaf


Ingredients:

Ham Loaf Mixture:

Ham loaf mix

2 Eggs

1 Cup bread crumbs

1/4 Cup Milk

Glaze:

1/2 Cup brown sugar

2 TBS Apple cider Vinegar

1/2 tsp dry mustard

Instructions:

1. Mix all of the ham loaf mixture together, shape into a loaf and place in a greased loaf dish.

2. Mix all of the glaze ingredients and pour onto ham loaf.

3. Bake at 350 degrees for 1 hour. 20 min uncovered, 20 min covered, 20 min uncovered.
