Pie Crust


Ingredients:

2 cups flour, sifted
3/4 cup shortening
1/3 cup milk (approx.)
1 teaspoon salt

Directions:

Mix dry ingredients.
Cut in shortening with knives until the mixture is mealy.
Sprinkle mixture with milk until you can form a ball.
Flour the surface and rolling pin, then roll out on wax paper or pastry cloth.
To bake a single pie crust: Bake at 475°F for 8–10 minutes.
