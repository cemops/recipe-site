Seven Layer Salad


Ingredients:

1 head of lettuce (chopped)
1 layer of celery (chopped)
1 layer of sliced onions
1 cup of mayonnaise
6 strips of cooked bacon
Shredded cheddar cheese

Directions:

Layer chopped lettuce as the base in a large bowl.
Add a layer of chopped celery.
Add a layer of sliced onions.
Spread 1 cup of mayonnaise evenly over the top.
Add a layer of crumbled bacon.
Top with shredded cheddar cheese.
Chill for several hours before serving.
