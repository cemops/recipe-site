Creamy Cabbage


Ingredients:

6 cups shredded cabbage
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/2 cup water
1 package (3 oz.) cream cheese, cut into pieces
1/2 teaspoon celery seed
Dash of pepper

Directions:

Cook cabbage in salted boiling water, covered, until tender.
Do not drain; add cream cheese and seasonings.
Stir well and serve hot.
