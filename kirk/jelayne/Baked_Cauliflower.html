Baked Cauliflower


Ingredients:

1 head of cauliflower
Salt (to taste)
1 can of mushroom soup
1 cup of milk
Velveeta cheese (enough to cover)

Directions:

Cook the head of cauliflower in salted water until tender.
Drain and place in a covered casserole dish.
Cover with 1 can of mushroom soup diluted with 1 cup of milk.
Top with Velveeta cheese.
Bake at 350°F for 45 minutes.
