Broccoli Casserole


Ingredients:

2 (10 oz) pkgs of frozen chopped broccoli
½ cup butter
½ lb Velveeta cheese, chopped
1 stack Ritz crackers, crushed

Directions:

Cook broccoli according to package directions.
Drain and add melted butter.
Mix with Velveeta cheese until well combined.
Pour mixture into a casserole dish and top with crushed Ritz crackers.
Bake at 350°F until heated through and crackers are golden brown.
