Texas Sheet Cake


Ingredients:

2 cups flour
2 cups white sugar
1/2 teaspoon salt
2 sticks butter
1 cup water
4 tablespoons cocoa
2 eggs
1/2 cup milk
1 teaspoon vanilla
1 teaspoon baking soda

Directions:

Preheat oven to 375°F.
Sift together flour, sugar, and salt in a large bowl. Set aside.
Bring butter, water, and cocoa to a boil, then pour over dry ingredients.
Beat together eggs, milk, vanilla, and baking soda. Add to the mixture and combine well.
Pour into a greased sheet pan and bake for 20 minutes.
