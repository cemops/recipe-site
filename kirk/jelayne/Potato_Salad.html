Grandma Goodman's Potato Salad


Ingredients:

2 qt. of potatoes
2 sticks celery
1 small onion
1 small cucumber
8–10 boiled eggs
1 qt. jar of Miracle Whip
Salt to taste

Directions:

Fill a 2 qt. saucepan or roasting pan with potatoes. Cover with water and boil until done.
Cool the potatoes, peel, and slice small.
Dice celery, onion, cucumber, and boiled eggs. Mix them with the potatoes.
Add salt to taste.
Combine with 1 qt. jar of Miracle Whip and mix well.
