Icing for Red Velvet Cake

Recipe from Grandma Goodman

Ingredients

  • 3 teaspoons flour
  • 1 cup canned milk
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup shortening
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Instructions

  1. Mix and cook 3 teaspoons of flour and 1 cup of canned milk over medium heat until thick. Let it cool.
  2. In a separate bowl, beat together 1 cup of sugar, 1 cup of shortening, 1/2 cup of butter, and 1 teaspoon of vanilla until light and fluffy.
  3. Combine the cooled flour mixture with the beaten mixture. Mix well until smooth and creamy.

Tip: Begin preparing this icing as soon as you place the cake in the oven so it is ready in time!
