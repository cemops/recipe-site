Meatballs


Ingredients:

1 Lb Ground Beef

1 Egg

½ Cup Cracker Crumbs

Parsley Flakes

Salt & Pepper

Milk

Onion

Garlic Salt

Worcestershire Sauce

Instructions:

1. Combine all ingredients and form into small balls.

2. Place on an oiled, foil lined baking sheet.

3. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.

4. Bake for 20 min.
