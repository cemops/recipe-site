Fresh String Green Beans


Ingredients:

5 lbs green beans
3 tbsp bacon grease (adjust for fewer beans)

Directions:

Melt bacon grease in the bottom of a pan.
Place wet, cleaned green beans in the pan.
Salt and sprinkle with brown sugar. Stir until coated with grease.
Add water until it surrounds the beans but doesn’t cover them.
Cook on high for 15 minutes, then reduce to warm or simmer for 2–3 hours.
Always check to ensure beans have water.
