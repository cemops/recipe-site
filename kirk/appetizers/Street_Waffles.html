Street Waffles


Ingredients:

4 egg yolks

3/4 cup milk

2 tsp vanilla extract

1/3 cup butter, melted

3/4 cup sugar

1 cup + 2 Tbsp flour

1 1/2 tsp baking powder

Pinch of freshly grated nutmeg

1/8 tsp salt

6 egg whites, beaten to stiff peaks

Vegetable oil (for cooking)

Optional toppings: Fresh fruit, honey, syrup, powdered sugar, whipped cream

Instructions:

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, milk, vanilla, butter, and sugar until combined; set aside.

2. In a large bowl, combine flour, baking powder, nutmeg, and salt. Whisk the egg yolk mixture into the flour mixture until batter is smooth and well blended.

3. Add one-third of the beaten egg whites to the batter and stir until lightened. Gently fold in the remaining whites.

4. Preheat waffle pan halves over medium heat and lightly oil both sides.

5. Pour 3/4 cup batter into the center of one side, place the other pan side on top, and flip the pan. Cook for 2-2.5 min per side.

6. Remove waffle from the pan and repeat with remaining batter. Serve with toppings as desired.
