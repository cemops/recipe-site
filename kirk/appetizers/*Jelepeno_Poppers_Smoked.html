Jalapeno Poppers - Hickory Smoked


Ingredients:

12 Jalapeno Peppers
12 Slices of Bacon
8 oz Cream Cheese
8 oz Cheddar Cheese
1 - 2 slices Bread

Directions:

Cut tops off of jalapenos, and using end of a fork or spoon, stick down inside and rotate. This will loosen the membranes and seeds, then dump them out.
Place cream cheese in a bowl to bring to room temp. Add Cheddar cheese and mix well.
Place cheese mixture into jalapeno and push down inside with finger. Tear off a small piece of bread and place inside the top of the jalapeno.
Start by draping the bacon over the opening of the jalapeno, then bring bacon down to tip of jalapeno and begin wrapping it back to the top. Secure with toothpick.
Smoke at about 200 - 250 degrees for about 1 - 1 1/2 hours.
