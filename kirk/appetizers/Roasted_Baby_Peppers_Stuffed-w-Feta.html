Roasted Baby Peppers Stuffed with Feta


Ingredients:

10 Sweet Baby Peppers

1 - 6oz Container Crumbled Feta Cheese

1/4 Cup Minced Red Onion

1 TBS EVOO (heavy)

1 TBS Red Wine Vinegar (heavy)

1 tsp Dried Oregano

Instructions:

1. Mix feta cheese stuffing in a bowl and set aside.

2. Cut tops off peppers, cut in half lengthwise, and seed.

3. Drizzle peppers with EVOO and season with salt.

4. Grill over medium-high heat for 4-5 minutes until blistered.

5. Let peppers cool before stuffing with feta mixture. Serve warm or chill.
