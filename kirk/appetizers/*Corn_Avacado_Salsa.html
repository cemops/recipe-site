Corn / Avocado Salsa

Ingredients:

  • 2 Bags Frozen White / Yellow Corn
  • 2 Avocados
  • 2 Small Cans Sliced Black Olives
  • 1 Small Red Onion
  • Cilantro
  • Dressing:
    • Red Wine Vinegar
    • Lime Juice
    • Olive Oil
    • Salt
    • Pepper

Directions:

  1. Prepare the Ingredients:
    • Thaw the Corn
    • Dice the Avocado and place in a small bowl with lime juice. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1/2 hour.
    • Dice the Black Olive Slices
    • Dice the Red Onion
    • Mince the Cilantro
  2. Combine all the Ingredients
  3. Then add the Dressing and lightly stir
