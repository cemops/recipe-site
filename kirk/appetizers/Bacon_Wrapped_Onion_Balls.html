Bacon Wrapped Onion Bombs


Ingredients:

1 lb Beef
1 Egg
1/2 Cup Bread Crumbs
1/4 Cup Onion
1 tsp Salt and Pepper
Dash Garlic Powder
1/4 Cup BBQ Sauce - Sweet Baby Rays Vidalia Onion
1/2 Cup Milk
2 Medium Onions
Bacon - thin sliced, long (not center cut)

Instructions:

1. Combine stuffing ingredients.
2. Cut tops and bottoms from onions and cut halfway through the center.
3. Remove outer layer and separate 3 layers from each onion.
4. Stuff each onion layer full with meat mixture.
5. Wrap with bacon completely, hold together with toothpicks.
6. Smoke for 2 hrs at 220 - 250 degrees.
7. In the last 15 min baste with BBQ sauce and let caramelize.
8. Cook to 165 degrees.
