Tomato & Mozzarella Stacks


Ingredients:

4 Large Tomatoes

8 oz Buffalo Mozzarella Cheese

24 Basil Leaves

Olive Oil

Salt & Pepper

Instructions:

1. Cut the tomatoes into 3 slices each.

2. Slice the mozzarella into 12 thin slices.

3. Slice the basil into thin ribbons, leaving 4 whole pieces.

4. Brush the center of a 7-inch square aluminum foil with oil.

5. Place a tomato slice on the foil, brush with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Add basil ribbons and a slice of cheese.

6. Repeat the layering, finishing with a slice of cheese, a whole basil leaf, then fold the edges of the foil to close completely.

7. Cook over medium heat, covered, for 8-10 minutes until the top tomato slice is heated and the cheese has melted.

8. Serve immediately.
