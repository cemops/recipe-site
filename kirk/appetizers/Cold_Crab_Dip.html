Cold Imitation Crab Dip

Serves 4–6


Ingredients

8 oz imitation crab meat, finely chopped
½ cup plain Greek yogurt (full-fat preferred)
¼ cup mayonnaise or 4 oz cream cheese, softened
¾–1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
2–3 tbsp pickle relish, drained well
1–2 tsp hot sauce (Frank’s, Crystal, or similar)
½ tsp garlic powder
¼ tsp onion powder
Salt & black pepper, to taste
Optional: 1–2 tbsp lemon juice or pickle juice for brightness
Optional garnish: chopped green onions or dill

Instructions

Base: Mix yogurt with mayonnaise or cream cheese until smooth.
Flavor: Stir in pickle relish, hot sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, and optional lemon/pickle juice.
Crab: Fold in chopped imitation crab.
Cheese: Gently mix in shredded mozzarella.
Season: Taste and adjust salt, pepper, hot sauce, or relish.
Chill: Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes (better after 1–2 hours).

Serving Ideas

Serve with crackers, pretzels, or pita chips
Serve with celery sticks or cucumber rounds
Spread on toast or bagels
Use in tortilla pinwheels

Variations

Extra creamy: Use both mayo (2 tbsp) and cream cheese (2 oz)
More bite: Add a pinch of cayenne or a splash of pickle juice
Seafood-salad vibe: Mix in finely diced celery or green onion
Lower fat: Use all yogurt and part-skim mozzarella
