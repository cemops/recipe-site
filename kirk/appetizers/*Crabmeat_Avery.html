Crabmeat Avery

Makes 8 appetizer servings

Ingredients:

  • 2 1/2 TBS unsalted butter
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped onions
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped celery
  • 1/3 cup finely chopped green bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped green onions
  • 1/2 tsp minced garlic
  • 6 TBS in all, Homemade Mayonnaise (in sides folder)
  • 4 tsp creole mustard or brown mustard
  • 2 tsp finely chopped fresh parsley
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp Tabasco
  • 1/2 tsp white pepper
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 egg
  • 1 lb lump crabmeat (picked over)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • Sweet paprika

Instructions:

  1. Combine the butter, onions, celery, bell peppers, green onions and garlic in a 1 qt sauce pan; sauté over high heat until vegetables are tender but still firm, about 5 min, stirring occasionally. Remove from heat.
  2. Add 2 1/2 TBS of the mayo, the mustard, parsley, Worcestershire, salt, cayenne, Tabasco and the white and black pepper, stirring well. Beat in the egg with a metal whisk.
  3. Add the crabmeat to vegetable mixture and gently toss, leaving the lumps intact as much as possible.
  4. Spoon into 8 1/2-cup ovenproof ramekins. Pour 1 TBS cream over the top of each, then spread a thin, even layer of the remaining mayo on top and sprinkle with paprika. Bake at 350 degrees until brown and bubbly, about 15 - 18 min. Serve immediately.
