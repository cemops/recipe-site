Avocado Feta Dip


Ingredients:

1 Avocado
4 oz. (1 Cup) Feta Cheese
1 Lemon, Juiced (about 2 Tbs)
2 - 3 Scallions, roughly chopped
1 Large handful of Parsley
8 twists of Black Pepper

Instructions:

1. Add all ingredients to food processor.
2. Pulse until desired consistency.
3. Serve with french bread, crudites, or chips.
