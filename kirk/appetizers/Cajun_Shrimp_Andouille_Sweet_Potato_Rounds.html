Cajun Shrimp & Andouille Sweet Potato Rounds

Appetizer / Party Version – ~20–24 bites


Ingredients

3–4 medium sweet potatoes
1 lb raw shrimp, peeled, deveined, chopped
6 oz andouille sausage, diced
2–3 cloves garlic, minced
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tbsp butter
1–2 tsp Cajun seasoning
¼–½ tsp cayenne pepper
3 tbsp whipped cream cheese
2–3 tbsp whole milk Greek yogurt
Kosher salt
Olive oil (for roasting rounds)
Optional garnish: green onions, lemon juice, smoked paprika

Instructions

Preheat oven to 425°F. Slice sweet potatoes ½-inch thick, pat dry, toss with olive oil & salt. Roast 20–25 min, flipping halfway. Optional broil 1–2 min for caramelization.
Heat olive oil & butter in skillet. Add garlic 30 sec. Add sausage 2–3 min. Add shrimp & Cajun seasoning, cook until opaque (1–2 min). Remove from heat.
Stir in cream cheese, Greek yogurt, cayenne. Optional: mash 1–2 tbsp roasted sweet potato to thicken.
Allow mixture to cool slightly, then spoon 1 tbsp onto each sweet potato round and lightly press to adhere.
Return to oven 400–425°F for 5–7 min to heat through and lightly set. Garnish if desired and serve warm.
