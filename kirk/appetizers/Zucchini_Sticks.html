Zucchini Sticks


Ingredients:

3 Zucchini

1/2 cup whole wheat bread crumbs

1/4 cup Parmesan cheese

Dash of Veggie Magic

2 eggs

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.

2. Mix bread crumbs, Parmesan cheese, and Veggie Magic in a shallow dish.

3. Whisk eggs in another shallow dish.

4. Cut off zucchini ends and cut them in half lengthwise.

5. Cut the zucchini halves into 1-inch sticks.

6. Soak zucchini sticks in the egg wash, then coat them in the bread crumb mixture.

7. Place the coated zucchini sticks onto a foil-lined, oiled sheet pan.

8. Bake for 20-25 minutes, turning halfway through the cooking time.
