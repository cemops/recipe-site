Tuna and Avocado Tartare


Ingredients:

2 tsp tan or black sesame seeds (toast the tan, do not toast the black)

1/2 small bunch of chives, finely chopped (or green onions)

1 tbsp soy sauce (reduce to a splash)

Juice of 1 lime, divided

1/4 tsp sesame oil

14 oz sushi-grade tuna, cut into small cubes

Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper (optional)

1 large avocado, peeled, pitted, and cubed

Instructions:

1. Toast tan sesame seeds in a small skillet over low heat until golden. Let cool.

2. Mix chives, soy sauce, half the lime juice, and sesame oil in a medium bowl. Add the tuna, season with salt and pepper, and toss.

3. In another bowl, gently fold in the avocado with the remaining lime juice.

4. Divide the tuna mixture into four plates. Form a small mound in the center.

5. Spoon avocado on top, sprinkle with sesame seeds, and serve immediately.


Notes:

  • Use a 2-3 inch pastry ring to mold the tuna and avocado neatly on the plate.
  • Serve with pita chips or tortillas for a delicious appetizer.
  • Pair with an arugula salad with ginger dressing for a complete meal.
