Tortilla Pinwheels


Ingredients:

8 oz. Sour Cream

8 oz. Cream Cheese, Softened

1 Can (4 oz) Diced Green Chilis

1 Can (4 oz) Chopped Black Olives

1 Cup Cheddar Cheese, Grated

1/2 Cup Green Onions

Garlic Powder to Taste

Flour Tortillas

Salsa for Dip

Instructions:

1. Combine all ingredients (except tortillas and salsa) in a bowl.

2. Spread the mixture evenly over the flour tortillas.

3. Roll the tortillas tightly to form a pinwheel.

4. Slice the rolled tortillas into 1-inch pieces.

5. Serve with salsa for dipping.
